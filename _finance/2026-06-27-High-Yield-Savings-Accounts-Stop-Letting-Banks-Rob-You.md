## Why Your Bank Is Paying Almost Nothing

Traditional banks — especially the big national ones — offer low savings rates for a simple reason: **they don't need to compete for your deposits.**

They have millions of customers who keep money in checking and savings accounts earning nothing, because inertia is powerful. The bank takes your deposits, lends them out at 7–8% mortgage rates, and pockets the spread.

Online banks and fintech companies don't have the same overhead (no branches, fewer employees) and actively compete for deposits. They pass those savings to you in the form of higher interest rates.

## What Is a High-Yield Savings Account?

A high-yield savings account is essentially the same product as a regular savings account, except it pays dramatically higher interest:

| Feature | Traditional Bank | HYSA |
|---------|-----------------|------|
| Interest rate | 0.01–0.10% | 4.00–5.20% |
| FDIC insured | Yes | Yes |
| Minimum balance | Often $0–$500 | Usually $0 |
| Withdrawal limits | 6/month (Reg D) | Varies |
| Mobile app | Yes | Yes |

**Your money is just as safe.** HYSAs at FDIC-insured institutions protect up to $250,000 per depositor. This is the same protection your bank's low-yield account has.

## Where to Keep Your HYSA Money

High-yield savings accounts are ideal for:

### Emergency Fund (Non-Negotiable)
Your 3–6 month expense cushion should be in an HYSA. It needs to be:
- Liquid (accessible within 1–2 business days)
- Safe (not subject to market risk)
- Earning something while it waits

An HYSA hits all three criteria perfectly.

### Short-Term Savings Goals (1–3 Years)
- Down payment fund
- Wedding fund
- Car replacement fund
- Vacation savings

Anything you'll need within 3 years should NOT be in the stock market (too risky). An HYSA is the right tool.

### Cash You're Waiting to Deploy
If you receive a bonus, inheritance, or sell something and you're deciding what to do with the money — park it in an HYSA while you decide. It's earning ~5% instead of nothing.

## What HYSAs Are NOT For

- **Long-term retirement savings** — Inflation over 20+ years erodes purchasing power even at 5%. Use index funds for this.
- **Money you need instantly** — Transfers take 1–2 business days. Keep a small buffer in your checking account.
- **Risk-tolerant investment money** — If you have a 10+ year horizon, the market will likely outperform 5%.

## How to Open One Today (15-Minute Process)

1. **Choose a provider** — Check current rates; look for FDIC insurance and no fees
2. **Apply online** — Usually just SSN, ID, and a few minutes
3. **Fund via ACH transfer** — Link your checking account, transfer your emergency fund
4. **Set up recurring transfers** — Automate monthly savings contributions
5. **Done** — You're now earning 100x more interest

Keep your checking account for daily spending. Move your savings to the HYSA. The two-account system is simple and effective.

That's the cost of inertia. A one-time 15-minute task eliminates that cost indefinitely.
