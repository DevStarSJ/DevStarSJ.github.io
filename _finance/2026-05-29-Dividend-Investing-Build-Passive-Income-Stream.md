Dividend investing is one of the most time-tested strategies for building passive income. Instead of waiting to sell stocks at a profit, you collect regular cash payments — often every quarter — just for holding shares.

## What Is Dividend Investing?

When a company earns profits, it can reinvest them or distribute a portion to shareholders. That distribution is called a **dividend**. Dividend investors build portfolios of stocks that pay consistent, growing dividends over time.

The goal: **let your portfolio pay your bills.**

## Why Dividends Beat Just Hoping for Price Gains

- **Predictable income** — dividends pay on a schedule regardless of stock price
- **Compounding power** — reinvested dividends buy more shares, which pay more dividends
- **Lower stress** — you don't need to sell to realize returns
- **Inflation hedge** — dividend growth stocks often raise payouts above inflation

## Key Metrics to Know Before You Buy

### 1. Dividend Yield
`Annual Dividend / Stock Price × 100`

A 3–5% yield is solid. Anything above 7% deserves extra scrutiny — high yields can signal a company in trouble.

### 2. Payout Ratio
`Dividends Paid / Net Income × 100`

Under 60% is generally healthy. A company paying out 90%+ of earnings has little room to grow or survive a downturn.

### 3. Dividend Growth Rate
Look for companies raising dividends **every year** for 10+ years. These are called **Dividend Aristocrats** (25+ years of raises) and **Dividend Kings** (50+ years).

### 4. Free Cash Flow
Dividends are paid from cash. Strong, consistent free cash flow = safe dividends.

## Building Your Dividend Portfolio Step by Step

**Step 1: Decide your income goal**
Want $1,000/month in dividends? With a 4% average yield, you need $300,000 invested. That's a long-term target — start small and build.

**Step 2: Choose your core holdings**
- **Dividend ETFs** — VYM, SCHD, DVY (instant diversification)
- **Dividend Aristocrats** — JNJ, KO, PG, MMM, ABBV
- **REITs** — real estate exposure with high yields (O, STAG, VICI)

**Step 3: Automate reinvestment (DRIP)**
Enable Dividend Reinvestment Plans (DRIP) in your brokerage. Every dividend automatically buys more shares — no action required.

**Step 4: Add monthly and build over time**
Consistency beats timing. Add $200–$500/month and let compounding do the work.

## The Power of Dividend Reinvestment

Assume you invest $10,000 in a fund yielding 4% with 6% annual dividend growth, reinvesting all dividends:

| Year | Portfolio Value | Annual Dividends |
|------|----------------|-----------------|
| 1    | $10,800        | $400            |
| 5    | $14,500        | $640            |
| 10   | $21,600        | $1,100          |
| 20   | $48,000        | $3,200          |

That's the snowball effect in action.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Chasing yield** — a 10% yield that gets cut in half is worse than a 3% yield that grows reliably.

**Ignoring taxes** — qualified dividends are taxed lower; ordinary dividends are not. Use tax-advantaged accounts (IRA, 401k) where possible.

**Neglecting diversification** — don't put all your dividend eggs in one sector. Spread across utilities, healthcare, consumer staples, financials, REITs.

**Selling during downturns** — dividend stocks fall too. Keep your long-term view.

## The Long Game

Dividend investing isn't a get-rich-quick strategy — it's a get-rich-eventually strategy. The people who started in their 30s and reinvested quietly for 20 years are now living off their portfolios without selling a single share.

Start with what you have. Even $50/month invested consistently in dividend stocks will grow into something meaningful over time.
