The average millionaire has **7 streams of income**. The average person has 1.

That gap isn't just about income — it's about resilience. When one stream dries up (layoff, illness, economic downturn), 6 more keep flowing.

Building multiple income streams doesn't happen overnight, and it doesn't require quitting your job. But it does require a strategy and honest expectations about what works.

## The 3 Types of Income

Before building streams, understand the three categories:

**1. Active Income** — Trading time for money (salary, freelance work, consulting)  
**2. Portfolio Income** — Returns from investments (dividends, capital gains, interest)  
**3. Passive Income** — Money earned with minimal ongoing effort (rental income, royalties, digital products)

Most people start at Type 1 and never reach Types 2 or 3. The goal is to **use Type 1 income to fund Types 2 and 3**.

## Stream 1: Maximize Your Primary Income First

Before adding streams, optimize what you already have.

- Negotiate a raise (studies show simply asking increases salary by 7–15% on average)
- Develop skills that command premium rates
- Switch to a higher-paying employer (job-hopping raises salaries 10–25% faster than staying)

**Why this matters:** Every extra $500/month from your primary job can be invested to build passive streams. More primary income = faster path to financial independence.

## Stream 2: High-Yield Savings + Dividend Investing

The easiest passive stream to start: **put your cash to work**.

- **HYSA:** 4–5% APY with zero effort. $20,000 parked in a high-yield account earns $800–1,000/year.
- **Dividend ETFs:** Invest in VYM (Vanguard High Dividend Yield ETF) or SCHD to receive quarterly dividends. At $50,000 invested, you might receive $1,500–2,000/year in dividends.

Neither of these replaces a salary, but they're **money you earn while sleeping** — and they compound over time.

## Stream 3: Monetize a Skill (Active → Passive Transition)

Your day job skills are worth more on the open market than your employer pays you.

**Start active, build toward passive:**

| Skill | Active Version | Passive Version |
|---|---|---|
| Writing | Freelance articles | Blog with ad revenue |
| Teaching | 1-on-1 tutoring | Online course |
| Design | Client projects | Templates marketplace |
| Coding | Contract work | SaaS tool or app |
| Photography | Gigs | Stock photo licensing |

The active version brings in cash quickly. The passive version takes longer to build but pays indefinitely.

## Stream 4: Digital Products

Create once, sell forever. Digital products are among the highest-margin income sources available to anyone.

**What to create:**
- **Ebooks/guides:** If you have expertise in anything — cooking, fitness, finance, career advice — package it
- **Templates:** Notion templates, Excel spreadsheets, Canva designs, Figma UI kits
- **Courses:** Teachable, Gumroad, Udemy — platforms handle payments and delivery
- **Presets/assets:** Lightroom presets, music loops, icon sets

**Where to sell:**
- Gumroad (zero upfront cost, takes 10% fee)
- Etsy (for templates, printables, designs)
- Your own website (zero fees, full control)

A $20 digital product sold 5 times a day = **$3,000/month with no inventory, no shipping, no restocking**.

## Stream 5: Real Estate — Starting Accessible

You don't need to buy a rental property to invest in real estate:

**REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts):** Buy shares of real estate portfolios on the stock market. Legally required to pay 90% of income as dividends. Accessible from $50.

**Fundrise / Arrived Homes:** Crowdfunded real estate platforms allowing investment from $10–100. Returns of 8–12% annually.

**House hacking:** Buy a multi-unit property, live in one unit, rent the others. Your tenants pay most/all of your mortgage.

## The Most Common Mistake: Starting Too Many at Once

Beginners try to build 5 income streams simultaneously and fail at all 5.

**The rule:** Master one before adding the next. Get your HYSA set up. Then learn dividend investing. Then build one digital product or freelance skill. Then reinvest into more.

**Sequential depth beats simultaneous breadth** — every time.
