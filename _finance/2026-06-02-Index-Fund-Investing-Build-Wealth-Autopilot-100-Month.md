## What Is an Index Fund?

![Stock market chart on computer screen showing long-term growth trend](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1611974789855-9c2a0a7236a3?w=1000&auto=format&fit=crop)

*Photo by Maxim Hopman on Unsplash*

An index fund is a type of investment that tracks a market index — a list of companies that represents a slice of the stock market.

The most famous example: the **S&P 500**, which tracks the 500 largest publicly traded companies in the United States. When you buy an S&P 500 index fund, you own a tiny piece of all 500 companies at once.

**Key characteristics:**
- **Diversified** — you own hundreds or thousands of stocks automatically
- **Low cost** — annual fees as low as 0.03% (vs 1-2% for active funds)
- **Passive** — no manager trying to beat the market (and usually failing)
- **Tax-efficient** — less trading means fewer taxable events

## The Math That Makes Index Funds Incredible

The S&P 500 has returned an average of **~10% per year** over the past 50 years (about 7% after inflation).

What does $100/month look like over time?

| Years Invested | Total Contributed | Value at 10% Annual Return |
|---|---|---|
| 10 years | $12,000 | $20,484 |
| 20 years | $24,000 | $75,937 |
| 30 years | $36,000 | $226,049 |
| 40 years | $48,000 | $637,678 |

**$637,000 from $100/month**. That's the power of compound interest and time.

## How to Start: Step by Step

**Step 1: Open a brokerage account**
Best options for beginners:
- **Fidelity** — $0 minimums, best for ZERO funds
- **Vanguard** — excellent for long-term passive investors
- **Charles Schwab** — great all-around platform

**Step 2: Choose your account type**
- **Roth IRA** — best for most people (tax-free growth and withdrawals)
- **Traditional IRA** — good if you want a tax deduction now
- **Taxable brokerage** — no contribution limits, more flexibility

**Step 3: Pick one or two funds**
For most people, this is enough:
- **One fund:** Vanguard Total World (VT) — instant global diversification
- **Two funds:** S&P 500 (VOO) + International (VXUS) — common "two-fund portfolio"

**Step 4: Set up automatic investments**
Most brokerages allow automatic monthly purchases. Set it and forget it.

## The Biggest Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Trying to time the market** — "I'll invest when the market dips" leads to never investing
2. **Checking your portfolio daily** — watching short-term volatility makes you emotional and irrational
3. **Selling during crashes** — every market crash in history has eventually recovered
4. **Chasing last year's winners** — past performance doesn't predict future returns
5. **Waiting until you have "enough" to start** — $25/month beats waiting until you have $1,000

## Passive Investing vs. Active Investing

| | Passive (Index Funds) | Active (Stock Picking) |
|---|---|---|
| Average annual fee | 0.03–0.10% | 1–2% |
| Time required | Minutes per year | Hours per week |

The data is clear: **over 10-20 year periods, 80-90% of actively managed funds underperform their benchmark index**.
