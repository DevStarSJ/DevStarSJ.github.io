"Passive income" is one of the most overused terms in personal finance. Most so-called passive income streams require significant upfront work, capital, or both. But that doesn't mean they're not worth it — it means you should go in with realistic expectations. Here are 7 that genuinely work.

## 1. Dividend Stocks & ETFs

**What it is:** Companies share profits with shareholders as regular cash payments (dividends). You buy shares, you get paid — quarterly, usually.

**How to start:** Open a brokerage account and invest in dividend-focused ETFs like:
- **VYM** (Vanguard High Dividend Yield) — ~3% yield
- **SCHD** (Schwab U.S. Dividend Equity) — ~3.5% yield
- **JEPI** (JPMorgan Equity Premium Income) — ~7-9% yield (options-based, higher risk)

**Realistic expectations:** At a 3% yield, you need $400,000 invested to generate $1,000/month. That's a long game — but every dollar invested pays you forever.

**Effort level:** Very low once invested. Set up automatic reinvestment (DRIP) and forget it.

## 2. High-Yield Savings Accounts & CDs

**What it is:** Park your cash in accounts paying 4-5% APY instead of the 0.01% at traditional banks.

**Where to look:** Online banks like Marcus (Goldman Sachs), Ally, or Discover typically offer competitive rates. Treasury Bills (T-bills) via TreasuryDirect.gov are another option.

**Realistic expectations:** $50,000 at 5% = $2,500/year, or about $208/month. Solid for emergency funds and short-term savings.

**Effort level:** Near-zero. Takes 30 minutes to open an account.

## 3. Rental Real Estate

**What it is:** Buy property, rent it out, collect monthly cash flow after paying mortgage and expenses.

**The math:** A $300,000 property renting for $2,000/month with $1,500 in mortgage/expenses = $500/month cash flow, plus appreciation and equity buildup.

**Modern shortcuts:** If full ownership is out of reach, consider:
- **Real estate crowdfunding** (Fundrise, RealtyMogul) — invest with as little as $500
- **REITs** (Real Estate Investment Trusts) — liquid, stock-like exposure to real estate

**Effort level:** High for direct ownership. Low for REITs/crowdfunding.

## 4. Creating Digital Products

**What it is:** Build something once — an ebook, course, template, or stock photo pack — and sell it indefinitely.

**High-potential niches:** Excel/Notion templates, photography presets, resume templates, niche how-to guides, educational video courses.

**Where to sell:** Gumroad, Etsy (digital downloads), Udemy/Teachable (courses), Creative Market (design assets).

**Realistic expectations:** Most creators earn $0. Some earn $500-2,000/month from a hit template. A few build courses earning $10k+/month. The upside is unlimited; the downside is wasted time.

**Effort level:** High upfront, very low once established.

## 5. Peer-to-Peer Lending / Private Notes

**What it is:** Lend money directly to individuals or small businesses and earn interest.

**Platforms:** Prosper, LendingClub (US), or private notes through your network.

**Risk warning:** This is higher risk than bank savings. Borrower default is real. Diversify across many small loans.

**Realistic expectations:** 5-8% returns with careful diversification. Not passive in the "hands-off" sense — requires active monitoring of your loan portfolio.

## 6. Affiliate Marketing & Content

**What it is:** Create content (blog, YouTube, newsletter) that recommends products. Earn a commission when readers buy through your link.

**Why it's genuinely passive:** A blog post from 3 years ago can still earn commissions today. Old YouTube videos still generate ad revenue.

**How to start:** Pick a niche you know, create useful content consistently for 12-18 months, apply to affiliate programs (Amazon Associates, ShareASale, niche-specific programs).

**Effort level:** Extremely high to build. Genuinely passive once you have traffic.

## 7. Licensing Your Skills

**What it is:** License photos, music, code, fonts, or other creative work on marketplaces. Get paid every time someone downloads it.

**Platforms:**
- **Photos:** Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, Getty Images
- **Music:** Artlist, Musicbed, Pond5
- **Fonts/Icons:** Creative Market, FontSpring
- **Code/Templates:** ThemeForest, CodeCanyon

**Realistic expectations:** Stock photography earns pennies per download. Top contributors on Shutterstock earn $500-2,000/month from large portfolios (1,000+ images). It compounds slowly.

## The Honest Truth About Passive Income

Every stream on this list requires either:
- **Capital** (money to invest), or
- **Time** (to build something people want), or
- **Both**

There's no free lunch. But there is a real compounding effect: each passive income stream makes the next one easier to build. Start with what you have, reinvest returns, and diversify over time.

The best passive income stream is the one you'll actually start.
