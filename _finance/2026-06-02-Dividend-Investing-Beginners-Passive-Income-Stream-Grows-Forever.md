What if your money worked a second job while you slept?

Dividend investing is one of the oldest wealth-building strategies in history. You buy shares of profitable companies, and those companies pay you a portion of their profits — every quarter, automatically, for as long as you hold the stock.

Done right, this creates a **passive income stream that grows larger every year without you doing anything**.

---

## How Dividends Work

![Passive income concept with coins growing into a tree](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1526304640581-d334cdbbf45e?w=1000&auto=format&fit=crop)

*Photo by micheile henderson on Unsplash*

When a company earns more profit than it needs to reinvest in growth, it can distribute that excess cash to shareholders as **dividends**.

**Example:**
- You own 100 shares of Johnson & Johnson
- J&J pays a $1.25 quarterly dividend per share
- You receive $125 every 3 months — $500 per year
- You did nothing except hold the stock

Over time, most dividend companies **increase their dividend annually**. J&J has raised its dividend every single year for over 60 consecutive years.

---

## Key Dividend Metrics to Understand

### Dividend Yield
`Annual dividend per share ÷ Current stock price × 100`

- A stock trading at $100 that pays $4/year has a **4% yield**
- Higher isn't always better — very high yields (8%+) can signal trouble

### Dividend Payout Ratio
`Annual dividends paid ÷ Annual earnings per share × 100`

- Under 60%: sustainable
- 60-80%: watch carefully
- Over 80%: potential dividend cut risk

### Dividend Growth Rate
How much the dividend increases each year. A company growing dividends 7% annually **doubles your income in 10 years**.

## DRIP: The Secret to Accelerating Growth

**Dividend Reinvestment Plan (DRIP)** automatically uses your dividends to buy more shares.

Instead of taking the cash, you buy more stock → which pays more dividends → which buys even more stock.

This creates a **compounding snowball effect** that dramatically accelerates wealth building in the early years.

**Recommendation:** Use DRIP until you need the income. Then switch to receiving cash dividends.

## Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Chasing yield** — A 12% yield often means the dividend will be cut. Quality matters more than yield.
2. **Ignoring dividend growth** — A 2% yield growing 10%/year beats a 5% yield growing 0%/year after 10 years.
3. **Not diversifying** — Own at least 15-20 stocks across different sectors, or use ETFs.
4. **Selling during downturns** — Dividend investors celebrate crashes (you can buy more shares at lower prices).
5. **Neglecting taxes** — Qualified dividends are taxed at lower rates. Understand your tax situation.

## Bottom Line

Dividend investing won't make you rich overnight. But it will build an ever-growing stream of passive income that requires almost no ongoing effort.

The journey: Start with dividend ETFs like SCHD or VIG, reinvest everything, and add consistently. In 15-20 years, you'll have a meaningful income stream that didn't exist before.

**Your money, quietly working while you live your life.**
