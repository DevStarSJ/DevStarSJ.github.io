For decades, real estate was the wealth-building tool of the rich — you needed large down payments, good credit, and the bandwidth to manage properties. Today, **real estate crowdfunding** has democratized access, letting anyone invest in income-producing properties for as little as $10.

## What Is Real Estate Crowdfunding?

Real estate crowdfunding platforms pool money from thousands of small investors to purchase commercial and residential properties. You earn returns through:

- **Rental income distributions** — monthly or quarterly cash flow from tenants
- **Property appreciation** — your share of any increase in property value when sold
- **Interest payments** — if you're lending money (debt-based platforms)

Instead of buying a $300,000 rental property yourself, you might invest $1,000 alongside 299 other investors in the same deal.

## How It Compares to REITs

Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) are publicly traded companies that own income-producing real estate. They're easy to buy through any brokerage and are highly liquid.

**REITs vs. Real Estate Crowdfunding:**

| Feature | REITs | Crowdfunding |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| Minimum investment | $1 (single share) | $10–$25,000 |
| Liquidity | High (sell any day) | Low (locked 1–10 years) |
| Diversification | High (basket of properties) | Medium (specific deals) |
| Returns | 4–8% average | 8–12%+ potential |
| Market correlation | High (trades like stock) | Low (less correlated) |
| Accredited required | No | Sometimes |

Crowdfunding can offer higher potential returns with lower market correlation — but at the cost of liquidity and higher risk.

## Top Real Estate Crowdfunding Platforms

### For Non-Accredited Investors (Anyone Can Join)

**Fundrise** *(minimum: $10)*
The most popular crowdfunding platform for beginners. Invests in diversified real estate portfolios across the US. Earns quarterly dividends and long-term appreciation. Historical returns: 8–13% annually. 0.85% annual management fee.

**Arrived Homes** *(minimum: $100)*
Specializes in single-family rental homes. You buy fractional shares in homes, earn rental income monthly, and participate in appreciation when the home sells. Very beginner-friendly.

**DiversyFund** *(minimum: $500)*
Focuses on multifamily apartment buildings. Growth-oriented strategy; no quarterly dividends but targets 10–18% total returns upon property sale.

### For Accredited Investors ($200K+ income or $1M+ net worth)

**Crowdstreet** *(minimum: $25,000)*
Direct access to institutional-quality commercial real estate deals. Higher minimums, higher potential returns (12–20%+), and more due diligence required.

**EquityMultiple** *(minimum: $5,000)*
Mix of debt and equity investments in commercial real estate. Target returns of 7–12% for debt, 15–25% for equity deals.

**RealtyMogul** *(minimum: $5,000)*
Both REITs (non-accredited) and individual deals (accredited). Good track record, transparent reporting.

## The Risk Profile: What Can Go Wrong

Real estate crowdfunding is **not risk-free**. Potential risks include:

1. **Illiquidity** — Most platforms lock your capital for 3–10 years. Emergency withdrawals are difficult or impossible.
2. **Platform risk** — If the crowdfunding company fails, your investment could be at risk (though underlying property should still exist).
3. **Deal-specific risk** — A single property investment could underperform due to bad tenants, market shifts, or construction overruns.
4. **Lack of FDIC protection** — Unlike bank accounts, these investments aren't guaranteed.
5. **Market downturns** — Real estate values can fall, particularly in overheated markets.

**Mitigate by:** diversifying across platforms and deal types, investing only what you can lock up long-term, and understanding each platform's track record.

## Tax Considerations

Real estate crowdfunding has favorable tax treatment in some areas:

- **Depreciation pass-through** — Some platforms pass along paper depreciation deductions, reducing your taxable income from distributions
- **Capital gains** — Long-term property sales typically taxed at lower capital gains rates
- **K-1 forms** — Some investments issue K-1 forms instead of 1099s, complicating tax filing

**Recommendation:** Consider holding crowdfunding investments in a **Self-Directed IRA** to defer or eliminate taxes on distributions. Consult a CPA familiar with real estate taxation.

## Is Real Estate Crowdfunding Right for You?

**Good fit if:**
- You want real estate exposure without the work of being a landlord
- You have money you won't need for 3–7 years
- You want to diversify beyond stocks and bonds
- You're looking for potential inflation hedge

**Not a good fit if:**
- You might need the money in emergencies
- You're uncomfortable with illiquidity
- You haven't built your emergency fund and basic investments first

## The Bottom Line

Real estate crowdfunding is a legitimate way to earn passive income from property without the headaches of landlording. Start with Fundrise ($10 minimum) to understand the mechanics, then diversify across platforms as your conviction and capital grow.

Real estate built fortunes for the previous generation. Crowdfunding means it can build yours — without needing hundreds of thousands in capital to start.
