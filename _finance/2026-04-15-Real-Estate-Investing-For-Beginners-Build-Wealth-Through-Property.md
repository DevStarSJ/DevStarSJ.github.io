## Why Real Estate?

Real estate is unique as an investment because it offers **multiple return streams** simultaneously:

1. **Cash flow** — monthly rental income after expenses
2. **Appreciation** — the property increases in value over time
3. **Mortgage paydown** — tenants pay off your loan
4. **Tax advantages** — depreciation, deductions, 1031 exchanges
5. **Leverage** — control a $400,000 asset with $80,000 down (20%)

No other common investment offers all five at once.

## Ways to Invest in Real Estate

### 1. Buy Rental Properties (Traditional)
Purchase a property, rent it out, and collect monthly income. The most hands-on approach, but also the most lucrative when done right.

**Best for:** Investors who want control, are comfortable being a landlord (or hiring a property manager), and have capital for a down payment.

**Key metrics:**
- **Cap Rate:** Net Operating Income ÷ Property Value × 100 (aim for 5–8%)
- **Cash-on-cash return:** Annual cash flow ÷ Cash invested × 100 (target 8–12%)
- **1% Rule:** Monthly rent should be ≥1% of purchase price (e.g., $300k house → $3,000/month rent)

### 2. House Hacking
Buy a multi-family property (duplex, triplex), live in one unit, and rent the others. Your tenants cover your mortgage — you live nearly free while building equity.

**Example:** Buy a $400k duplex with 3.5% FHA loan ($14,000 down), rent one side for $1,800/month, your mortgage is $2,400/month → you pay only $600/month to live there.

### 3. REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts)
Buy shares of companies that own income-producing real estate — like publicly traded stocks. Required by law to pay 90%+ of taxable income as dividends.

**Best for:** Beginners with limited capital, those who don't want to manage property.

**Top REITs to know:**
- **Realty Income (O)** — "The Monthly Dividend Company," 65+ years of dividends
- **VICI Properties** — casino and entertainment real estate
- **Public Storage** — self-storage, recession-resistant
- **REIT ETFs:** VNQ (Vanguard Real Estate ETF) for instant diversification

### 4. Real Estate Crowdfunding
Platforms like **Fundrise**, **RealtyMogul**, and **CrowdStreet** let you invest in commercial and residential real estate deals with as little as $10–$500. You own fractional shares of properties.

**Best for:** Passive investors who want real estate exposure without the work.

### 5. Short-Term Rentals (Airbnb)
Rent a property (or spare room) on Airbnb or VRBO. Short-term rentals can generate 2–3x the income of traditional long-term rentals in the right markets.

**Caution:** Higher management burden, local regulations increasingly restrict STRs.

## Rental Property Analysis: A Real Example

Let's analyze a simple rental property:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Purchase price | $250,000 |
| Down payment (20%) | $50,000 |
| Monthly rent | $2,200 |
| Mortgage (P&I) | $1,074 |
| Property taxes | $220 |
| Insurance | $100 |
| Property management (10%) | $220 |
| Maintenance/vacancy reserve | $150 |
| **Total monthly expenses** | **$1,764** |
| **Monthly cash flow** | **$436** |
| **Annual cash flow** | **$5,232** |
| **Cash-on-cash return** | **10.5%** |

That's $5,232/year in passive income *plus* equity building as the tenant pays down the mortgage *plus* property appreciation.

## Financing Your First Investment Property

**Conventional loan:** 20–25% down for investment properties, best rates

**FHA loan:** As low as 3.5% down — but must owner-occupy one unit (perfect for house hacking)

**BRRRR strategy:** Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat — extract your equity after renovation and use it to buy the next property

**Seller financing:** The seller acts as the bank. Great for buyers who can't qualify for traditional loans.

## Common Beginner Mistakes

1. **Not running the numbers** — emotional decisions destroy returns
2. **Underestimating expenses** — budget 1–2% of property value annually for maintenance
3. **Buying in a bad market** — location, location, location still matters
4. **Neglecting due diligence** — always get an inspection and review local rental laws
5. **Over-leveraging** — leaving yourself cash-flow negative from day one is a recipe for disaster

## Getting Started With $10,000 or Less

- **REITs:** Start with $100 — buy VNQ or individual REITs in a brokerage account
- **Fundrise:** Start with $10 — diversified real estate portfolio
- **REI Club / Bigger Pockets:** Join the community, learn the market, build your team before buying

## The Bottom Line

Real estate isn't passive in the beginning — but it becomes increasingly passive once systems are in place. Start with education, analyze dozens of deals before buying your first, and consider a REIT or crowdfunding platform while you learn.

The best real estate investors aren't gamblers — they're analysts who understand the numbers cold, buy in good markets, and manage their properties professionally.

**Your first property is the hardest. Your second is twice as easy. Start learning today.**
