$10,000. It's a number that feels overwhelming if you stare at it all at once.

But break it down? That's just **$833 per month**. Or **$192 per week**. Or **$27 per day**.

Suddenly it sounds a lot more possible.

This isn't about deprivation or eating ramen every night. It's about making intentional decisions with your money — building a system that saves automatically and cuts waste without ruining your life.

Here's the plan.

---

## Step 1: Get Your Numbers on Paper (Day 1)

You can't save what you can't see. Before cutting anything, you need to know exactly where your money goes.

**Do this now:**
1. Download your last 2 months of bank and credit card statements
2. Categorize every transaction: housing, food, transportation, subscriptions, entertainment, etc.
3. Add up each category total

Most people are shocked at what they find. Common surprises:
- **Dining out**: People routinely underestimate this by 2–3x
- **Subscriptions**: The average American spends ~$200/month on subscriptions they've forgotten about
- **Impulse purchases**: Small amounts add up shockingly fast

**The goal:** Know exactly where every dollar went last month. Now you have targets.

## Step 2: Set Up a Dedicated Savings Account

Before you change a single spending habit, open a **separate high-yield savings account** just for your $10,000 goal.

Why separate? Out of sight, out of mind. When savings live in the same account as your spending money, it's too easy to dip in.

**Best high-yield savings accounts (2026):**
- **Marcus by Goldman Sachs** – consistently competitive APY
- **Ally Bank** – popular, user-friendly
- **SoFi** – bonus rate for direct deposit users
- **Discover Online Savings** – no fees, no minimums

These accounts offer **4–5% APY**, so your $10,000 goal also earns meaningful interest as it grows.

**Action item:** Set up an automatic transfer equal to your target weekly savings — the moment your paycheck hits.

## Step 3: Find Your $10,000 (Cutting + Earning)

Here's where most people go wrong: they try to either cut *everything* OR earn *more* — when the real answer is combining both.

### The Cutting Side: Find $400–500/Month

**Housing (if applicable)**
- Negotiate rent at lease renewal — even 5% matters
- Get a roommate: saves $400–800/month instantly
- Refinance if you're a homeowner with a higher-rate mortgage

**Food: The #1 Category to Attack**
- **Meal prep Sunday**: Cook 4–5 meals at once. Saves $200–300/month vs. daily takeout
- **Grocery strategy**: Shop with a list, never hungry. Use apps like Flipp to match sales
- **Coffee**: Making coffee at home vs. daily $6 latte = $130/month saved
- **Rule of thumb**: Every restaurant meal you cook at home saves $15–25

**Subscriptions: Easy Money**
- Cancel everything you haven't used in 30 days
- Share accounts with family (Netflix, Spotify Family, Amazon Prime)
- Use apps like **Rocket Money** or **Trim** to find forgotten subscriptions
- Typical savings: $50–150/month from subscriptions alone

**Transportation**
- Refinance your car loan if rates are better than when you bought
- Shop around for car insurance annually — switching saves average $400/year
- Carpool or use public transit 2–3 days/week

**Entertainment**
- Library cards give free access to books, audiobooks, movies, and magazines
- Use your existing subscriptions fully before adding more
- Invite friends over instead of going out — same connection, fraction of the cost

---

### The Earning Side: Find $300–500/Month

Cutting has limits. Earning has none.

**Quick wins (this week):**
- **Sell stuff you don't use**: eBay, Facebook Marketplace, Poshmark. One person's trash — seriously, one weekend clearing your house can generate $500–2,000
- **Cash back apps**: Rakuten, Ibotta, Capital One Shopping — earn on things you're already buying
- **Switch banks**: Many banks offer $200–400 bonuses for new accounts with direct deposit

**Side income (1–3 months to start):**
- **Freelancing your skills**: writing, design, coding, marketing — Upwork, Fiverr, direct outreach
- **Gig economy**: DoorDash, Instacart, Uber — flexible, start this weekend
- **Tutoring**: Academic, music, sports, languages. $20–60/hour, find students on Wyzant or Craigslist
- **Reselling**: Buy clearance items, sell on Amazon/eBay. Learn one product niche well

**Monetize your assets:**
- **Rent your parking space**: SpotHero, JustPark — $100–300/month in urban areas
- **Airbnb a spare room** — potentially $500–1,500/month depending on location
- **Rent your car**: Turo — $300–700/month if you don't drive much

## Step 5: Automate Everything

The #1 predictor of savings success is **removing willpower from the equation**.

Every time you have to *decide* to save, there's a chance you'll spend instead. Automation eliminates that moment.

**Automation checklist:**
- ✅ Auto-transfer to savings on payday (same day your check clears)
- ✅ Auto-pay all bills (no late fees)
- ✅ Enable round-up savings (Acorns, Chime — rounds every purchase up, saves the difference)
- ✅ Set up side hustle income to auto-sweep to savings account

Treat your savings like a bill. It's non-negotiable. Everything else is spent *after* savings are moved.

## Step 7: What to Do When You Fall Behind

Life happens. You'll have a bad month. Don't let one bad month become twelve.

**When you miss your target:**
- Don't try to "make up" everything at once (causes burnout)
- Instead, add 10–20% extra to next month's auto-transfer
- Review what went wrong — was it a one-time thing, or a systemic issue?
- Adjust the plan, not the goal

**The mindset shift:** You're not "failing" if you save $700 instead of $833 this month. You're still saving $700. That's $700 more than last year.

Progress, not perfection.
