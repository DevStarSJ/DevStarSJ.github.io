# Real Estate Crowdfunding: How to Invest in Property with $500

For decades, real estate investing was reserved for the wealthy. You needed $50,000–$200,000 for a down payment, landlord responsibilities, and the knowledge to manage tenants, maintenance, and vacancies.

Real estate crowdfunding changes all of that. Today you can invest in apartment complexes, commercial buildings, and industrial properties with as little as **$500** — and let professional managers handle everything.

## How Real Estate Crowdfunding Works

Real estate crowdfunding platforms pool money from many investors to purchase, develop, or finance real estate projects.

**Two main models:**

### 1. Equity Crowdfunding
You own a fractional share of the property. You earn money through:
- Rental income distributions (usually quarterly)
- Appreciation when the property is sold

**Risk:** Higher. If the property underperforms, you lose value.
**Return potential:** Higher (8–15% annually on successful deals).

### 2. Debt Crowdfunding
You lend money to real estate developers. They pay you interest; you don't own the property.

**Risk:** Lower. You're a creditor, not an owner.
**Return potential:** Lower but more predictable (7–10% fixed).

## Accredited vs. Non-Accredited Investors

**Accredited investor requirements (SEC):**
- Net worth > $1 million (excluding primary residence), OR
- Annual income > $200,000 (single) or $300,000 (joint) for 2 years

Most Americans are non-accredited. Fundrise, Arrived, and Yieldstreet have strong options for non-accredited investors.

## Key Risks to Understand

### Illiquidity
You can't sell tomorrow. Most crowdfunded real estate locks your money for 2–10 years. Never invest money you might need soon.

### Operator Risk
The platform and property managers make decisions. Vet them carefully. Crowdstreet had a fraud case in 2023 — due diligence matters.

### Market Risk
Real estate values can fall. The 2008 crash reminded everyone of this. Diversify across platforms and property types.

### Platform Risk
If the platform fails, your investment may be frozen or lost. Stick with established platforms with strong track records.

### Fees
Annual management fees of 1–2.5% eat into returns. A 1.5% fee reduces your effective 10% return to 8.5%.

## How to Get Started (Step by Step)

1. **Determine your eligibility** — Accredited or non-accredited?
2. **Start with Fundrise** — Low minimums ($10), diversified, beginner-friendly
3. **Invest no more than 10% of portfolio** in illiquid assets initially
4. **Reinvest dividends** for compounding
5. **Add more platforms** as you get comfortable (Arrived for single-family, RealtyMogul for REITs)
6. **Review quarterly** — Check distributions and property updates

## The Passive Income Reality Check

Real estate crowdfunding is **genuinely passive** — no tenants to deal with, no repairs, no management.

But "passive" doesn't mean "risk-free" or "guaranteed." The best platforms have lost investor money in bad deals.

**Realistic expectations:**
- 7–12% average annual returns (including income + appreciation)
- 2–5 year minimum time horizon
- Some deals will underperform; diversify accordingly

For most investors, **Fundrise + VNQ (REIT ETF)** is the right combination: one provides passive income with professional management, the other provides liquidity and market exposure.
