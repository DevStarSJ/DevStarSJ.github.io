## What Is Reclaim AI?

Reclaim AI is an intelligent calendar automation tool that integrates with Google Calendar to:
- Auto-schedule **tasks** in available time slots
- Protect recurring **habits** (gym, lunch, focus time)
- Automatically **reschedule** when priorities shift
- Coordinate **1:1 meetings** with teammates intelligently

It's less about telling you what to do and more about making sure the important things actually get on your calendar — and stay there.

---

## Core Features

### 1. Smart Tasks
Connect your task list (Asana, Linear, Jira, Todoist, or manual) and Reclaim automatically finds the best time to work on each task, respecting:
- Task priority and deadlines
- Your preferred working hours
- Existing meetings
- Your focus time preferences

When a meeting moves, your tasks automatically reschedule too.

### 2. Habits
Create recurring blocks that Reclaim protects but flexes around your schedule:
- Morning routine (9:00–9:30 AM daily)
- Lunch break (12:00–1:00 PM)
- Focus time (90 min, 3x per week)
- Exercise, end-of-day review, etc.

If a meeting lands on your habit block, Reclaim moves the habit to the next available slot instead of just deleting it.

### 3. Scheduling Links
Like Calendly, but smarter. Share a link and Reclaim finds optimal meeting times that:
- Preserve your focus blocks
- Avoid back-to-back meetings
- Match everyone's working hours

### 4. 1:1 Meeting Sync
Connect with teammates and Reclaim intelligently schedules recurring 1:1s at times that work for both people — automatically adjusting as schedules change.

### 5. Buffer Time
Automatically adds travel/prep/recovery time before and after meetings. No more back-to-back calls with zero breathing room.

## Setup Guide

### Step 1: Connect Google Calendar
1. Sign up at [reclaim.ai](https://reclaim.ai)
2. Connect your Google account
3. Reclaim reads your existing events — nothing deleted

### Step 2: Define Working Hours
Tell Reclaim when you work and what hours are "prime" for deep work.

```
Working hours: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Focus time preference: 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM (peak energy)
No meetings before: 10:00 AM
```

### Step 3: Create Habits
Add your recurring commitments:
- **Daily:** Morning review (30 min), Lunch (60 min)
- **3x week:** Focus work (90 min), Exercise (60 min)
- **Weekly:** Weekly review (60 min Friday)

### Step 4: Connect Tasks
Link Asana/Linear/Jira or add tasks directly. Set:
- Estimated duration
- Priority level
- Deadline
- Earliest start date

Reclaim does the rest.
