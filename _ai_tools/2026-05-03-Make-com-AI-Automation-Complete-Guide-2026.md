# Make.com AI: The No-Code Automation Platform for AI Workflows (2026 Guide)

![Automation and integration workflows](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1518770660439-4636190af475?w=1200&auto=format&fit=crop)
*Photo by Alexandre Debiève on Unsplash*

In the age of AI agents, **Make.com** (formerly Integromat) has evolved from a simple app integration tool to a full-blown AI workflow orchestration platform. With its visual drag-and-drop editor, 2,000+ app connections, and native AI modules for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and more — Make is the backbone of countless automated businesses in 2026.

---

## What Is Make.com?

Make is a **no-code visual automation platform** that lets you build complex workflows (called "scenarios") by connecting apps, APIs, and AI models in a flowchart-style interface.

Unlike Zapier's simple linear triggers-and-actions model, Make supports:
- **Multi-branch workflows** with conditions and routing
- **Loops and iterators** for processing arrays
- **Error handling** with fallback paths
- **Custom API calls** to any REST endpoint
- **Data transformation** with built-in functions

In 2026, Make added dedicated **AI Agents** and **AI modules** that let you incorporate LLMs directly into any workflow step.

## Key Features

### 🔗 2,000+ App Integrations

Make connects to virtually everything: Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, Airtable, Shopify, Stripe, Twitter/X, Discord, GitHub, HubSpot, and thousands more — plus any service with an API via HTTP modules.

### 🤖 Native AI Modules

Make's AI toolkit in 2026 includes:

- **OpenAI / ChatGPT** — text generation, image generation (DALL-E), transcription (Whisper)
- **Anthropic Claude** — long-context summarization and analysis
- **Google Gemini** — multimodal tasks
- **Cohere, Mistral, Hugging Face** — open model options
- **AI Router** — route tasks to different models based on cost or capability

### 🎭 Visual Scenario Builder

Drag modules onto a canvas, connect them with wires, and configure each with a visual form. No YAML, no JSON config files — just point and click.

### 📊 Data Stores

Make includes built-in key-value stores so your scenarios can persist state, cache data, and build simple databases without external services.

## Building AI Workflows: Real Examples

### Example 1: Automated Customer Support Triage

**Trigger:** New email arrives in Gmail  
**Step 1:** Extract email subject and body  
**Step 2:** Claude module — classify urgency (high/medium/low) and extract key details  
**Step 3:** Router — high urgency → create Zendesk ticket + Slack alert; low urgency → draft reply with ChatGPT → send  

**Time saved:** ~3 hours/day for a small team

### Example 2: Content Repurposing Pipeline

**Trigger:** New blog post published (RSS)  
**Step 1:** Fetch full article content  
**Step 2:** ChatGPT — create Twitter thread (10 tweets)  
**Step 3:** ChatGPT — write LinkedIn post  
**Step 4:** ChatGPT — summarize for email newsletter  
**Step 5:** Schedule all posts via Buffer/Hootsuite  

**Time saved:** 45 minutes per article

### Example 3: AI-Powered Lead Enrichment

**Trigger:** New lead in HubSpot CRM  
**Step 1:** Get company domain from email  
**Step 2:** Clearbit API — fetch company data  
**Step 3:** Perplexity/Web search — find recent news about company  
**Step 4:** GPT-4 — generate personalized outreach draft  
**Step 5:** Update HubSpot with enriched data and draft

## Getting Started

1. Sign up at [make.com](https://make.com) (free tier: 1,000 ops/month)
2. Click **Create a new scenario**
3. Add your first module (e.g., "Watch Gmail for new emails")
4. Chain an **OpenAI** module to process the email content
5. Add output modules (Slack, Google Sheets, etc.)
6. Turn on your scenario and watch it run

**Tips:**
- Use **instant triggers** (webhooks) for real-time workflows
- Explore the **Template library** for pre-built AI scenarios
- Enable **error notifications** so you know when something breaks
