## Core Features

### 1. Text-to-Video
Turn written descriptions into cinematic video clips:

**Example prompts that work exceptionally well:**

```
"A lone astronaut walks across the red surface of Mars, 
sun setting on the horizon, cinematic wide shot, 
golden hour lighting, 8K, photorealistic"

"Close-up of a steaming cup of coffee being placed on 
a wooden table in a cozy café, shallow depth of field, 
warm lighting, 4K cinematic"

"A young woman runs through a field of sunflowers in 
slow motion, golden hour, film grain, dreamlike quality"
```

### 2. Image-to-Video (I2V)
Upload any image and bring it to life:

1. Upload your reference image (portrait, landscape, product)
2. Describe how you want it to move
3. Hailuo generates a 6-10 second video

**Best use cases for I2V:**
- **Portraits:** Make still photos of people blink, smile, turn their head
- **Products:** Rotate products, add liquid drops, create dynamic product ads
- **Landscapes:** Add moving clouds, flowing water, swaying trees
- **Art/Illustrations:** Animate artwork and paintings

### 3. Director Mode (Advanced)
Hailuo's Director Mode gives you camera control:

```
Camera Controls:
- Zoom In / Zoom Out
- Pan Left / Pan Right  
- Tilt Up / Tilt Down
- Rotate / Orbit around subject
- Dolly (push toward or pull from subject)
- Truck (camera slides sideways)
- Crane (camera moves up/down on arc)
```

### 4. Character Reference
Upload a photo of a face and have Hailuo maintain that character's appearance throughout a video:

```
Upload: [Photo of your character]
Prompt: "The character walks into a coffee shop, 
looks around, smiles, and sits down at a table"

Hailuo maintains the face consistently through all motion
```

## Step-by-Step: Creating Your First Video

### Step 1: Access Hailuo
- **Primary platform:** [hailuoai.video](https://hailuoai.video)
- **Also available via:** MiniMax API for developers

### Step 2: Choose Your Generation Mode
```
Text to Video → Pure text description
Image to Video → Upload image + motion description
Director Mode → Advanced camera control options
```

### Step 3: Craft Your Prompt

**The Hailuo Prompt Formula:**
```
[Subject] + [Action] + [Setting] + [Camera/Style] + [Mood/Lighting]

Example:
Subject: "A golden retriever puppy"
Action: "runs along the beach, jumping over waves"
Setting: "at sunset on a tropical beach"
Camera: "medium tracking shot, following the dog"  
Mood: "joyful, cinematic, warm golden light"

Full prompt: "A golden retriever puppy runs along the beach 
at sunset on a tropical beach, jumping over waves, 
medium tracking shot following the dog, joyful and cinematic, 
warm golden light, 4K quality"
```

### Step 4: Set Parameters
- **Duration:** 3s, 6s, or 10s
- **Resolution:** 720p or 1080p
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (landscape), 9:16 (vertical/mobile), 1:1 (square)

### Step 5: Generate and Download
- Generation time: 2-5 minutes
- Download as MP4 (no watermark on paid plans)
- Free tier includes watermark

## Tips for Best Results

### Do's ✅
- **Be specific:** More detail = better results
- **Include lighting:** Light descriptions dramatically improve quality
- **Reference film styles:** "cinematography style of [movie]" works well
- **Specify camera movement:** Even subtle movement descriptions help
- **Use adjectives:** "lush", "ethereal", "gritty", "pristine"

### Don'ts ❌
- Don't ask for very long action sequences (stick to simple, continuous motion)
- Don't include too many subjects or complex interactions
- Don't expect perfect text rendering in video frames
- Don't use vague prompts like "make a nice video"
