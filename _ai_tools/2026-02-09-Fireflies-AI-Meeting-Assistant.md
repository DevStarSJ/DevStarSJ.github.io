## What is Fireflies.ai?

Fireflies.ai is an **AI-powered meeting assistant** that automatically records, transcribes, and summarizes your meetings. It joins your video calls as a participant, captures everything, and turns conversations into searchable, actionable notes.

### Why Teams Love Fireflies

- **Automatic Recording**: Joins Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, and more
- **Accurate Transcription**: 90%+ accuracy with speaker identification
- **AI Summaries**: Key points, action items, and decisions extracted
- **Searchable History**: Find any conversation with keyword search
- **Integration Ready**: Connects with 50+ tools (Slack, Notion, CRM)

## How It Works

### 1. Connect Your Calendar

Fireflies reads your calendar and automatically joins scheduled meetings:

```
1. Sign up at fireflies.ai
2. Connect Google Calendar or Outlook
3. Grant meeting access permissions
4. Fireflies joins your meetings automatically
```

### 2. The Bot Joins Your Call

A participant named "Fireflies.ai Notetaker" joins your meeting:

- Works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex
- Records audio (and video if enabled)
- Runs silently in the background

### 3. Get Your Notes

After the meeting ends, Fireflies processes the recording:

- **Transcript**: Word-for-word transcription with timestamps
- **Summary**: AI-generated overview of key points
- **Action Items**: Tasks mentioned during the call
- **Topics**: Automatic categorization of discussion themes

## Getting Started

### Quick Setup (5 minutes)

```
1. Visit fireflies.ai and sign up
2. Connect your calendar (Google/Outlook)
3. Choose which meetings to record
4. Set notification preferences
5. Done! Fireflies joins your next meeting
```

### Customization Options

- **Auto-join rules**: Only internal, only external, all meetings
- **Notification settings**: Real-time or daily digest
- **Privacy controls**: Announce recording, hide sensitive meetings
- **Output format**: Transcript, summary, or both

### Meeting Etiquette

When Fireflies joins, attendees see the bot. Best practices:

1. Announce recording at meeting start
2. Give attendees option to opt-out
3. Follow company recording policies
4. Check local consent laws
