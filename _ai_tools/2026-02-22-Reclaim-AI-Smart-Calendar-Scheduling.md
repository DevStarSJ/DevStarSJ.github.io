# Reclaim.ai: The AI Scheduler That Defends Your Time Automatically

![Organized desk calendar and planner](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1506784365847-bbad939e9335?w=800)

Most calendar apps show you what you've committed to. **Reclaim.ai** fights for your time. It's an AI scheduling assistant that automatically protects space for focus work, habits, and personal priorities — dynamically rescheduling around meetings, deadlines, and calendar conflicts without you lifting a finger. If you've ever ended a week having done nothing but attend meetings, Reclaim might be the tool that changes that.

## What is Reclaim.ai?

Reclaim.ai is an AI-powered calendar optimization platform that:

- **Automatically schedules Tasks** from your to-do list into available calendar slots
- **Protects Habits** (workouts, lunch, learning) by holding time that can be bumped only intelligently
- **Schedules Focus Time** by blocking deep work windows based on your productivity patterns
- **Facilitates Smart Meetings** by finding optimal slots for all parties, avoiding fragmentation
- **Syncs with Google Calendar** bidirectionally in real time

The core philosophy: your calendar should reflect your priorities, not just your commitments.

## Key Features

### 1. Tasks — Auto-Scheduled To-Do Items

Connect your task managers or create tasks directly in Reclaim:

- Set **priority** (P1–P4), **duration**, and **deadline**
- Reclaim finds available slots and schedules them automatically
- If a meeting takes a task's slot, Reclaim reschedules it elsewhere before the deadline
- Native integrations with: **Asana, Todoist, Linear, Jira, ClickUp, Google Tasks**

**Example:** You add "Write Q1 report" with 4 hours needed, due Friday. Reclaim will automatically block 4 hours across your available windows before Friday, rearranging as your schedule changes.

### 2. Habits — Recurring Flexible Blocks

Habits are recurring activities that need to happen regularly but can flex within a window:

- **Exercise**: Hold 45 min daily, ideally before 9am, but allow move to up to 12pm
- **Deep work**: 2 hours every weekday, in the morning zone
- **Learning**: 30 min, afternoons only, 3x per week
- **Lunch**: 45 min, between 12–2pm, protected from meetings

Habits have **ideal times** and **acceptable windows**. Reclaim schedules them at the ideal time but can move them within the window if conflicts arise.

### 3. Smart Meetings

Reclaim's Smart Meetings feature makes scheduling with others dramatically better:

- **Scheduling Links**: Share a personal link; Reclaim shows slots that fit your optimal meeting times
- **Team Scheduling**: Finds slots that work for all attendees while minimizing schedule fragmentation
- **Buffer Time**: Automatically adds travel/prep time around meetings
- **Meeting Breaks**: Prevents back-to-back meetings by holding buffer slots

### 4. Focus Time Protection

Reclaim identifies your peak productivity hours and defends them:

- Analyzes your historical calendar to understand your patterns
- Blocks Focus Time during your high-energy windows
- Uses **No Meeting Zones** to prevent meetings from fragmenting your day
- Can sync Focus Time blocks to Slack as **Do Not Disturb** automatically

### 5. Work-Life Balance Controls

Set hard boundaries that Reclaim respects:

| Setting | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Work hours | Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm only |
| Personal time | Evenings and weekends blocked |
| Commute buffer | 30 min before/after work blocks |
| Max meetings/day | No more than 4 meetings in one day |

## Getting Started

### Setup Steps

1. Visit [reclaim.ai](https://reclaim.ai) and sign up
2. Connect your **Google Calendar** (required; Outlook coming)
3. Set your **work hours** and **personal time**
4. Create your first **Habit** (e.g., lunch, exercise)
5. Connect a **task manager** or add tasks directly
6. Review the auto-scheduled blocks Reclaim creates

Initial setup takes about 15 minutes. By end of day one, your calendar will look meaningfully different.

### Configuring Habits

Navigate to **Habits** and click **New Habit**:

```
Name: Morning Exercise
Duration: 45 minutes
Frequency: Every weekday
Ideal time: 7:00 – 8:00 AM
Acceptable window: 6:00 – 10:00 AM
Priority: Medium
```

Reclaim will place this at your ideal time and only move it within the window if necessary.

### Task Priorities and Time Windows

When creating tasks, set constraints:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Priority | P1 = must-do; P4 = nice-to-do |
| Duration | Estimated total work time |
| Deadline | Latest completion date |
| Scheduling window | Times when this task can be placed |
| Snooze until | Don't schedule before this date |

High-priority tasks with tight deadlines get scheduled first; low-priority ones fill remaining gaps.
