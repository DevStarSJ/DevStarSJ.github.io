## What Is Adobe Firefly?

Adobe Firefly is a **family of generative AI models** trained on licensed content (Adobe Stock, openly licensed content, public domain). This "commercially safe" training is Firefly's biggest differentiator — images you generate are **safe to use in commercial work** without copyright concerns.

### The Firefly Model Family (2026)
- **Firefly Image 3:** Text-to-image, Style Match
- **Firefly Vector:** Text-to-vector (SVG generation)
- **Firefly Design:** Template generation, layout creation
- **Firefly Video:** Text-to-video (in Premiere Pro)

## Firefly's Core Features

### 1. Text to Image
Generate photorealistic images, illustrations, and artwork from text descriptions. Firefly excels at:
- Product photography backdrops
- Concept art and mood boards
- Marketing visuals
- Social media graphics

**Sample prompt:**
```
A minimalist flat-lay of coffee products on a white marble surface,
soft morning light from the left, commercial photography style,
ultra-sharp detail
```

### 2. Generative Fill (Photoshop)
The killer feature. Select any area of a photo, type what you want, and Firefly fills it seamlessly — matching lighting, perspective, and texture.

Use cases:
- Remove distracting objects from photos
- Extend image backgrounds (Generative Expand)
- Replace skies, outfits, products
- Add elements that weren't in the original shoot

### 3. Generative Expand
Upload a horizontal image, expand the canvas to vertical, and Firefly fills the new areas intelligently. Perfect for repurposing photography for different aspect ratios without reshooting.

### 4. Text Effects
Apply stunning typographic effects using AI — fur, liquid metal, neon glow, woven fabric, fire. Each letter is rendered with 3D depth and material accuracy.

### 5. Recolor (Illustrator)
Upload a vector illustration, describe a new color palette, and Firefly recolors the entire piece. Essential for brand consistency across seasonal campaigns.

### 6. Structure Reference & Style Reference
- **Structure Reference:** Upload an image, use its composition without copying its style
- **Style Reference:** Apply the visual style of a reference image to new generations

## Getting Started: Step-by-Step

### Option 1: Firefly Web App
1. Go to [firefly.adobe.com](https://firefly.adobe.com)
2. Sign in with your Adobe ID (free tier available)
3. Choose a module (Text to Image, Generative Fill, etc.)
4. Enter your prompt and click Generate

### Option 2: In Photoshop (Recommended for Professionals)
1. Open any image in Photoshop (2024 or later)
2. Use the **Lasso or Rectangular Marquee** to select an area
3. Click **Generative Fill** in the contextual toolbar
4. Type a description (or leave empty to remove the selection)
5. Click Generate — Photoshop creates 3 variations in a new layer

### Option 3: Adobe Express
For quick marketing assets, Firefly is embedded throughout Express — generating images, removing backgrounds, and creating social media graphics without Photoshop expertise.

## Prompt Writing for Firefly

Firefly responds well to structured prompts:

### The Formula
```
[Subject] + [Setting/Context] + [Lighting] + [Style] + [Camera/Mood]
```

### Example: Product Shot
```
A luxury perfume bottle, placed on a dark obsidian surface,
dramatic single-source lighting from above, commercial product photography,
crisp focus with bokeh background, high-end editorial magazine style
```

### Example: Marketing Visual
```
A diverse group of young professionals celebrating in a modern office,
golden hour sunlight through floor-to-ceiling windows,
candid authentic moment, shallow depth of field,
warm color grading, lifestyle photography
```

### Power Modifiers
- **Lighting:** "soft diffused light," "dramatic rim lighting," "golden hour," "studio strobe"
- **Style:** "hyperrealistic," "watercolor illustration," "3D render," "editorial photography"
- **Mood:** "cinematic," "dreamlike," "energetic," "melancholic"
- **Technical:** "8K resolution," "tilt-shift," "macro photography," "wide-angle lens"

## Tips & Best Practices

1. **Use Content Credentials** — Firefly adds metadata proving AI origin; useful for compliance
2. **Structure Reference > Style Reference** — Composition references give more predictable results
3. **Iterate fast** — Generate 4 at a time, keep the best, refine from there
4. **Be specific with lighting** — Lighting direction dramatically affects realism
5. **Combine with real photography** — Firefly's best results often combine real foreground with generated backgrounds
