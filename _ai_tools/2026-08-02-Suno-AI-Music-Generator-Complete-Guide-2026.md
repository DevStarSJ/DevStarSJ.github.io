## What Is Suno AI?

Suno is an AI music generation platform that transforms text prompts into complete songs. Founded in 2023 and based in Cambridge, MA, Suno has quickly become the go-to tool for:

- **Content creators** who need custom background music

Suno v4 (the current version as of 2026) can generate songs up to 4 minutes long with remarkably coherent structure, consistent style, and lyrics that actually make sense.

## How Suno Works

### Simple Mode: Just a Prompt

The fastest way to use Suno is to type a description in the "Song Description" box:

> *"An upbeat country pop song about a road trip through Texas, female vocalist, acoustic guitar and drums"*

Hit generate, and within 30 seconds you get two song variations. Pick the one you like, extend it, or regenerate.

### Custom Mode: Full Control

For more control, switch to Custom Mode:

- **Write your own lyrics** — paste in verses, chorus, and bridge
- **Set the style prompt** — "dreamy lo-fi hip hop with vinyl crackle"
- **Name the song** — appears in the metadata

This gives you much more precise output and is the mode professionals use.

### Style Prompting: The Secret to Great Results

The style field is where Suno shines. Some effective patterns:

| Goal | Style Prompt Example |
|------|---------------------|
| Cinematic trailer | `epic orchestral, Hans Zimmer-style, rising tension, no lyrics` |
| 90s R&B | `90s R&B, smooth vocals, slow jam, neo-soul, 808 bass` |
| Indie folk | `indie folk, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, breathy female vocals, melancholic` |
| Electronic dance | `progressive house, 128 BPM, euphoric drop, festival anthem` |
| K-pop inspired | `K-pop, bright synths, tight vocal chops, catchy hook, polished production` |

The more specific and genre-aware your prompt, the better the output.

## Suno v4 Key Features

### Longer Songs
Suno v4 supports songs up to **4 minutes** with the "Continue" feature. You can chain generations together to build full-length tracks.

### Remaster
Take an older Suno song and remaster it with improved audio quality using the v4 model — great for salvaging great ideas from earlier versions.

### Cover Mode
Upload or describe an existing song and Suno will create a cover in a different style. Turn a heavy metal song into bossa nova, or a pop hit into an orchestral arrangement.

### Personas
Save specific voice and style combinations as "Personas" to maintain a consistent artist identity across multiple tracks.

### Stems (Pro)
Pro subscribers can download individual stems — vocals, drums, bass, instruments separately — making Suno output useful in professional DAWs like Ableton or Logic Pro.

## Tips for Better Results

1. **Be genre-specific** — "indie rock" beats just "rock"; "UK garage" beats "electronic"
2. **Mention instruments explicitly** — "Rhodes piano, upright bass, brushed drums"
3. **Set the mood** — "melancholic," "euphoric," "tense," "nostalgic"
4. **Reference era** — "80s synth-pop" or "2000s R&B" dramatically narrows the style
5. **Use the Extend feature** to build out song sections, not just regenerate
6. **Iterate, don't abandon** — small prompt tweaks often unlock dramatically better results
