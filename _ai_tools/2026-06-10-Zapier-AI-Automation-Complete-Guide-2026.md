## What Is Zapier?

Zapier is the world's largest automation platform, connecting 6,000+ apps. It works on a simple trigger/action model:

- **Trigger**: Something happens (new email, form submission, calendar event)
- **Action**: Something gets done (send a message, create a record, update a spreadsheet)

A single automation is called a **Zap**. With AI, you now build Zaps by describing them in natural language instead of configuring them manually.

## Zapier AI Features in 2026

### 🤖 AI Zap Builder
The headline feature. Describe your automation goal in plain language:

> "When someone fills out my Typeform contact form, add them to my Mailchimp list and send me a Slack message with their details"

Zapier AI:
1. Identifies the apps involved (Typeform, Mailchimp, Slack)
2. Maps out the trigger and actions
3. Pre-fills the configuration
4. Suggests steps you might have missed
5. You review and activate

**What used to take 20 minutes of setup now takes 2 minutes of describing.**

### 🧠 AI Steps (Powered by ChatGPT)
Add AI as a *step inside your automation*. Use it to:
- **Transform data** — Reformat addresses, standardize phone numbers
- **Write content** — Generate personalized email from form responses
- **Classify** — Tag support tickets by topic automatically
- **Extract info** — Pull out specific fields from unstructured text
- **Translate** — Convert content to different languages mid-workflow
- **Summarize** — Condense long emails or documents automatically

### 💬 Zapier Chatbots
Build custom AI chatbots in minutes, connected to your data:
- Customer support bots trained on your knowledge base
- Internal tools for HR FAQs, IT helpdesk
- Lead qualification bots that connect directly to your CRM

### 🔌 AI-Powered Canvas (Visual Builder)
The enhanced visual workflow builder now suggests:
- Next steps based on what you've configured so far
- Error handling you might have forgotten
- Optimizations based on how others use similar Zaps

## Setting Up Your First AI-Powered Zap

1. **Go to zapier.com** and sign in
2. Click **Create Zap**
3. Select **Describe your Zap** (the AI builder option)
4. Type your workflow in plain English
5. Review what Zapier built and adjust if needed
6. Connect your app accounts when prompted
7. Test the Zap and turn it on

**Pro tip:** Be specific about what triggers what. "When X happens, do Y, then Z" is clearer than "connect X and Y together."

---

## Adding AI Steps to Existing Zaps

1. Open any existing Zap in the editor
2. Click the **+** button to add a step
3. Search for **"AI by Zapier"**
4. Choose an action: Transform, Format, Generate, Classify, etc.
5. Write your AI instruction in the text field
6. Map the output to the next step

**Example instruction for email summarization:**
{% raw %}
```
Summarize the following email in 3 bullet points, highlighting 
the main request and any deadlines mentioned: {{Email Body}}
```

## Best Practices

### ✅ Do This
- Test every Zap before turning it on
- Use filters to prevent unwanted triggers
- Add error handling paths for critical workflows
- Document what each Zap does (the description field)
- Use Zapier's Task History to audit what ran

### ❌ Avoid This
- Over-automating without testing (broken Zaps can loop)
- Connecting live systems without a staging test
- Storing sensitive data in AI steps
- Building complex logic without fallback handling

## Security Considerations

- Zapier is SOC 2 Type II certified
- You control which apps are connected and what permissions they have
- AI Steps run on OpenAI's infrastructure — review their data processing policies
