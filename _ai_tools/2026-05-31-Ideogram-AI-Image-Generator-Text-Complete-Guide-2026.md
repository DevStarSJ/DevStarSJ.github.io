## Prompt Tips for Best Text Results

1. **Put text in quotes**: `"Your exact text here"` helps the model identify what to render accurately
2. **Specify font style**: "bold sans-serif", "elegant cursive", "retro slab serif"
3. **Describe text placement**: "centered at the top", "bottom left corner"
4. **Keep text short**: Single words or short phrases render more accurately than long sentences
5. **Use Design mode**: For anything with prominent text, Design mode typically performs best
