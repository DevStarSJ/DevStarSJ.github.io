# Taskade AI: The All-in-One AI Project Management and Team Workspace (2026 Guide)

![Team collaboration at desk](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1611532736597-de2d4265fba3?w=800&auto=format&fit=crop)

Project management tools are a dime a dozen. But **Taskade** is doing something different: it's weaving AI agents directly into your project structure, turning task lists into living, intelligent workspaces where AI can research, draft, organize, and execute — right alongside your team.

## What Is Taskade?

Taskade is an AI-powered project management and team collaboration platform. It combines familiar tools — tasks, notes, docs, mind maps, calendars — with a layer of AI that goes far beyond simple autocomplete.

Core capabilities:

- **AI Agents** — assign tasks to AI agents that can browse the web, write documents, and take actions
- **Multiple views** — switch between list, board, calendar, mindmap, and table views
- **AI-generated workflows** — describe a project and Taskade creates the task structure
- **Real-time collaboration** — teammates see changes live
- **AI chat within tasks** — ask questions about your project directly in context
- **Templates library** — 500+ AI-enhanced project templates

## AI Agents: The Killer Feature

What sets Taskade apart in 2026 is its **AI Agent system**. These aren't just chatbots — they're persistent agents tied to your workspace that can:

- **Research topics** and compile findings into notes
- **Draft documents** based on your task descriptions
- **Summarize threads** and meeting notes
- **Execute multi-step tasks** autonomously
- **Integrate with external tools** via actions

You can create a custom agent for your project ("Product Research Agent"), give it a persona and instructions, and it will work within your project context — understanding your goals, your team's history, and your documents.

```
Example: Create an agent called "Competitive Intel" 
→ Assign it: "Research [Competitor X] and summarize their latest features"
→ Agent browses the web, reads their changelog, and creates a summary note
→ Auto-assigns a follow-up task to your team
```
