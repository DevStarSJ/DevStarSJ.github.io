# Taskade AI: The All-in-One AI Workspace for Teams and Solopreneurs

![Organized desk with notebook and productivity tools](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1484480974693-6ca0a78fb36b?w=800)
*Photo by [Lili Popper](https://unsplash.com/@lili_popper) on Unsplash*

Most productivity tools do one thing well. Project management apps manage projects. Note-taking apps store notes. AI tools generate text. **Taskade** is different — it's trying to collapse all of these into a single, AI-native workspace where thinking, planning, doing, and automating all happen in one place.

## What is Taskade?

Taskade is an AI-powered productivity platform launched in 2019 and significantly upgraded with AI capabilities in 2023. It combines:

- **Task & project management** — to-do lists, kanban boards, Gantt charts
- **Note-taking & documents** — rich text notes with AI generation
- **Mind maps & flowcharts** — visual thinking and planning
- **AI agents** — autonomous AI that completes tasks on your behalf
- **Real-time collaboration** — multiplayer editing for teams
- **Workflow automation** — trigger-based automations between apps

The core thesis: instead of switching between Notion, Trello, ChatGPT, and Zapier, you can do it all inside Taskade.

## The AI Agents: Taskade's Killer Feature

Taskade's AI agents are what set it apart from traditional productivity tools. These are persistent AI assistants you configure for specific jobs:

### What AI Agents Can Do

**Project management agent:**
- Break down a project goal into tasks automatically
- Assign priorities and due dates
- Write task descriptions with acceptance criteria
- Generate project timelines

**Research agent:**
- Search the web and summarize findings
- Compile competitive analyses
- Pull relevant information from your document library
- Generate reports from gathered information

**Content agent:**
- Draft blog posts, newsletters, social content
- Follow your brand voice guidelines
- Repurpose existing content for different formats
- Generate content calendars

**Custom agents:**
- Train an agent with your own instructions and context
- Connect it to your documents and knowledge base
- Deploy it to specific projects or share with your team

### Building an Agent

1. Go to **Agents** in the sidebar
2. Click **New Agent**
3. Define the agent's role: *"You are a project manager who helps break down goals into actionable tasks with clear acceptance criteria."*
4. Add context: upload relevant documents, past examples
5. Set the agent's tools (web search, document access, task creation)
6. Deploy to a project workspace

Once deployed, you can chat with the agent or trigger it to run tasks automatically.

## Workflow Automation

Taskade's automation builder (similar to Zapier but built-in) lets you create trigger-action automations:

**Example automations:**

- *When a task is marked complete → Send a Slack message to #team*
- *When a new task is created → AI agent generates a description*
- *Daily at 9 AM → Create today's tasks from a recurring template*
- *When a form is submitted → Create a project from the responses*
- *When a due date is missed → Reassign task and notify the assignee*

Automation triggers include:
- Task events (created, completed, overdue)
- Time-based (daily, weekly, specific date)
- Form submissions
- Webhook events from external apps

Available actions:
- Create tasks, projects, documents
- Run AI agents
- Send notifications (Slack, email, in-app)
- Call external webhooks

## Getting Started: Quick Start Guide

### Day 1 Setup (30 minutes)

1. **Sign up** at [taskade.com](https://taskade.com) — free tier is generous
2. **Create your first workspace** — name it after your main project area (e.g., "Client Work" or "Personal Projects")
3. **Pick a template** — browse 1,000+ templates to find one matching your work
4. **Activate AI** — open any project, type `/ai`, and explore what it generates
5. **Build your first agent** — create a simple task-breakdown agent for your main project

### The 3-Agent Starter Kit

For immediate productivity gains, set up these three agents:

1. **Project Kick-off Agent**: *"Given a project goal, generate a comprehensive task list with priorities, time estimates, and dependencies."*

2. **Daily Planner Agent**: *"Review my open tasks and help me prioritize today's work based on urgency and deadlines."*

3. **Meeting Notes Agent**: *"Convert rough meeting notes into action items with owners, due dates, and follow-up summaries."*

These three cover 80% of the productivity overhead most people face.

## Tips for Power Users

1. **Nest tasks** — Taskade supports unlimited subtask depth for complex breakdowns
2. **Link between docs** — Use `@mention` to connect related projects and documents
3. **Custom fields** — Add metadata to tasks (status, priority, custom labels)
4. **Keyboard shortcuts** — `Ctrl+/` shows full shortcut list; learns fast
5. **Mobile app** — iOS and Android apps with full feature parity
6. **Export** — Markdown, PDF, CSV exports for portability
