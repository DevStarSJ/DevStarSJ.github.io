## What is Loom AI?

Loom is a screen recording and video messaging platform that lets you record your screen, camera, and microphone simultaneously and share the result as a link in seconds. The AI features layer on top to make every video smarter:

- **Auto-generated transcripts** — searchable, accurate, multi-language
- **AI Video Summaries** — key points extracted automatically
- **Auto Chapters** — AI segments your video by topic
- **Action Items** — tasks detected and exported directly to Notion/Jira/Linear
- **Filler Word Removal** — automatically cuts "um," "uh," "like" from recordings
- **Auto Title Generation** — describe the video based on its content
- **AI Message Composer** — turn bullet points into a polished video script
- **Translation** — auto-translate captions into 50+ languages

## Getting Started

### Recording Your First Loom

**Browser Extension (Recommended for most users)**

1. Install [Loom Chrome Extension](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/loom)
2. Click the Loom button in your browser toolbar
3. Choose recording mode:
   - **Screen + Camera** — show your face while presenting
   - **Screen Only** — focus on what's on screen
   - **Camera Only** — talking head videos
4. Choose recording area: Full screen, window, or custom region
5. Hit record (3-2-1 countdown)
6. Stop → link copies to clipboard automatically

**Desktop App**
For higher quality, less browser overhead, and more control, download the Loom desktop app.

**Mobile App**
Record from iPhone/Android for on-the-go video updates.

### AI Features Setup

After recording, Loom AI activates automatically:

1. Navigate to your video in the Loom library
2. The **AI Summary** panel appears on the right
3. Review the auto-generated:
   - Title
   - Summary (3-5 bullet points)
   - Chapters (timestamped sections)
   - Action items

## Loom AI Features in Depth

### Auto Transcripts & Search

Every Loom is transcribed with impressive accuracy. Benefits:
- **Search across all your Looms** by keyword
- **Link to exact timestamp** from the transcript
- **Copy-paste quotes** directly from the video

Languages supported: English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and 40+ more.

### AI Summaries

Instead of watching a 10-minute Loom, a viewer can read the summary in 30 seconds and decide if they need to watch. Format:

```
📋 Summary:
John walks through the Q3 marketing campaign performance, 
highlighting a 23% increase in conversion rate from the new 
landing pages, and proposes doubling the budget for paid search.

📌 Key Points:
• Conversion rate up 23% from redesigned landing pages
• Email campaign underperformed (12% open rate vs 20% target)
• Recommends $50K additional budget for paid search in Q4
• Needs approval from Sarah by October 15

✅ Action Items:
• Review landing page analytics [Sarah]
• Prepare Q4 budget proposal [John - due Oct 10]
• Schedule follow-up call for Q4 planning [Team]
```

### Filler Word Removal

Enable in settings and Loom AI automatically cuts:
- "Um" / "Uh"
- "Like" (filler use)
- "You know"
- Awkward silences longer than 1.5 seconds

Result: a tighter, more professional video with zero editing effort.

### Action Item Extraction & Integration

Loom detects task language ("I'll send you...", "Can you please...", "We need to...") and creates actionable items. Export to:

- **Notion** — creates tasks in your workspace
- **Jira** — generates issues with auto-populated fields
- **Linear** — creates issues in the right team
- **Asana/Monday.com** — syncs tasks bidirectionally
- **Slack** — posts a summary to the relevant channel

### Video Chapters

For longer videos, AI automatically segments by topic:

```
0:00 - Introduction to Q3 results
1:45 - Website conversion analysis
4:20 - Email campaign review
7:10 - Q4 budget recommendations
9:30 - Next steps and timeline
```

Viewers can jump directly to the section they care about.

## Loom AI Tips & Best Practices

### Perfect Your Recording Setup

1. **Lighting**: Face a window or use a ring light — avoid backlight
2. **Background**: Use virtual background or keep it clean
3. **Audio**: USB microphone or AirPods significantly improves quality
4. **Frame rate**: Use 60fps for screen recordings with fast movement

### Scripting with AI Message Composer

Before recording, use the AI composer:
1. Type bullet points of what you want to cover
2. AI expands them into a natural speaking script
3. Record with the script visible as a teleprompter

### Engagement Best Practices

- **Keep it under 5 minutes** for routine updates (AI summary helps for longer)
- **Start with the point** — don't spend 30 seconds saying hello
- **Use chapters** for anything over 3 minutes
- **Add a CTA** — tell viewers exactly what you need from them
- **Enable comments** and respond to create dialogue
