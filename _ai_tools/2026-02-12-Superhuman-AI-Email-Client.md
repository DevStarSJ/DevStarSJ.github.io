## Power User Tips

### 1. Use the Command Palette

Press `Cmd+K` to access any feature without remembering specific shortcuts.

### 2. Master Snooze

Use `H` to snooze emails for:
- Later today
- Tomorrow
- This weekend
- Next week
- Specific date

### 3. Create VIP Splits

Set up splits for:
- Your boss
- Key clients
- Important projects

### 4. Batch Process

Set specific times to process email:
- Morning (8 AM)
- After lunch (1 PM)
- End of day (5 PM)

Avoid constant checking throughout the day.
