## What is Opus Clip?

Opus Clip is an AI-powered video repurposing tool that automatically analyzes long-form videos and extracts the most engaging moments as short clips. It's designed for content creators, marketers, and businesses who want to maximize their content reach across platforms like TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels.

## Why Short-Form Content Matters

The numbers don't lie:
- TikTok users spend **95 minutes per day** on the app
- YouTube Shorts receives **70 billion daily views**
- Instagram Reels have **22% more engagement** than regular posts

But creating short-form content from long videos is time-consuming. That's where Opus Clip shines.

## Key Features

### 1. AI-Powered Clip Selection

Opus Clip uses AI to identify the most viral-worthy moments:

- **Virality Score**: Each clip gets a score predicting engagement potential
- **Hook Detection**: Finds attention-grabbing opening moments
- **Natural Endpoints**: Identifies satisfying clip conclusions
- **Topic Clustering**: Groups related moments together

### 2. Automatic Face Tracking

Keep speakers centered and visible:

- Tracks faces across the frame
- Reframes for vertical video
- Handles multiple speakers
- Smooth, natural movements

### 3. Auto-Generated Captions

Captions are essential for short-form success:

- 97% accuracy in transcription
- Multiple caption styles
- Animated text effects
- Word-by-word highlighting
- Multi-language support

### 4. Brand Customization

Make clips match your brand:

- Custom intro/outro
- Logo watermarks
- Brand color themes
- Font customization
- Template presets

### 5. B-Roll and Filler Removal

AI identifies and removes:

- Awkward pauses
- "Um" and "uh" moments
- Off-topic tangents
- Technical difficulties

## How Opus Clip Works

### Step 1: Upload Your Video

Paste a YouTube/Vimeo URL or upload directly. Supports videos up to 3 hours long.

### Step 2: AI Analysis

Opus Clip analyzes:
- Speech patterns
- Emotional peaks
- Topic transitions
- Visual engagement points

### Step 3: Review Generated Clips

Get 10-20 clips per hour of source video, each with:
- Virality score (0-100)
- Suggested hashtags
- Platform recommendations

### Step 4: Customize and Export

Fine-tune clips with:
- Caption styling
- Crop adjustments
- Length trimming
- Music and effects
