## Gen-3 Alpha Key Features

### 🎬 Text-to-Video
The core capability. Describe a scene in natural language and Gen-3 generates a 5-10 second video clip with:
- Realistic physics and motion
- Cinematic camera work
- Photorealistic lighting and shadows
- Consistent character appearance within the clip

**Example prompts:**
- *"A lone astronaut walks on Mars, red dust swirling around her boots, slow motion, cinematic"*
- *"Coffee being poured into a cup in extreme close-up, droplets splashing, golden hour lighting"*
- *"A crowded Tokyo street at night, neon reflections in rain puddles, bokeh background"*

### 🖼️ Image-to-Video
Upload any still image and animate it. Bring product photos, illustrations, or portraits to life with natural motion. Perfect for:
- Animating artwork
- Creating dynamic product videos from still photos
- Converting infographics into explainer videos

### 🎥 Video-to-Video
Transform existing footage with AI:
- Change the style (make footage look like anime, oil painting, film noir)
- Remove or replace backgrounds
- Alter lighting and atmosphere
- Age or de-age subjects

### ✏️ Motion Brush
Paint motion onto specific regions of an image. Tell one element to move while the rest stays static — making leaves rustle, water flow, or hair blow in the wind. Unprecedented control over AI-generated movement.

### 🔧 Advanced Camera Controls
Specify camera movements with precision:
- Dolly in/out
- Pan left/right
- Tilt up/down
- Orbit (circular motion around subject)
- Crane shot

Combined with subject motion prompts, this enables complex cinematic sequences.

## Prompting Guide for Best Results

### Be Specific About Camera
Instead of: *"A forest"*
Try: *"Slow dolly forward through a misty pine forest at dawn, shafts of light breaking through the canopy, 16mm film grain"*

### Include Lighting Details
*"Golden hour backlight"*, *"harsh midday sun casting long shadows"*, *"neon-lit interior, blue and pink light"*

### Specify Film Style
*"Shot on 35mm film"*, *"4K drone footage"*, *"handheld documentary style"*, *"Kubrick-style symmetry"*

### Use Negative Prompting
Describe what you DON'T want: *"no text, no watermarks, no distorted faces, no sudden cuts"*

## Limitations

- **5-10 second clips**: Gen-3 is not yet for long-form video generation
- **Consistency across shots**: Characters don't maintain appearance between separate generations
- **Text in video**: Rendering readable text in video is unreliable
- **Hands and fingers**: Still a known weakness (as with image AI)
- **Watermarks on free tier**: Runway watermarks free and Basic tier outputs
