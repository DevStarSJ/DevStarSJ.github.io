## What Is Fathom AI?

Fathom is an **AI meeting assistant** that joins your video calls as a bot, records everything, generates transcripts, and produces AI-written summaries — all while you focus on the conversation instead of scrambling to take notes.

What makes Fathom stand out: it's **free for individuals** with a very generous feature set. The transcripts are accurate, the summaries are genuinely useful (not just meeting-length synopses), and it integrates directly with CRMs and project management tools.

## Key Features

### 🎙️ Automatic Recording & Transcription
Fathom joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams calls. It records the meeting and produces a **word-accurate transcript** with speaker identification — you see who said what, timestamped.

### 📝 AI Summaries
After each call, Fathom generates:
- **Action items** extracted from the conversation
- **Key decisions** made during the meeting  
- **Summary paragraphs** by topic
- **Full transcript** for reference

### ⚡ Real-Time Highlights
During the meeting, hit a keyboard shortcut to **highlight important moments**. Fathom clips those moments and adds them to your notes automatically.

### 🔗 CRM Integration
Fathom syncs meeting notes directly to:
- Salesforce
- HubSpot
- Notion
- Slack
- Linear and others

One click after the call and your CRM is updated with the full summary.

### 🌍 Multi-Language Support
Fathom transcribes in 25+ languages and can translate summaries. Useful for international teams where meetings mix languages.

## How to Set Up Fathom

### Step 1: Install the App
Download the Fathom desktop app from [fathom.video](https://fathom.video) or install the Chrome extension. Sign in with Google.

### Step 2: Connect Your Calendar
Authorize Fathom to access your Google Calendar or Outlook. It will automatically detect upcoming meetings and prepare to join.

### Step 3: Run a Meeting
When your meeting starts, Fathom asks your permission to join. Accept, and it begins recording in the background. You'll see the Fathom bot appear as a participant.

**Tip:** You can also invite Fathom to any meeting via its bot email address — useful for one-off calls not on your calendar.

### Step 4: Get Your Summary
Within minutes of the call ending, Fathom emails you the summary and makes it available in your dashboard. Share it with participants with one click.

✅ DECISIONS MADE

- Q3 budget review (minutes 5-18)

This format makes post-meeting action actually happen — the items are already extracted.

## Privacy Considerations

Fathom takes privacy seriously for a product that records conversations:
- Participants are **notified** when Fathom joins
- Meeting hosts can **remove Fathom** at any time
- Recordings are stored encrypted
- You can **delete recordings** at any time
- GDPR and SOC 2 compliant

Some organizations have meeting recording policies — check with your IT/legal team if in doubt.

## Best Practices

**Let participants know Fathom is joining.** Not just legally — it creates a culture of transparency and means people trust that action items will be tracked.

**Use the highlight feature during calls.** When a key decision or commitment is made, tap the shortcut. Review highlights before sending your summary.

**Configure your CRM templates.** Spend 10 minutes setting up which fields Fathom populates in your CRM — it pays off every call after.

**Share recordings selectively.** Fathom makes it easy to share, but not every call needs full distribution. Use "Share summary only" for external stakeholders.
