## Tips for Natural-Sounding Output

1. **Write for speech, not reading**: Short sentences, contractions, natural flow
2. **Use punctuation strategically**: Commas = short pause, periods = longer pause
3. **Mark emphasis**: Use the emphasis tool for key words
4. **Preview often**: Iterate until it sounds right
5. **Match voice to content**: Serious topic? Serious voice.

### Script Writing Example

**❌ Written style:**
"The implementation of artificial intelligence in healthcare settings has demonstrated significant potential for improving diagnostic accuracy."

**✅ Spoken style:**
"AI in healthcare? It's already improving how doctors diagnose patients. And the results are impressive."

## Limitations

- **Extremely emotional content**: AI still can't match human actors for deeply emotional performances
- **Unusual pronunciations**: Some technical terms or names need manual adjustment
- **Real-time use**: Not designed for live applications
- **Voice distinctiveness**: Popular voices may appear in others' content

## Best Practices

### Do:
- Preview voices with your actual script before committing
- Use the pronunciation editor for technical terms
- Match voice characteristics to your audience
- Keep scripts concise and conversational

### Don't:
- Use AI voices to impersonate real people
- Expect perfect first-take rendering
- Ignore emotional context of your content
- Skip the fine-tuning step
