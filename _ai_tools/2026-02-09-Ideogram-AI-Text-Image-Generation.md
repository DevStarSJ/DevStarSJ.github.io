## What is Ideogram?

Ideogram is an **AI image generator** that excels at one thing other tools struggle with: **rendering text correctly in images**. While Midjourney and DALL-E often produce garbled letters, Ideogram consistently generates readable, stylized text as part of the image.

### Why Ideogram Stands Out

- **Accurate Text**: Finally, AI-generated signs, logos, and typography that work
- **Photorealistic & Stylized**: From photos to illustrations
- **Fast Generation**: Results in seconds
- **Free Tier Available**: Try before you subscribe
- **Active Development**: Frequent model updates

## The Text Problem Solved

Traditional AI image generators fail at text because they don't truly understand letters - they treat text as shapes. Ideogram was specifically trained to handle typography:

### Before Ideogram (Other AIs)
```
Prompt: "A coffee shop sign that says 'Morning Brew'"
Result: "Mronig Bwer" or completely illegible
```

### With Ideogram
```
Prompt: "A coffee shop sign that says 'Morning Brew'"
Result: Clear, stylized "Morning Brew" sign
```

1. Type your prompt in the text box

4. Choose style (or leave as Auto)
5. Click "Generate"

### Pro Tips for Text

When you want text in your image:

```
Good: A vintage poster with the text "JAZZ NIGHT" in art deco style
Better: A 1920s art deco poster, bold golden text reading "JAZZ NIGHT", 
        geometric patterns, deep blue background
```

Be specific about:
- **What the text says** (in quotes)
- **Typography style** (serif, script, bold, etc.)
- **Where text appears** (as a sign, on a t-shirt, etc.)
- **Overall aesthetic** (vintage, modern, playful)

## Advanced Techniques

### 1. Combining Styles

Mix style keywords for unique results:

```
Prompt: "Cyberpunk meets art nouveau poster, neon lights, 
organic flowing patterns, text 'NEW EDEN'"
```

### 2. Reference Specific Artists/Eras

```
Prompt: "Swiss design style poster, Helvetica typography, 
geometric shapes, text 'DESIGN CONFERENCE 2026'"
```

### 3. Material Specifications

Be specific about how text should appear:

```
Prompt: "Storefront window with gold leaf lettering spelling 
'ANTIQUES', vintage shop, warm interior lighting"
```

### 4. Negative Prompting

Use "avoid" or describe what you don't want:

```
Prompt: "Clean minimalist logo, text 'ZEN', avoid: cluttered, 
busy, multiple colors, gradients"
```

## Limitations

- **Not perfect**: Text still occasionally has issues
- **Specific fonts**: You can't specify exact fonts, only styles
- **Complex layouts**: Multi-paragraph text doesn't work well
- **Editing**: No inpainting or selective editing yet

## Best Practices

1. **Keep text short**: 1-5 words work best
2. **Use quotes**: Always put your text in "quotes"
3. **Describe the context**: Where does the text appear?
4. **Iterate**: Generate multiple times, pick the best
5. **Check spelling**: AI will copy your typos
