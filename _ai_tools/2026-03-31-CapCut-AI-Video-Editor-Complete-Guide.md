## Getting Started: Your First AI Video

### Step 1: Open CapCut Web or Desktop
Go to [capcut.com](https://capcut.com) or open the desktop app.

### Step 2: Try AI Script to Video
1. Click **"AI tools" → "Script to Video"**
2. Paste your script or let AI generate one from a topic
3. Select video style (documentary, social, corporate, etc.)
4. Choose avatar or stock footage mode
5. Click Generate — wait 60-90 seconds

### Step 3: Customize
- Adjust timing of clips
- Replace any stock footage you don't like
- Change the voiceover voice or re-record manually
- Add your brand colors and logo

### Step 4: Export
- Export in up to 4K resolution
- Platform-specific presets (TikTok 9:16, YouTube 16:9, etc.)
- Direct upload to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube
