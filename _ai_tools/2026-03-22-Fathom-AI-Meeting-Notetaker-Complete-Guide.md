## What Is Fathom?

Fathom is a **meeting recording and AI summarization tool** that joins your video calls as a "bot participant" (or via screen recording) and:

1. **Records** the entire meeting in HD
2. **Transcribes** speech to text in real time with speaker identification
3. **Summarizes** the call into action items, key decisions, and topic summaries
4. **Integrates** with your CRM, Slack, Notion, and more

The free plan includes unlimited recordings and summaries — a genuinely generous offer that has made Fathom the default choice for millions of professionals.

## Key Features

### 🎙️ Real-Time Transcription
Fathom transcribes your meeting live as it happens. You can see the running transcript during the call, which is useful for catching something you missed without asking the speaker to repeat themselves.

**Accuracy:** Fathom uses best-in-class speech recognition with ~95% accuracy for clear audio. It correctly identifies multiple speakers and labels them by name after brief calibration.

### 🤖 AI Meeting Summaries
When a call ends, Fathom automatically generates:
- **Key moments:** Timestamped highlights of the most important discussion points
- **Action items:** Specific tasks mentioned, with the person responsible
- **Meeting summary:** A 3-5 paragraph overview of what was discussed
- **Follow-up email draft:** Ready-to-send email based on what was decided

The summaries use GPT-4-class models and are genuinely useful — not just keyword dumps.

### 🔖 Highlight Clipping
During live calls, you can press a hotkey to **bookmark moments** in real time. After the meeting, these bookmarks are available as clickable clips you can share with teammates who weren't on the call.

### 🔗 CRM Integration
Fathom integrates with:
- **Salesforce** — Auto-log call notes and action items to contact records
- **HubSpot** — Sync meeting summaries to deals and contacts
- **Notion** — Push meeting notes to your workspace
- **Slack** — Post summaries to channels or DMs automatically
- **Google Docs / Drive** — Export formatted notes

### 📱 Multi-Platform Support
- **Zoom** — Deepest integration, native Zoom app available
- **Google Meet** — Full support
- **Microsoft Teams** — Full support
- **Webex** — Supported
- **Any platform** — Screen recording mode as a fallback

## Getting Started with Fathom

### Step 1: Sign Up
Visit [fathom.video](https://fathom.video) and create a free account (Google sign-in supported).

### Step 2: Install the Extension
Fathom works via a **browser extension** (Chrome) or **desktop app** (Mac/Windows):
- Chrome extension: Adds a Fathom panel to Zoom Web, Google Meet, Teams
- Desktop app: Recommended for the best recording quality and Zoom integration

### Step 3: Grant Permissions
Allow Fathom to access:
- Your microphone and system audio
- Browser tabs (for web-based meetings)
- Calendar (optional, for automatic join)

### Step 4: Enable Auto-Join (Optional)
Connect your Google or Outlook calendar. Fathom will automatically start recording all meetings that appear in your calendar — no manual activation needed.

### Step 5: Your First Meeting
When you join a meeting, you'll see a small Fathom overlay:
- Click **Record** to start (or it auto-starts if configured)
- During the call, the transcript appears live in the sidebar
- After the call, summaries are emailed to you and available in your Fathom dashboard

## Advanced Tips

### Custom Summary Templates
In Fathom's settings, you can create **custom summary templates** for different meeting types:
- Sales calls → "Prospect pain points, next steps, close date"
- 1:1s → "Progress since last meeting, blockers, priorities"
- Sprint reviews → "Completed items, bugs found, velocity"

### Smart Chapters
After a meeting, Fathom automatically divides the recording into **topic-based chapters**. Click any chapter to jump directly to that moment in the video. This is invaluable for long meetings.

### Share Clips with Non-Users
You can generate a **shareable link** to any clip or the full recording. Recipients don't need a Fathom account to view — just a link. Perfect for sharing with clients or stakeholders.

### Calendar Color Coding
In Fathom's calendar view, you can see all upcoming meetings and tag them (internal, client, interview) so the AI generates appropriately styled summaries.

### Keyboard Shortcuts
During meetings:
- **Ctrl+Shift+H** (Mac: **Cmd+Shift+H**): Highlight the current moment
- **Ctrl+Shift+N**: Add a note
- **Ctrl+Shift+A**: Mark action item
