Chatsonic will search the web, gather current data, and produce a cited, up-to-date article.

1. **Enter your topic or keyword**
2. **AI generates a structured outline** (you can edit)
3. **AI writes each section** with web-researched data
4. **Built-in SEO suggestions** — keyword density, meta description, readability

The result: a 1,500-3,000 word, SEO-optimized article in minutes.

## Pro Tips

**1. Always Edit Before Publishing**
AI content is a starting point, not a finished product. Add statistics, personal examples, and human perspective.

**2. Use Chatsonic for Research**
Before writing an article, use Chatsonic to research the topic, gather stats, and understand the competitive landscape.

**3. Set Up Templates**
Create custom templates for your most common content types. This ensures consistency and saves time.

**4. Factcheck Everything**
Even with web search enabled, AI can hallucinate details. Verify all statistics, dates, and claims before publishing.

**5. Train Brand Voice Properly**
Paste 5-10 of your best-performing articles into Brand Voice training. The more examples, the more accurate the style matching.
