## What is Zapier?

**Zapier** is a no-code automation platform that connects apps and automates repetitive tasks. The basic idea:

**Trigger → Action(s)**

Examples:
- When a new email arrives → Save attachment to Google Drive
- When a form is submitted → Add row to spreadsheet + Send Slack notification
- When a new customer signs up → Add to CRM + Send welcome email

Zapier connects over **7,000 apps** including Gmail, Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion, Airtable, Shopify, and thousands more.

## What's New: Zapier AI Features

Zapier has embedded AI throughout the platform:

### 1. AI-Powered Zap Builder
Describe what you want and Zapier builds it:
- "When I get an email with an invoice, extract the amount and vendor name, then add it to my Google Sheet"
- Zapier interprets the intent, selects the right apps, and suggests the complete workflow

### 2. Zapier Agents (Canvas AI Agents)
Build AI agents that run autonomously on schedules or triggers:
- An agent that monitors your email, categorizes messages, and drafts responses
- An agent that scrapes competitor pricing weekly and updates a Google Sheet
- An agent that monitors social media mentions and flags ones needing responses

### 3. AI Actions in Workflows
Add AI steps directly to your Zaps:
- **AI Text Generation** — Generate summaries, emails, or content as part of a workflow
- **AI Classification** — Categorize or label data automatically
- **AI Extraction** — Pull structured data from unstructured text (emails, forms, etc.)
- **AI Translation** — Translate content in-flight

### 4. Formatter AI
Zapier Formatter with AI can:
- Transform text formats intelligently ("Convert this casual email to formal business language")
- Extract specific data from messy text
- Standardize data formats across your workflow

## Getting Started with Zapier

### Step 1: Create Your Account
Go to [zapier.com](https://zapier.com) and sign up for free. The free tier lets you build 5 Zaps with 100 tasks/month — perfect for testing.

### Step 2: Connect Your Apps
Click **My Apps** → **Connect a new account** and authenticate your first apps (start with Gmail, Slack, or whatever you use daily).

### Step 3: Create Your First Zap
Click **Create Zap** → Describe what you want in the AI builder, or build manually by selecting:
1. **Trigger app** (e.g., Gmail)
2. **Trigger event** (e.g., "New email matching search")
3. **Action app** (e.g., Slack)
4. **Action** (e.g., "Send message")

### Step 4: Test and Turn On
Test each step with real data, verify the output looks right, then toggle the Zap on.

## Zapier Agents — The Next Level

**Zapier Agents** (Canvas-powered) are autonomous AI agents you configure once, and they run continuously. Unlike traditional Zaps that are trigger→action sequences, Agents can:

- Make decisions based on context
- Use tools (web search, app actions) to gather information
- Loop and retry intelligently
- Handle complex multi-step reasoning

**Example Agent: Competitive Intelligence Monitor**
- Runs every Monday morning
- Checks competitor websites for pricing changes
- Reads their latest blog posts and extracts key announcements
- Summarizes findings and emails you a competitive intelligence report

This would previously require a developer, a script, and significant maintenance. With Zapier Agents, it's a form fill.

## Zapier AI Tips

**Start with the AI builder:** Even if you know what you want to build, try describing it in natural language first. The AI often suggests apps or steps you didn't think of.

**Use AI steps for data transformation:** Rather than wrestling with Zapier Formatter syntax, use AI steps to transform data: "Convert this date from MM/DD/YYYY to YYYY-MM-DD format."

**Build incrementally:** Start with a 2-step Zap and add complexity. Don't build a 10-step Zap and wonder why it fails.

**Use Zapier Tables as your data layer:** Zapier Tables is a built-in database. Use it as an intermediate storage layer for complex workflows.

**Monitor your task usage:** Each action step in a Zap uses a task. Watch your monthly usage — complex Zaps can consume tasks quickly.

## Limitations

**Cost at scale:** Zapier's task-based pricing can get expensive as automation usage grows. Enterprise pricing helps but isn't cheap.

**No real-time data:** Most Zapier Zaps check for triggers on a polling interval (every 5-15 minutes on lower plans), not instantly.

**Complex logic can be cumbersome:** For very complex conditional workflows, Zapier can feel limiting compared to code or Make's visual builder.

**AI steps use extra tasks:** Each AI step in a workflow consumes additional tasks.
