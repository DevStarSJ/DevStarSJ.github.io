## What is Runway Gen-4?

Runway Gen-4 is the fourth generation of Runway's flagship video generation model. Released in 2025, it represents a major leap in video quality, consistency, and control. Gen-4 can generate up to 10-second video clips at up to 4K resolution, with dramatically improved motion consistency, character consistency, and physical accuracy compared to earlier models.

Runway is unique in that it's not just an AI model — it's a full creative suite with video editing, color grading, audio tools, and collaboration features built around its AI models.

## Key Features of Gen-4

### Text-to-Video
Describe a scene in text, and Gen-4 will generate a video clip. The quality of Gen-4's text-to-video is among the best available, with realistic motion physics, natural lighting, and coherent scene composition.

**Example prompts:**
- "A lone astronaut walks across a red desert at sunrise, slow motion, cinematic"
- "Rain falls on a neon-lit Tokyo street at night, bokeh lights, 4K"

### Image-to-Video
Upload a still image and Gen-4 animates it, maintaining the visual style, composition, and subject of the original while adding believable motion.

### Video-to-Video
Transform existing video footage using style transfer, motion modification, or subject replacement. This is powerful for artistic transformation and style matching.

### Character & Scene Consistency
Gen-4's biggest technical breakthrough is maintaining character consistency across multiple clips. Earlier models would generate slightly different-looking characters in each clip; Gen-4 can maintain a consistent protagonist across an entire short film.

### Camera Controls
Gen-4 offers unprecedented camera control:
- Pan, tilt, zoom, and dolly movements
- First-person and third-person perspectives
- Tracking shots that follow subjects
- Specific focal lengths and depth-of-field effects

### Act-One: Character Animation
Act-One is Runway's character animation feature that lets you drive AI-generated characters using reference video of a real performer. This bridges AI generation and motion capture in a way that's accessible to creators without expensive equipment.

## Tips for Better Results

1. **Be specific about camera movement** — "slow dolly shot moving left" produces far better results than "camera moves."
2. **Reference real cinematography terms** — "shallow depth of field," "dutch angle," "rack focus" all work well.
3. **Start with image-to-video for consistency** — generate or find a matching still first, then animate it.
4. **Use negative prompts** — specify what you *don't* want ("no camera shake, no blur").
5. **Upscale after generation** — generate at lower resolution and use Runway's upscaler or a third-party tool.
6. **Chain clips together** — generate multiple 5-10 second clips and assemble in Runway's editor.
