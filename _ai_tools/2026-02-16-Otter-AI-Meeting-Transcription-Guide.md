## What is Otter.ai?

Otter.ai is an AI-powered transcription service that converts speech to text in real-time. Whether you're in a Zoom meeting, conducting an interview, or attending a lecture, Otter captures every word and generates intelligent summaries.

**Key Features:**
- Real-time transcription with 95%+ accuracy
- Automatic speaker identification
- AI-generated meeting summaries
- Integration with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams
- Searchable transcript archive
- Collaborative editing and comments

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Visit [otter.ai](https://otter.ai)
2. Sign up with Google, Microsoft, or email
3. Download the mobile app (iOS/Android)
4. Install the Chrome extension for web meetings

### Step 2: Configure Integrations

Connect Otter to your calendar:

```
Settings → Integrations → Calendar
- Google Calendar ✓
- Microsoft Outlook ✓
```

Connect to meeting platforms:
```
Settings → Integrations → Meeting Apps
- Zoom (requires admin approval for auto-join)
- Google Meet
- Microsoft Teams
```

### Step 3: Set OtterPilot Preferences

```
Settings → OtterPilot
- Auto-join meetings: ON
- Send summary emails: ON
- Generate action items: ON
- Share with participants: Custom
```

During or after meetings:

## Best Practices

### For Remote Meetings

1. **Use good audio equipment** - Better input = better transcription
2. **Enable original audio** in Zoom for clearer capture
3. **Mute when not speaking** to reduce noise
4. **Speak clearly** and avoid talking over others

### For In-Person Meetings

1. **Place device centrally** for equal speaker pickup
2. **Use the mobile app** for best portability
3. **Start recording** before the meeting begins
4. **Name participants** for easier identification

### For Interviews

1. **Test audio levels** beforehand
2. **Create separate workspaces** for different projects
3. **Use timestamps** when reviewing
4. **Export to your preferred format**

## Common Issues and Solutions

### Poor Transcription Quality

**Solution:**
- Check microphone settings
- Reduce background noise
- Update custom vocabulary
- Speak at moderate pace

### OtterPilot Not Joining

**Solution:**
- Verify calendar integration
- Check meeting platform permissions
- Ensure meeting is scheduled (not ad-hoc)
- Review firewall settings

### Speaker Misidentification

**Solution:**
- Correct speakers manually
- Train voice profiles
- Ensure distinct audio per speaker
- Use dedicated microphones
