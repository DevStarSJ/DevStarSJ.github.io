## What Is Wordtune?

**Wordtune** is an AI-powered writing assistant developed by AI21 Labs. Unlike basic grammar tools, Wordtune understands the *meaning* of your writing and offers rewrites that preserve your intent while improving clarity, flow, and style.

It integrates seamlessly into your browser (Chrome, Edge), Google Docs, Microsoft Word, and has a standalone web editor.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Smart Rewrite
Select any sentence and Wordtune offers multiple rewrite options instantly. Each suggestion preserves your original meaning while offering a fresh angle.

**Before:** "The meeting went okay but there were some issues that came up unexpectedly."  
**After:** "The meeting proceeded reasonably well, though a few unexpected issues surfaced."

### 2. Tone Adjustment
Switch between **Casual** and **Formal** tones with one click. Wordtune rewrites your sentence to match:
- 📝 **Casual:** "Hey, just checking if you got my email"
- 🎩 **Formal:** "I am writing to follow up on my previous correspondence"

### 3. Expand & Shorten
- **Expand:** Turn a brief note into a full paragraph
- **Shorten:** Compress verbose writing into crisp, punchy sentences

### 4. Continue Writing
Stuck mid-sentence? Wordtune's AI can continue your thought, helping you push past writer's block.

### 5. Spices (Contextual Examples & Stats)
Wordtune can inject relevant **examples, analogies, counterarguments, and statistics** into your writing automatically — a game-changer for content creators and academics.

### 6. Summarize
Paste long articles or PDFs and Wordtune generates bullet-point summaries in seconds.

## Pro Tips

**1. Use Multiple Suggestions**
Don't just take the first rewrite. Wordtune gives 3-7 options — read them all and combine the best parts.

**2. Rewrite in Chunks**
Work sentence by sentence. Trying to rewrite entire paragraphs at once gives less precise results.

**3. Pair with Grammarly**
Wordtune for better phrasing + Grammarly for grammar errors = unbeatable combination.

**4. Use Spices Sparingly**
Spices (auto-inserted examples/stats) are powerful but should be verified for accuracy before publishing.

**5. The Shortening Feature is Underrated**
Paste a 500-word email draft and use "Shorten" repeatedly until it's tight. You'll be amazed.
