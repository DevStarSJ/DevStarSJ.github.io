## What is Ideogram AI?

**Ideogram AI** is an AI image generation tool that has solved one of the biggest challenges in the field: rendering text accurately within images. While other AI art generators like Midjourney and DALL-E struggle with text, Ideogram excels at creating images with readable, stylized typography.

Founded by former Google Brain researchers, Ideogram launched in 2023 and quickly became the go-to tool for designers needing text-heavy visuals like logos, posters, social media graphics, and more.

## How to Use Ideogram

### Step 1: Sign Up

Visit [ideogram.ai](https://ideogram.ai) and create a free account using Google.

### Step 2: Write Your Prompt

Be specific about the text you want and its style:

```
A vintage coffee shop logo with the text "MORNING BREW" 
in elegant script font, warm brown tones, rustic wooden 
background, professional design
```

### Step 3: Choose Settings

- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1, 16:9, 9:16, etc.
- **Style**: Photo, 3D, Illustration, etc.
- **Magic Prompt**: AI enhancement (on/off)

### Step 4: Generate

Click generate and receive 4 variations. Select your favorite or regenerate.

### Step 5: Download

Download in high resolution for your projects.

## Limitations

- Complex multi-line text can still have errors
- Very small text may be illegible
- Non-Latin scripts (Chinese, Arabic, etc.) are less reliable
- Some artistic styles may distort text slightly

## Pro Tips

1. **Keep text short**: Shorter text = better accuracy
2. **Specify font style**: "bold", "script", "sans-serif"
3. **Use quotation marks**: Put exact text in quotes
4. **Describe placement**: "text at top", "centered text"
5. **Generate multiple**: Pick best from variations
