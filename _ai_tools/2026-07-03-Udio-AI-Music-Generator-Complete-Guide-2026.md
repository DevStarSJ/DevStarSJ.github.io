## What is Udio AI?

Udio is an **AI music generation platform** that creates complete, production-ready songs from text prompts. Unlike loop-based tools, Udio generates fully coherent music with vocals, instruments, and structure — indistinguishable from human-made music in many cases.

### What Udio Can Create

- **Full songs with vocals** — Including lyrics written by the AI
- **Instrumental tracks** — Background music, soundscapes, cinematic scores
- **Genre-specific music** — Hip-hop, jazz, classical, electronic, rock, folk, and 100+ more
- **Custom length tracks** — From short jingles to full 4-minute songs
- **Stems and variations** — Generate alternative versions of any track
- **Audio extensions** — Extend existing tracks to any length

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Sign Up

Visit **[udio.com](https://udio.com)** and create an account. The free tier includes a generous credit allowance for new users.

### Step 2: Write Your First Prompt

The prompt is where the magic happens. A good Udio prompt includes:

1. **Genre/style** — Be specific (e.g., "indie folk" not just "folk")
2. **Mood/energy** — "melancholic", "upbeat", "cinematic", "aggressive"
3. **Instrumentation** — "acoustic guitar", "orchestral strings", "808 bass"
4. **Vocal style** — "female vocals", "male rap", "choir", "no vocals"
5. **Reference artists** — "in the style of The National" (use sparingly)

**Example prompts:**

```
Upbeat indie pop song, female vocals, catchy chorus, 
acoustic guitar and light drums, summer vibes, 
happy and energetic, radio-friendly
```

```
Dark cinematic orchestral score, no vocals, 
building tension, strings and brass, 
dramatic climax, suitable for a thriller film
```

```
Lo-fi hip hop beat, chill atmosphere, vinyl crackle, 
mellow piano, soft kick drum, study music vibes
```

### Step 3: Generate and Iterate

Hit generate and Udio creates 2 variations. Listen to both and:
- **Keep the best one** — Download or add to your library
- **Extend** — Make it longer by adding sections
- **Remix** — Generate variations with different energy or instrumentation
- **Edit** — Use the inpainting feature to change specific sections

## Core Features Deep Dive

### Custom Lyrics

Tell Udio exactly what to sing:

```
[Prompt]: Indie rock song, male vocals
[Lyrics]:
(Verse 1)
Walking through the city lights at night
Every shadow feels like it's alive
I've been searching for a piece of mind
Left the past where the sun don't shine

(Chorus)
We're not lost, we're just finding our way
Through the noise and the beautiful day
Hold on tight, the storm's gonna fade
We're not lost, we're just unafraid
```

Udio integrates your lyrics naturally into the generated music.

### Audio Inpainting

One of Udio's most powerful features — **selectively edit sections** of a generated track:

1. Upload or generate a track
2. Select a specific time range (e.g., the 0:45–1:15 section)
3. Describe what you want to change: "make this section more intense, add electric guitar"
4. Udio regenerates only that section while keeping the rest intact

This is invaluable for fixing one weak section in an otherwise great track.

### Extend Any Track

Start with a 30-second clip and build it into a full song:

1. Generate an intro (15-30 seconds)
2. Use "Extend" to add a verse section
3. Extend again for the chorus
4. Add a bridge, second chorus, and outro
5. Result: a full 3-4 minute professional-quality track

## Advanced Techniques

### Technique 1: The Reference Stack Method

Build prompts using multiple specific references:

```
Electronic ambient music combining:
- The atmosphere of Brian Eno's Music for Airports
- The bass frequencies of Nicolas Jaar  
- The granular textures of Stars of the Lid
Slow-building, 90 BPM, no percussion in first minute
```

### Technique 2: Negative Prompting

Tell Udio what to avoid:

```
Upbeat jazz, piano and double bass, warm tones,
café atmosphere — avoid: drums, modern production, 
reverb-heavy mixing, electric instruments
```

### Technique 3: Section-by-Section Building

For maximum control, build your track piece by piece:

```
Prompt 1: "Cinematic intro, strings only, quiet and mysterious, 
building slowly, no drums, 30 seconds"

[Extend with:]
Prompt 2: "Add drums and brass, increasing intensity, 
matching the established string motif"

[Extend with:]
Prompt 3: "Full orchestral climax, all instruments, 
triumphant resolution, same key and tempo"
```

### Technique 4: Genre Fusion

Combine unexpected genres for unique results:

```
Folk metal ballad: acoustic fingerpicking combined with 
distorted electric guitar, operatic female vocals,
celtic flute melody, heavy drum kit
```

## Tips for Better Results

1. **Describe texture, not just genre** — "warm analog synths" > "electronic music"
2. **Include BPM when it matters** — "120 BPM dance music" gives more consistent results
3. **Use musical terms** — "polyphonic", "chord progressions", "pentatonic scale" work
4. **Generate 2x and pick** — Always get both variations before extending
5. **Save prompt templates** — Keep a notes file of prompts that work well for your use case
6. **Extend in short chunks** — 30-second extensions are more consistent than 2-minute ones
