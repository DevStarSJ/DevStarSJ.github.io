## Tips for Best Results

1. **Write natural scripts** — conversational language sounds better than formal prose
2. **Use punctuation for pacing** — commas and periods control avatar cadence
3. **Choose avatars by style** — some look professional (suits), others casual
4. **Test voice speed** — 0.9x–1.1x speed often sounds most natural
5. **Use branded backgrounds** — upload your company slides or office images
6. **Keep videos under 3 minutes** — engagement drops significantly after that

## Limitations to Know

- Custom avatar requires "consent video" and 24–48h processing
- Free tier is very limited (1 minute total)
- Very fast speech or heavy accents may produce imperfect lip-sync
- Real-time avatar (Interactive) is still in beta — not production-ready for all use cases
- Videos don't include natural blinking/micro-expressions on all avatars
