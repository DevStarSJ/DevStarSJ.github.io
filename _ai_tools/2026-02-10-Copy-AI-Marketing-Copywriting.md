## Tips for Better Results

### 1. Provide Context

Bad prompt:
> "Write ad copy for shoes"

Good prompt:
> "Write Facebook ad copy for sustainable running shoes targeting eco-conscious millennials. Highlight the recycled materials and carbon-neutral shipping. Tone: inspiring but not preachy."

### 2. Use the "Improve" Feature

After generation, use Copy.ai's improve button to:
- Make it shorter
- Add urgency
- Include specific benefits
- Change the emotional angle

### 3. Generate Multiple Variations

Never settle for the first output. Generate 5-10 variations and pick the best elements from each.

## Limitations to Know

1. **Still needs human editing** - AI gets you 80% there
2. **Fact-checking required** - It can generate plausible-sounding but incorrect claims
3. **Generic without context** - Brand voice training is essential
4. **Not great for technical content** - Stick to marketing copy
