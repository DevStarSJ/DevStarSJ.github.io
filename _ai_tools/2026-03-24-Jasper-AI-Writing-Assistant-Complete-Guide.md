## What Is Jasper AI?

Jasper AI (formerly Jarvis) is an **AI-powered writing assistant** built specifically for marketing and business content. Unlike general-purpose chatbots, Jasper is purpose-built for:

- Marketing copy (ads, emails, landing pages)
- Long-form content (blog posts, white papers, ebooks)
- Brand-consistent content across channels
- Team collaboration on content workflows

Jasper runs on a combination of GPT-4, Claude, and proprietary models tuned specifically for marketing copy quality.

## Core Features

### 🎯 Jasper Brand Voice
Train Jasper on your brand's tone, style, and voice guidelines. Upload your existing content, style guide, or simply describe your brand personality. Every piece Jasper generates stays on-brand — even across a team of 50 writers.

### 📄 Documents
The primary workspace for long-form content. Start with an outline or let Jasper build one, then expand each section. The AI can maintain context throughout a 5,000-word article, keeping tone and messaging consistent.

### 🚀 Campaigns
Create entire marketing campaigns from a single brief:
- Blog post + social captions + email newsletter + ad copy
- All from the same source content
- Consistent messaging across every channel

This is where Jasper's ROI really shines.

### 🧩 Templates (50+ Ready-Made)
Jump-start your content with proven templates:
- AIDA Framework (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)
- PAS Framework (Problem, Agitate, Solution)
- Product descriptions
- Google/Facebook ad copy
- Email subject lines
- YouTube video scripts

### 🌐 Jasper Chat
A conversational interface for brainstorming, asking questions, and iterating quickly. Like ChatGPT, but with your brand context and content history baked in.

### 🔍 SEO Mode (Surfer Integration)
Jasper integrates natively with **Surfer SEO**, analyzing top-ranking content and suggesting keywords to include. Write SEO-optimized articles without switching tools.

## Jasper for Different Use Cases

### Content Marketing Teams
Jasper excels at scaling blog production. A team of 3 content writers can produce the output of 10 with Jasper handling first drafts, headline variations, and meta descriptions.

**Workflow:**
1. Brief the article (topic, audience, keywords)
2. Jasper generates outline + intro
3. Human writer refines and expands
4. Jasper fills in supporting sections
5. Human edits for accuracy and voice

### Social Media Managers
Generate 30 days of social content in an afternoon. Jasper understands platform-specific tone (LinkedIn vs. Instagram vs. Twitter) and adapts automatically.

### Email Marketers
From subject lines to full email sequences, Jasper can generate entire nurture campaigns. It understands email marketing frameworks and conversion optimization principles.

### Ad Copywriters
A/B test more copy variants than humanly possible. Generate 20 headline variations in minutes and let data pick the winner.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Set Up Your Brand Voice
Before writing anything, invest 15 minutes in brand voice setup:
1. Go to Settings → Brand Voice
2. Paste sample content that represents your ideal tone
3. Add specific instructions (e.g., "Never use exclamation points", "Always use second-person 'you'")
4. Save and apply to all future content

This single step will 10x the quality of everything Jasper generates for you.

### Step 2: Try a Template First
Don't start with a blank Document. Pick the template closest to your use case, fill in the inputs, and see what Jasper produces. This teaches you what good inputs look like.

### Step 3: Use Campaigns for Scale
Once comfortable with individual pieces, use Campaigns to multiply your output. One campaign brief → full channel suite.

## Limitations to Know

- **Fact accuracy**: Jasper can hallucinate facts and statistics — always fact-check
- **Not a replacement for strategy**: Jasper writes well, but doesn't have business context or strategic judgment
- **Best for English**: Quality drops significantly in non-English languages
- **Needs human editing**: Raw Jasper output sounds AI-ish; human polish is essential for premium content
