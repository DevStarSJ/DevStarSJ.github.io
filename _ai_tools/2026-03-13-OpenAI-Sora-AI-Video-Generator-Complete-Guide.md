## What Is OpenAI Sora?

Sora is OpenAI's **text-to-video AI model** capable of generating videos up to 60 seconds long from natural language descriptions. It understands physical world dynamics — light, motion, depth, and causality — producing videos that look and feel cinematic.

**Core capabilities:**
- **Text-to-video:** Describe a scene, get a video
- **Image-to-video:** Animate a still image
- **Video extension:** Extend existing clips forward or backward in time
- **Video remixing:** Transform footage with new styles or elements
- **Storyboard:** Generate multiple video clips from a script

## Key Features

### 1. Cinematic Quality
Sora generates **1080p videos** with proper depth of field, lighting continuity, and realistic motion. Characters maintain consistent appearance across frames — something earlier AI video tools struggled with.

### 2. Long-Form Coherence
Unlike many competitors limited to 4–6 second clips, Sora produces **up to 60-second videos** with logical scene continuity. A character walking through a door in one shot will appear in the next room in the following shot.

### 3. Multi-Shot Generation
Describe a complete narrative, and Sora can generate **multiple shots** in sequence — like a mini film with cuts and angles.

### 4. Style Control
From photorealistic to anime, watercolor to film noir — Sora adapts to virtually any visual style specified in your prompt.

### 5. Storyboard Mode
Provide a full script or storyboard and Sora generates video for each scene, maintaining consistent characters and settings throughout.

## How to Use Sora

### Step 1: Access Sora
Go to **sora.com** or open ChatGPT and navigate to the Sora tab (Plus/Pro required).

### Step 2: Write Your Prompt
Be descriptive. Include:
- **Subject:** Who or what is in the video
- **Action:** What is happening
- **Setting:** Where and when
- **Style:** Cinematic, animated, documentary, etc.
- **Camera:** Angle, movement, lens type

**Example prompt:**
> *"A lone astronaut walks across a red Martian landscape at sunset, wide-angle shot, cinematic, 4K quality, dust particles floating in the air"*

### Step 3: Choose Settings
- **Duration:** 5s, 10s, 20s, or 60s
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9, 9:16 (vertical), 1:1 (square)
- **Resolution:** 720p or 1080p

### Step 4: Generate & Refine
Click generate. Review the result. Use the **remix** feature to modify elements while keeping the overall scene structure.

### Step 5: Export
Download your video as MP4. Use in social media, presentations, websites, or as part of a larger production.

## Prompt Engineering Tips for Sora

### Be Specific About Motion
❌ "A bird flying"
✅ "A red-tailed hawk soaring in slow motion over a misty mountain valley, wings fully extended, golden hour lighting"

### Use Cinematic Language
Terms like *"dolly shot," "handheld," "tracking shot," "aerial view"* significantly improve output quality.

### Describe Lighting Explicitly
*"Soft morning light," "neon-lit rain-soaked street," "harsh desert midday sun"* — lighting dramatically affects mood and realism.

### Keep Physics in Mind
Sora understands physics, so prompts that violate natural laws may produce inconsistent results. Work with physics, not against it.

### Iterate and Remix
Your first generation is a starting point. Use **remix mode** to adjust colors, add elements, or change the style while keeping the structure you like.

## Limitations to Know

- **No audio generation** — video only, no music or speech (yet)
- **Content policy:** Cannot generate realistic violence, explicit content, or real celebrities' likenesses without consent
- **Complex actions:** Very intricate movements (like detailed hand gestures) can still be inconsistent
- **Generation time:** High-quality 60s videos can take several minutes to generate
- **Commercial rights:** Verify licensing terms for commercial use before publishing

---

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Study the Sora showcase** at sora.com/explore — the prompts used are often shown
2. **Start short:** Generate 5-10s clips first, then extend what works
3. **Use reference images** when available — image-to-video produces more predictable results
4. **Layer your edits:** Generate a base, remix with style changes, then extend
5. **Batch generate:** Create multiple variations and pick the best one
