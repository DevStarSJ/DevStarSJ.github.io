## What Is Leonardo AI?

Leonardo AI is an **AI-powered image generation platform** built on Stable Diffusion with a comprehensive set of additional features: fine-tuned models for specific art styles, image editing tools, ControlNet integration, and a training system for custom models.

Where Midjourney focuses on quality and aesthetics, Leonardo focuses on **control and versatility** — it's the tool of choice for game developers, 3D artists, and creators who need consistent character design, game assets, and concept art with precise control.

## Key Features

### 🎨 Multiple Specialized Models
Leonardo hosts dozens of fine-tuned models alongside its flagship:
- **Leonardo Kino XL** — cinematic, photorealistic
- **Leonardo Diffusion XL** — versatile, high quality
- **AlbedoBase XL** — game assets, concept art
- **DreamShaper** — painterly, fantasy styles
- Community-trained models for anime, architecture, product design

### 🖼️ Image-to-Image
Upload a reference image and generate variations that maintain the composition, style, or specific elements. Essential for iterating on character designs or maintaining visual consistency.

### 🎮 Canvas (Outpainting & Inpainting)
The AI Canvas lets you:
- **Outpaint** — extend images beyond their borders
- **Inpaint** — selectively edit regions of an image
- **Sketch-to-image** — draw rough shapes and let AI render them

### 🔧 ControlNet Integration
ControlNet lets you control image generation through pose maps, edge detection, depth maps, and more. Generate images that match a specific pose, structure, or composition — invaluable for consistent character art.

### 🏋️ Model Training
Train your own custom model on Leonardo using as few as 15 images. Creates a fine-tuned model that generates images in your specific style or of a specific character/product.

### ✨ PhotoReal & Alchemy
**PhotoReal** mode generates hyper-realistic photography-like images. **Alchemy** applies additional upscaling, coherence, and quality enhancement to any generation.

## How to Get Started

### Step 1: Sign Up
Go to [leonardo.ai](https://leonardo.ai) and create a free account. The free tier is surprisingly capable — 150 tokens/day is enough for 15-30 standard images.

### Step 2: Choose a Model
The model selection is key. Browse the **Model Library** and filter by style:
- For photorealism → **Leonardo Kino XL** or **PhotoReal mode**
- For game assets → **AlbedoBase XL**
- For fantasy/illustration → **DreamShaper** or **Leonardo Vision XL**

### Step 3: Craft Your Prompt
Leonardo responds well to structured prompts:
```
A warrior woman in ornate silver armor, fantasy RPG style, 
detailed textures, dramatic lighting, front-facing, 
white background, game character concept art
```

Add **negative prompts** to exclude unwanted elements: `blurry, deformed hands, bad anatomy, low quality`

### Step 4: Use Guidance Settings
- **Guidance Scale (CFG):** How strictly the model follows your prompt (7-12 is typical)
- **Steps:** More steps = more detail but slower (30-50 is a good range)
- **Aspect Ratio:** Match to your use case (1:1 for social, 16:9 for wallpapers)

### Step 5: Iterate
Generated 8 images? Select the best, use Image-to-Image to create variations, or bring it into Canvas for touch-ups.

## Leonardo for Game Development

Leonardo is particularly well-suited for game developers:

**Consistent Character Sheets**
Train a custom model on your character design → generate the character in different poses, emotions, and scenarios consistently.

**Tile & Asset Generation**
Use tiling modes to generate seamless textures. AlbedoBase XL was specifically designed for this use case.

**Concept Art Workflows**
Sketch roughly in Canvas, let AI refine. Use ControlNet to maintain exact poses/compositions across iterations.

**Item & UI Assets**
Generate icons, inventory items, weapons, and UI elements with consistent art direction using a fine-tuned model.

## Tips for Power Users

**Batch generate:** Set generations to 4 or 8 at a time rather than 1. Variation in a batch is often higher quality than repetitive single generations.

**Save your settings as presets:** Once you find a model + prompt style that works, save it for future consistency.

**Use negative prompts aggressively:** A solid negative prompt prevents common issues (blurry eyes, extra fingers, text artifacts) before they appear.

**PhotoReal + Raw mode:** For photography-style images, combine PhotoReal with Raw mode for unprocessed, natural-looking results.

**Model training:** If you need 50+ images of the same character or style, training a custom model pays off quickly.
