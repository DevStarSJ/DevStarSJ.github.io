## What is Suno AI?

**Suno AI** is an AI-powered music generation platform that creates complete songs — including vocals, melodies, harmonies, and instrumentation — from a simple text prompt. Unlike tools that just generate loops or beats, Suno produces full songs with verses, choruses, and bridges.

**Key stats:**
- Over 10 million users worldwide
- Generates songs in 30+ genres
- Supports multiple languages for lyrics
- Integrated with Microsoft Copilot and other platforms

## How Suno Works

Suno uses a combination of large language models (for lyrics) and audio diffusion models (for the actual sound generation) to produce music that sounds surprisingly professional.

The workflow is simple:

1. **Write a prompt** — describe the genre, mood, topic, and style
2. **Choose a mode** — let Suno write lyrics, or provide your own
3. **Generate** — two variations are created in ~30 seconds
4. **Iterate** — extend, remix, or regenerate sections you don't like

### Prompt Anatomy

A good Suno prompt has three parts:

```
[Style tags] + [Mood/Energy] + [Topic/Theme]

Example:
"upbeat pop rock, energetic, female vocals, song about chasing your dreams"

Example 2:
"lo-fi hip hop, chill, rainy day, studying late at night"

Example 3:
"80s synth pop, dramatic, heartbreak ballad, losing someone you love"
```

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Sign Up

Visit [suno.com](https://suno.com) and sign in with Google, Discord, or Microsoft. Free accounts get **50 credits per day** (enough for ~10 songs).

### Step 2: Create Your First Song

1. Click **"Create"** in the sidebar
2. In the **Song Description** box, type your prompt
3. Choose **Custom Mode** if you want to provide your own lyrics

## Advanced Features

### Custom Lyrics Mode

Want full control? Switch to Custom Mode and write your own lyrics with Suno's formatting tags:

```
[Verse 1]
Walking down the empty street
Neon lights beneath my feet

[Chorus]
I found my way back home
No longer feel alone

[Bridge]
The world is spinning fast
But this moment has to last
```

Tags Suno understands:
- `[Verse]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Bridge]`, `[Outro]`
- `[Intro]`, `[Pre-Chorus]`, `[Instrumental]`
- `[spoken word]`, `[whispered]`, `[falsetto]`

### Song Extension

If you love the first 30 seconds but want a full song, use the **Extend** feature:
1. Click the **"..."** menu on any generated song
2. Select **Extend**
3. Suno continues the song from where it left off

You can extend multiple times to create songs of any length.

### Cover Songs & Style Matching

While Suno won't reproduce copyrighted songs, you can match a **style**:

```
"In the style of 90s grunge, similar energy to Nirvana, distorted guitars, 
raspy male vocals, angst-ridden lyrics about modern life"
```

### Instrumental Tracks

Need background music without vocals? Just add "instrumental" to your prompt:

```
"cinematic orchestral, instrumental, epic battle scene, strings and brass, 
no vocals, Hans Zimmer style"
```

## Tips for Better Results

### Be Specific with Genre Tags
❌ `"pop song about love"`  
✅ `"indie pop, dreamy production, female vocals, reverb-heavy guitar, bittersweet love song, Taylor Swift vibes"`

### Use Emotion Words
Words like *melancholic*, *triumphant*, *anxious*, *euphoric*, *nostalgic* dramatically affect the feel of the output.

### Specify Tempo & Energy
- *slow burn*, *mid-tempo*, *uptempo*, *frenetic*
- *quiet and intimate*, *stadium anthem*, *club banger*

### Layer Multiple Styles
```
"blues-infused soul with jazz harmonies, smoky bar atmosphere, late night, 
minor key, female vocalist, piano and saxophone prominent"
```

- **Pro/Premier plans:** You own the songs and can use them commercially

For serious commercial use, always use a paid plan and keep records of your prompts.
