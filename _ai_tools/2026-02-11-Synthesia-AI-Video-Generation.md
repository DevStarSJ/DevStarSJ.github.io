## Best Practices

### Script Writing

**Do:**
```
"Welcome to our quarterly update. 
[PAUSE]
I'm excited to share three key achievements..."
```

**Don't:**
```
"Welcome to our Q3 2026 quarterly business 
update presentation video recording session..."
```

Keep it conversational, not corporate-speak.

### Avatar Selection

Match avatar to content:
- Technical content → Professional appearance
- Casual updates → Friendly presenter
- Global audience → Diverse representation

### Quality Tips

1. **Break into scenes** - Easier to edit
2. **Add b-roll** - Mix avatar with other visuals
3. **Include captions** - Accessibility and engagement
4. **Use pauses** - Natural pacing
5. **Preview first** - Catch issues early
