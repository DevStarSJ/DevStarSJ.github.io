## What is InVideo AI?

**InVideo AI** is a revolutionary video creation platform that transforms simple text prompts into fully edited, professional videos complete with stock footage, music, voiceovers, and subtitles. Unlike AI tools that generate synthetic video frames, InVideo AI intelligently assembles real stock footage, creating polished videos in minutes.

Perfect for marketers, content creators, and businesses, InVideo AI eliminates the need for video editing skills or expensive production teams.

## How InVideo AI Works

### The Magic of AI Video Assembly

1. **You describe** your video idea in plain English
2. **AI writes** a script based on your description
3. **AI selects** matching stock footage, music, and transitions
4. **AI generates** voiceover narration
5. **You receive** a complete, ready-to-publish video

```
Prompt: "Create a 60-second promotional video for a 
coffee shop called 'Morning Brew' highlighting 
our artisanal coffee and cozy atmosphere"
```

Result: A professionally edited video with:
- Relevant coffee/café footage
- Background music
- AI voiceover
- Animated text overlays
- Smooth transitions

### 2. Script Generation

InVideo AI writes scripts automatically, but you can:

- Edit generated scripts
- Provide your own script
- Adjust tone and style
- Modify length and pacing

### 3. AI Voiceover

Choose from multiple AI voices:

- Male/Female options
- Various accents
- Different speaking styles
- Multiple languages

### 4. Clone Your Voice

Record 30 seconds of your voice and InVideo AI creates a clone for consistent branding across all videos.

### 5. Intelligent Media Selection

The AI analyzes your script and automatically selects:

- Stock video clips
- Background music
- Sound effects
- Transitions

### 6. Editing Commands

After generation, refine with natural language:

```
"Make the intro shorter"
"Add more energy to the music"
"Change the voiceover to a female voice"
"Replace the third clip with something more modern"
```

## Tips for Best Results

### 1. Be Specific in Prompts

❌ "Make a video about cars"
✅ "Create a 90-second video comparing electric vs gas cars for first-time car buyers, with a balanced, informative tone"

### 2. Specify Your Audience

Include who the video is for:
- "for small business owners"
- "targeting Gen Z viewers"
- "for technical professionals"

### 3. Define the Platform

Mention where it will be posted:
- YouTube (longer, educational)
- TikTok (short, engaging)
- LinkedIn (professional)

### 4. Use Edit Commands Iteratively

Don't try to fix everything at once. Make small adjustments and regenerate.
