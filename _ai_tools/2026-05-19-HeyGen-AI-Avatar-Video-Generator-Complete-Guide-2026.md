## Key Features

### 1. Instant AI Avatar Videos
Select from 300+ diverse avatars (professional, casual, various ethnicities and ages), type your script, choose a voice, and generate. Videos typically render in 2-5 minutes for a 2-minute clip.

### 2. Custom Avatar (Digital Twin)
Record yourself speaking on camera for 2-5 minutes. HeyGen processes the footage to create your personal AI avatar — it replicates your appearance, facial expressions, and lip movements. Future videos just need a script.

### 3. Video Translation & Dubbing
Upload any video (yours or third-party with permission) and HeyGen will:
- Transcribe the original audio
- Translate the script into target languages
- Regenerate the lip movements to match the new audio
- Produce a dubbed video that looks native in 40+ languages

This is transformative for global content distribution.

### 4. Interactive AI Avatar
Build a real-time conversational avatar that:
- Answers questions via text or voice

- Embeds into websites as a customer support agent

### 5. Personalized Video at Scale
Connect HeyGen to a spreadsheet of names, companies, or custom fields — it generates thousands of unique personalized videos (e.g., *"Hi Sarah, I noticed you work at Acme Corp..."*) for outreach campaigns.

### 6. Slide & Screen Integration
Combine avatar video with presentation slides, screen recordings, or animated backgrounds to create polished explainer and tutorial videos.

## How to Create a HeyGen Video

### Basic Video in 5 Steps

1. **Sign in** at [heygen.com](https://heygen.com) and click **Create Video**
2. **Choose a template** or start from blank
3. **Select an Avatar** — browse by gender, style, or language
4. **Write your script** in the text panel (or paste existing copy)
5. **Choose a voice** (matches avatar or custom upload) → **Generate**

Your video appears in the dashboard within minutes.

### Custom Avatar Setup

1. Go to **Avatars → Create Custom Avatar**
2. Film yourself following the on-screen prompts (good lighting, neutral background, 2-5 min)
3. Upload the footage — processing takes ~24 hours
4. Your avatar appears in **My Avatars** — use it in any future video

### Tips for Best Results
- **Script format:** Write conversationally; avoid bullet points as they disrupt natural pacing
- **Pause tags:** Use `[pause 1s]` in scripts for natural breathing room
- **Lighting consistency:** For custom avatars, consistent studio-quality lighting improves realism
- **Segment long videos:** Break 10+ minute videos into 2-3 minute chunks for faster generation

## Limitations

- **Realism ceiling:** AI avatars are very good but not perfect — subtle uncanny valley effects remain in close-up shots
- **Script dependency:** Complex emotional delivery (humor, sarcasm) may not translate well
- **Processing time:** Custom avatar creation takes up to 24 hours; standard videos are fast
- **Consent requirements:** Using real people's likenesses requires explicit consent
- **Language accents:** Auto-translated audio may carry AI-generated accent artifacts
