## What Is NightCafe?

- Multiple AI models (Stable Diffusion XL, DALL-E 3, Coherent, Artistic)

It's designed for both beginners who want quick, beautiful results and power users who want fine-grained control over every parameter.

## Getting Started: Your First Creation

### Step 1: Sign Up
Visit [creator.nightcafe.studio](https://creator.nightcafe.studio) and create a free account.

### Step 2: Choose Your Mode
- **Text to Image** — Type a prompt, get an image
- **Style Transfer** — Upload a photo and apply an art style
- **Text + Image** — Use an input image as a starting point

### Step 3: Write Your Prompt

A good NightCafe prompt has three parts:
```
[Subject] + [Style/Medium] + [Quality Modifiers]
```

**Example:**
```
A lone lighthouse on a rocky cliff at sunset, 
dramatic oil painting, moody atmosphere, golden hour lighting, 
highly detailed, award-winning digital art, artstation trending
```

### Step 4: Set Parameters
- **Model:** Choose SDXL for realism, DALL-E 3 for accuracy, Coherent for style
- **Aspect ratio:** Portrait (3:4), Landscape (4:3), Square (1:1), Wide (16:9)
- **Steps:** 30-50 is a good balance; higher = more refined but slower
- **Negative prompts:** Add `blurry, ugly, duplicate, watermark, text` to avoid common issues

### Step 5: Generate & Iterate
Generate 4 variations, pick the best, then either upscale it or use it as a base for another generation.

## Prompt Engineering Tips for NightCafe

### Style Keywords That Work Well
- For realism: `photorealistic, 8K, RAW photo, DSLR, bokeh, sharp focus`
- For painting: `oil painting, by [artist name], thick brushstrokes, impasto`
- For fantasy: `concept art, artstation, by Greg Rutkowski, vivid colors`
- For anime: `anime style, by Studio Ghibli, cel shading, soft lighting`

### The Negative Prompt Power Move
Always add these to negative prompts for better results:
```
blurry, out of focus, low quality, ugly, distorted, watermark, 
signature, extra limbs, bad anatomy, duplicate
```

### Aspect Ratio Strategy
- **Portraits:** 2:3 or 3:4
- **Landscapes:** 16:9 or 4:3
- **Social media covers:** 1:1 (Instagram) or 3:1 (Twitter/X header)
- **Wallpapers:** 16:9

### Use the Community for Inspiration
Browse the **Public Feed** to find prompts that produced great results. Most creators share their full prompt — copy it, modify it, and learn what works.
