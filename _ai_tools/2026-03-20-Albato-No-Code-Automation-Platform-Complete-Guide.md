## What Is Albato?

**Albato** is a cloud-based workflow automation platform that lets you connect apps and automate repetitive tasks without writing code. Think Zapier or Make, but with more competitive pricing and a growing app ecosystem.

Founded in 2020, Albato has grown to support **800+ integrations** and serves businesses of all sizes looking to streamline their operations.

---

## Core Features

### 1. Visual Automation Builder
Create workflows (called "automations") with a drag-and-drop visual interface. Connect triggers from one app to actions in another:

**Example workflow:**
> New lead in Facebook Ads → Create contact in HubSpot → Send welcome email via Mailchimp → Post notification to Slack

### 2. 800+ App Integrations
Albato connects popular tools across categories:
- **CRM:** HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Bitrix24
- **Communication:** Slack, Telegram, WhatsApp Business, Gmail
- **E-commerce:** Shopify, WooCommerce, Stripe
- **Marketing:** Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, Facebook Ads
- **Project Management:** Trello, Asana, Notion, ClickUp
- **Storage:** Google Drive, Dropbox, Airtable
- **Forms:** Typeform, JotForm, Google Forms

### 3. Multi-Step Workflows
Build complex, multi-step automations with conditions, filters, and data transformations — not just simple A→B connections.

**Features:**
- **Conditions (If/Else):** Route data differently based on criteria
- **Delay:** Wait before executing next step
- **Data Formatter:** Transform, parse, or calculate data
- **Iterator:** Process list items one by one
- **Code Step:** Run JavaScript for custom logic

### 4. Real-Time & Scheduled Triggers
- **Webhooks:** Instant triggers from external apps
- **Polling:** Check apps on a schedule (every 5 min, hourly, daily)
- **Scheduled:** Run automations at specific times

### 5. Embedded Automation (White-Label)
Albato's standout feature: **embed Albato's automation capabilities into your own SaaS product**. Offer your users pre-built integrations without building your own backend. Similar to Zapier's Embedded product but more affordable.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Sign Up
Go to [albato.com](https://albato.com) and create a free account. The free tier gives you 5 automations and 100 tasks/month to explore.

### Step 2: Create Your First Automation
Click "Create Automation" → choose your trigger app → authenticate it → choose trigger event.

### Step 3: Add an Action
Click "+" to add an action → choose your target app → map the data fields from the trigger to the action.

### Step 4: Test & Activate
Click "Test Run" to verify it works, then toggle the automation to "Active."

## Albato for SaaS Builders (Embedded)

This is Albato's most interesting play. If you're building a SaaS product, you can **embed Albato's automation engine** into your product — giving your users the ability to connect your app with 800+ others without you building each integration yourself.

**How it works:**
1. Integrate Albato's SDK into your product
2. Create templates for common integrations (e.g., "Connect with Salesforce")
3. Your users see a white-labeled integration panel inside your app
4. They set up automations — you don't write a single integration

This normally costs six figures with Zapier's embedded product. Albato offers it at significantly lower cost.

## Pro Tips

**1. Start with Templates**
Albato has a template library. Browse it before building from scratch — your use case probably already exists.

**2. Use the Data Formatter**
Many automations need data transformation (date format changes, text splitting, number formatting). The built-in formatter handles most cases without custom code.

**3. Add Error Notifications**
In automation settings, enable email notifications for failed runs. You don't want critical workflows silently failing.

**4. Use Webhooks for Instant Triggers**
If your trigger app supports webhooks, always use them instead of polling. Faster and uses fewer operations.

**5. Test with Real Data**
Always test with actual data, not just the sample data. Edge cases often only appear with real payloads.
