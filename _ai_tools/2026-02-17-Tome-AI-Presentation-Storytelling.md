## What is Tome?

Tome is an AI-native presentation platform that generates complete, narrative-driven presentations from text prompts. Unlike traditional slide tools, Tome focuses on **storytelling**—helping you craft compelling narratives, not just bullet points.

## Why Tome is Different

Traditional presentation tools like PowerPoint and Google Slides require you to:
1. Outline your content manually
2. Design each slide individually
3. Find and place images
4. Ensure visual consistency

Tome flips this process. Describe what you want to communicate, and AI creates a complete narrative with:
- Logical story structure
- Relevant visuals
- Consistent design
- Engaging flow

## Pro Tips for Better Tomes

### 1. Write Detailed Prompts

**Basic:** "Presentation about AI"

**Better:** "Create a 10-slide presentation explaining how AI is transforming healthcare, targeting hospital administrators who are considering AI adoption. Include statistics, real-world examples, and implementation considerations."

### 2. Use the Outline Feature

Before generating, review and edit the AI-suggested outline. Better structure = better results.

### 3. Iterate Slide by Slide

Generate the full presentation first, then refine individual slides with AI assistance.

### 4. Leverage Document Upload

Have existing content? Upload it and let Tome reshape it rather than starting from scratch.

### 5. Customize the AI Images

DALL-E images can be regenerated. If an image doesn't fit, click to generate alternatives with modified prompts.
