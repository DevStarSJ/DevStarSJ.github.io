## What is tl;dv?

tl;dv (short for "too long; didn't view") is an AI-powered meeting recorder that automatically joins your calls, records them, transcribes the conversation, and generates summaries. It's designed for teams who want to stay informed without attending every meeting.

## The Meeting Problem

We've all been there:
- **Too many meetings** competing for attention
- **Important details** get forgotten
- **Team members** miss crucial discussions
- **Action items** fall through the cracks
- **No time** to rewatch hour-long recordings

tl;dv solves this by letting AI handle the note-taking while you focus on the conversation.

## How tl;dv Works

### Step 1: Connect Your Calendar

Link your Google or Microsoft calendar. tl;dv identifies meetings with video call links.

### Step 2: Bot Joins Automatically

The tl;dv bot joins as a participant (attendees see it listed). You can also invite manually.

### Step 3: Record & Transcribe

Everything is captured in real-time. Highlight key moments as they happen.

### Step 4: Get AI Summary

Within minutes of the meeting ending, receive:
- Full transcript
- AI-generated summary
- Extracted action items
- Shareable highlight clips

### Step 5: Share & Integrate

Send insights to team members, push to CRM, or store in your knowledge base.
