# Perplexity AI: The AI Search Engine That Actually Cites Its Sources (2026 Guide)

Google answers questions with a list of links. ChatGPT answers questions but might hallucinate. **Perplexity AI** answers questions with real-time information, direct answers, and — crucially — citations for everything it says.

It's the search engine reimagined for the age of AI, and it's quickly becoming the go-to research tool for professionals, students, and anyone who wants answers, not just links.

## What is Perplexity AI?

Perplexity AI is an **AI-powered answer engine** that combines the breadth of web search with the natural language understanding of large language models. Ask it a question and you get:

- A clear, direct answer synthesized from multiple sources
- Citations for every claim (numbered, clickable, verifiable)
- Follow-up question suggestions
- The ability to ask natural language follow-ups

Think of it as having a research assistant who reads 10 articles and gives you the executive summary — with footnotes.

## Key Features

### Real-Time Web Search
Unlike ChatGPT (without plugins) or Claude, Perplexity searches the web *as you ask*. You get current information, not training data cutoffs. Ask about yesterday's news, today's stock prices, or the latest research paper — it works.

### Cited Sources
Every answer includes numbered citations. Click any number to see exactly which website that information came from. This is transformative for:
- Fact-checking AI output
- Finding primary sources for further reading
- Building trust in the answers you receive

### Perplexity Spaces
Create focused research spaces with persistent context. Collaborate with teammates. Build knowledge bases on specific topics that stay organized over time.

### Pro Search Mode
Perplexity Pro (the paid tier) unlocks:
- **Pro Search:** Deeper research with more sources, synthesized better
- **Model Choice:** GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, or Sonar Large
- **File Upload:** Analyze PDFs, documents, images
- **Image Generation:** DALL-E 3 and Flux integration

Ask Perplexity about a competitor and it'll pull from their website, recent press coverage, LinkedIn updates, and industry analyses — synthesized into a readable briefing.

## How to Use Perplexity Effectively

### Master the Search Modes
- **Quick Search:** Fast answer with basic sources (free)
- **Pro Search:** Deep research mode, clarifying questions, more sources (Pro)
- **Academic Mode:** Focuses on peer-reviewed papers

Switch modes via the mode selector before asking your question.

### Use Focus Modes
Perplexity has focus modes that limit searches to specific domains:
- **Web** — General internet search
- **Academic** — Scholarly articles only
- **YouTube** — Video results with timestamps
- **Reddit** — Community discussions
- **News** — Recent news articles only

For medical questions, use Academic. For product reviews, Reddit. For breaking news, News mode.

### Chain Questions for Deep Research
Perplexity shines in conversation chains:
1. Start broad: *"What is retrieval-augmented generation?"*
2. Go deeper: *"What are the main RAG architectures in 2026?"*
3. Get specific: *"Compare ColBERT vs DPR for RAG retrieval?"*
4. Get practical: *"How do I implement RAG with LangChain?"*

Each follow-up maintains context from previous answers.

### Save & Organize with Collections
Save important searches to Collections. Build a research library organized by project. Share collections with teammates. This turns Perplexity from a search tool into a knowledge management system.

## Pro Tips

- **Start questions with context:** "As a marketing manager, what should I know about..." gets more relevant answers
- **Ask for comparisons:** "Compare X vs Y for [specific use case]" is where Perplexity excels
- **Request format:** "Give me this as a bulleted summary" or "Write this as a table" works well
- **Use the Sources panel:** Click through to primary sources — the AI summary is a starting point, not an endpoint
- **Cite Perplexity searches:** The URL is shareable; great for showing research to colleagues

## Should You Switch to Perplexity?

**Use Perplexity when:**
- You need current information (not training-data answers)
- Citations and source verification matter
- You're doing structured research on a topic
- You want synthesis across multiple sources, not just links

**Stick with Google when:**
- You need image search or Maps
- You're looking for a specific website
- Local search matters (nearby restaurants, etc.)

**Stick with ChatGPT/Claude when:**
- You're doing creative work or writing
- You need a long back-and-forth conversation
- You're working with code or documents

The truth? These tools complement each other. Many power users open Perplexity for research, then take findings into Claude or ChatGPT for drafting and analysis.

## Conclusion

Perplexity AI solves the fundamental problem with AI-generated answers: you never knew if they were true. By grounding every response in real-time web sources and showing you exactly where each claim came from, Perplexity makes AI search *trustworthy*.

It won't replace Google for everything, but for research, fact-checking, and getting up to speed on any topic quickly — it's genuinely better.
