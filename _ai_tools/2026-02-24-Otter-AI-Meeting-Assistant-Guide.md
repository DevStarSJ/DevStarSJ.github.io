# Otter.ai: The AI Meeting Assistant That Transcribes, Summarizes, and Acts

![Business meeting around a table](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1552664730-d307ca884978?w=800)
*Photo by [Jason Goodman](https://unsplash.com/@jasongoodman_youxventures) on Unsplash*

How much time do you spend taking notes in meetings? And how often do you finish a 60-minute call and realize you missed key decisions or action items? **Otter.ai** was built to solve exactly this problem — it joins your meetings, transcribes everything in real time, and delivers a clean summary with action items before you've even closed your laptop.

## What is Otter.ai?

Otter.ai is an AI-powered meeting assistant and transcription platform that:

- **Live transcribes** meetings on Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and in-person
- **Generates AI summaries** at the end of each meeting
- **Extracts action items** and assigns them automatically
- **Answers questions** about your meeting content via chat
- **Integrates** with your calendar, CRM, and project management tools
- **Records audio and video** for later review

Since its founding in 2016, Otter has processed billions of minutes of conversation and become one of the most widely-used productivity tools for professionals and teams.

## Core Features Deep Dive

### Live Transcription

Otter's real-time transcription is remarkably accurate — typically 90%+ for clear speech in English. It supports:
- Speaker identification and labeling (learn who said what)
- Timestamped transcript (click any word to hear that moment)
- Live editing during the meeting
- Custom vocabulary for technical terms, company names, acronyms

**How it works:**
1. Otter joins your meeting via "OtterPilot" bot or you open Otter on your device
2. Transcription begins immediately
3. Speakers are identified and labeled
4. Transcript syncs across all your devices in real time

### AI Meeting Summary

After the meeting, Otter generates:
- **Meeting title** (auto-generated from content)
- **Key takeaways** — bullet point summary of main discussion points
- **Action items** — tasks with assigned owners and due dates (when mentioned)
- **Outline** — hierarchical view of topics covered

The summaries are remarkably useful. Instead of reading a full transcript, you get the distilled essence of what was decided and what needs to happen next.

### OtterPilot — Automated Meeting Bot

For video calls, OtterPilot is a bot that:
- Joins your calendar meetings automatically (configurable)
- Records and transcribes without you having to remember to start it
- Can join meetings even when you're absent (read the notes later)
- Notifies participants that the meeting is being recorded

### AI Chat (Ask Otter)

After transcription, you can ask Otter questions:
- *"What was the final decision on the Q3 budget?"*
- *"What action items were assigned to Sarah?"*
- *"What did we decide about the product launch date?"*
- *"Summarize the main objections from the client"*

This turns your meeting notes into a searchable, queryable knowledge base.

## Getting Started

### Setup (5 minutes)

1. Go to [otter.ai](https://otter.ai) and sign up
2. Connect your Google or Microsoft calendar (recommended)
3. Configure OtterPilot settings — which meetings to join automatically
4. Download the mobile app for on-the-go transcription

### Your First Meeting

**For Zoom/Meet/Teams:**
- When a meeting starts, OtterPilot auto-joins (if configured)
- Or manually click "Start Sync" in your Otter dashboard
- Otter appears as a participant in the meeting

**For in-person meetings:**
- Open the Otter mobile app
- Tap the record button
- Otter transcribes everything spoken nearby

### Managing Your Transcripts

Each meeting creates a "Note" in Otter with:
- Full searchable transcript
- Audio playback synchronized to text
- AI summary panel
- Highlights (you or others can mark key moments)
- Comments and @mentions for team collaboration

## Best Practices

### Before the Meeting

- Teach Otter custom vocabulary: Settings → My Vocab → Add technical terms
- Name your speakers: Set up speaker profiles for regular meeting participants
- Configure OtterPilot: Decide which calendar events trigger auto-join

### During the Meeting

- **Add highlights**: Press the highlighter button when something important is said
- **Add comments**: Note context that won't be clear from transcript alone
- **Don't be distracted**: Let Otter handle notes so you can focus on the conversation

### After the Meeting

- **Review action items**: Edit and assign them properly before sharing
- **Edit speaker labels**: Fix any misidentifications (especially similar voices)
- **Share strategically**: Send the AI summary (not the full transcript) for stakeholders who weren't there
- **Archive regularly**: Organize notes into folders by project or team

## Accuracy Tips

Otter's accuracy depends on:
- **Microphone quality**: Better mic = better transcription
- **Background noise**: Quieter environments dramatically improve accuracy
- **Speaking pace**: Clear, moderately paced speech transcribes better
- **Accents**: Otter handles most accents well; may struggle with very strong accents
- **Technical terms**: Add them to custom vocabulary for better results
