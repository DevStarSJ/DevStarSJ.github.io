## What Is Udio?

**Udio** is an AI music generation platform that creates original, full-length songs from text prompts. It was founded by former Google DeepMind researchers and launched in 2024, quickly establishing itself alongside Suno AI as one of the two best AI music generators.

Unlike simple beat generators or loop makers, Udio produces:
- Complete songs with verses, choruses, and bridges
- Realistic human-sounding vocals (any language, any style)
- Full band instrumentation
- Professional-level mixing and mastering

## Key Features

### 1. Text-to-Music Generation
Describe what you want in natural language:

> *"Upbeat indie pop song about summer road trips, female vocalist, catchy chorus, driving guitar riff, 2010s alternative feel"*

Udio generates two 30-second clips that match your description. You can then extend, remix, or regenerate sections.

### 2. Extend & Edit
- **Extend:** Add verses, intros, bridges, or outros
- **Remix:** Change the style, tempo, or instruments while keeping the melody
- **Regenerate:** Keep the lyrics but try different musical interpretations

### 3. Custom Lyrics
You can provide your own lyrics and Udio will set them to music. Or let the AI write the lyrics based on your theme description.

### 4. Genre Mastery
Udio excels across an impressive range of genres:
- Pop, Rock, Hip-Hop, R&B
- Electronic (EDM, Lo-Fi, Synthwave)
- Country, Folk, Bluegrass
- Jazz, Blues, Classical-inspired
- Metal, Punk, Indie
- K-Pop, Latin, Reggaeton

### 5. Stems & Production Control
Advanced users can influence:
- Tempo and BPM
- Vocal style (male/female/group harmonies)
- Instrumentation emphasis
- Production era (70s, 80s, 90s, modern)

## Getting Started with Udio

### Step 1: Sign Up
Go to [udio.com](https://www.udio.com) and create a free account. You get 40 free credits per month (each generation uses 2 credits, so 20 songs/month free).

### Step 2: Write Your Prompt
Click "Create" and describe your song. Be specific:
- ✅ "Melancholic lo-fi hip-hop instrumental, rainy evening, slow tempo, vinyl crackle"
- ❌ "A good song"

### Step 3: Generate & Listen
Udio produces two 30-second clips in about 30 seconds. Listen to both.

### Step 4: Extend Your Song
Click "Extend" on the clip you prefer to add more sections. Build your complete song one section at a time.

### Step 5: Download
Download the finished track as an MP3 or WAV file.

## Prompt Engineering for Better Music

The quality of your output depends heavily on your prompt. Here's how to write great prompts:

### Structure Your Prompt
```
[Genre/Subgenre] + [Mood/Emotion] + [Theme/Topic] + [Vocal style] + [Instruments] + [Production era/style]
```

### Examples That Work Great

**Cinematic Orchestral:**
> "Epic orchestral film score, building tension, full string section, brass fanfare, percussion, Hans Zimmer style, dramatic"

**Chill Lo-Fi:**
> "Lo-fi hip-hop study beats, jazzy piano, mellow mood, soft drums, late night vibes, lo-fi vinyl aesthetic"

**80s Synthwave:**
> "80s synthwave, neon cyberpunk atmosphere, pulsing synth bass, arpeggiated lead synth, drum machine, nostalgic"

**Folk Acoustic:**
> "Acoustic folk ballad, storytelling, fingerpicking guitar, harmonica, emotional, rural Americana, male vocalist"

## Pro Tips

**1. Stack Genre Tags**
"Lo-fi hip-hop + jazz + bossa nova influences" creates more interesting results than a single genre.

**2. Describe the Feeling, Not Just the Genre**
"Melancholic longing, late autumn evening" gives better results than just "sad music."

**3. Reference Artist Styles (Carefully)**
You can reference general styles: "Daft Punk-inspired electronic" — though direct copying is against terms.

**4. Build Songs in Sections**
Start with the chorus (most important), then extend the verse, then the intro. Don't try to generate the whole song at once.

**5. Use the Remix Feature**
Generated something that's almost right? Use Remix to tweak one element (e.g., change the tempo or swap the vocal style) without starting over.
