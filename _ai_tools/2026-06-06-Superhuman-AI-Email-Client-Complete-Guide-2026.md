## What Is Superhuman?

Superhuman is a **premium AI-powered email client** built on top of Gmail and Outlook. It replaces the default email interface with a blazing-fast, keyboard-shortcut-driven experience layered with AI features designed to help you reach Inbox Zero daily.

Founded by Rahul Vohra in 2015 and launched publicly in 2019, Superhuman has been the go-to email tool for Silicon Valley founders, operators, and investors. In 2026, it's evolved significantly — adding deep AI capabilities that go well beyond simple autocomplete.

### Core Promise

> *"Spend less than an hour a day on email — forever."*

Superhuman achieves this through a combination of:
- **Speed** — everything is keyboard-first, sub-100ms responses
- **AI Triage** — automatically surfaces what matters
- **AI Replies** — draft full email responses in seconds
- **Split Inbox** — separate streams for different contexts
- **Snippets** — reusable reply templates at lightning speed

## Key AI Features in 2026

### 1. AI Triage
Superhuman's AI analyzes your inbox and identifies which emails actually need your attention. It learns from your behavior — what you read, reply to, archive — and surfaces the right emails at the right time.

### 2. Ask AI
Type a question and Superhuman searches your entire email history intelligently. "When did I last talk to Sarah about the contract?" — it finds it instantly, summarizing the relevant thread.

### 3. Write with AI
Highlight a few bullet points about what you want to say, and Superhuman drafts a full, professional email in your voice. You can adjust tone (formal/casual), length (concise/detailed), and iterate until it's right.

![Email composition interface showing AI writing assistance](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1633265486064-086b219458ec?w=1200&auto=format&fit=crop)
*Photo by Maxim Ilyahov on Unsplash*

### 4. Summarize Threads
Long email threads? Superhuman can summarize any thread in one click — giving you the key points without reading 50 messages.

### 5. Auto-Labels and Split Inbox
AI automatically categorizes incoming email into custom streams: newsletters, receipts, team updates, direct messages. Your "important" inbox stays clean and focused.

### 6. Follow-Up Reminders
Superhuman tracks emails you sent and didn't get a reply to. If a deadline is approaching or someone hasn't responded, it reminds you automatically.

### 7. Read Statuses
Know when your emails are read (when recipients allow it). Useful for following up at exactly the right moment.

## Who Is Superhuman For?

Superhuman is designed for **high-volume email users** who value their time:

- **Founders and executives** managing hundreds of emails per day

- **Investors** conducting due diligence over email threads

## The Superhuman Onboarding Experience

One of Superhuman's most famous features is its **white-glove onboarding**. Every new user gets a live 30-minute 1-on-1 session with a Superhuman expert who customizes the setup for your workflow, teaches the keyboard shortcuts, and answers questions.

This isn't just a luxury — it's a deliberate product decision. Superhuman's power comes from its keyboard-driven workflow. Learning it properly is what makes users 2-4x faster at email.
