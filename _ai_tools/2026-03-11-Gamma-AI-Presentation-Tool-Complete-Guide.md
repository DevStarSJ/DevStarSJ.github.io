## What Is Gamma AI?

Gamma is an **AI-powered presentation and document builder** that creates:
- Slide decks (like PowerPoint/Google Slides)
- Documents (like Notion pages)
- Webpages (shareable links)

Unlike traditional tools where you start from scratch, Gamma starts with AI-generated content and structure that you refine — a fundamentally different (and faster) workflow.

## How to Create Your First Presentation

### Step 1: Start with AI
1. Go to [gamma.app](https://gamma.app)
2. Click **"New"** → **"Generate"**
3. Choose **Presentation**, **Document**, or **Webpage**
4. Enter your prompt:

```
"Create a 12-slide presentation on the benefits of remote work 
for a company considering a hybrid policy. Include data and statistics."
```

### Step 2: Customize Theme
- Choose from suggested themes or browse all 50+
- Preview updates live as you change themes
- Adjust accent colors to match your brand

### Step 3: Edit Content
- Click any text block to edit
- Use AI assist (✨) to rewrite, expand, or shorten
- Drag to reorder slides
- Add new slides with "+New Card"

### Step 4: Add Media
- Click the image placeholder → "Generate image" or "Search photos"
- Embed YouTube videos with a URL paste
- Upload your own images, logos, charts

### Step 5: Share
- **Link sharing** — instant shareable URL
- **Export to PDF or PPT** (Pro)
- **Embed** on any website

## Power User Tips

### Tip 1: Paste Your Outline for Better Results
Instead of a vague topic, give Gamma a structured outline:
```
Q1 2026 Marketing Review
- Executive Summary: Key metrics and highlights
- Campaign Performance: Paid vs organic breakdown  
- Social Media: Engagement trends across platforms
- Email Marketing: Open rates and conversion
- Q2 Strategy: Budget allocation and priorities
```

### Tip 2: Use AI to Polish Slide by Slide
After generation, click each slide and use:
- **"Make shorter"** — for text-heavy slides
- **"Add bullet points"** — to break up paragraphs
- **"Suggest icon"** — to find relevant emoji/icon

### Tip 3: Convert Documents to Presentations
Have a Word doc or report? Paste it into Gamma:
```
[Paste entire document text]
"Convert this into a 10-slide executive summary presentation"
```

### Tip 4: Use for More Than Slides
- **Proposals** — Beautiful one-pagers that impress clients
- **Case studies** — Structured with visual flow
- **Newsletters** — Share-as-webpage format
- **Training materials** — Interactive documents
