## What Is Adobe Firefly 4?

Adobe Firefly is Adobe's family of generative AI models, trained on Adobe Stock, openly licensed content, and public domain images. Every image you generate is **commercially safe** — no copyright exposure.

Firefly 4, released in early 2026, brings:
- Dramatically improved photorealism
- Better text rendering in images
- Stronger style consistency
- Video generation (Firefly Video)
- Improved Photoshop/Illustrator integration

---

## Key Features

### Text to Image
Generate images from text prompts with high fidelity. Firefly 4 excels at:
- Product photography
- Marketing visuals
- Concept art
- Realistic portraits and environments

### Generative Fill (Photoshop)
The killer feature. Select any area of a photo, describe what you want, and Firefly fills it — seamlessly matching lighting, perspective, and style.

Real use case: Remove a distracting object from a photo and fill it with matching background. 30 seconds of work.

### Generative Expand
Extend images beyond their borders. Perfect for:
- Resizing images for different aspect ratios
- Adding background to tight product shots
- Expanding scenes without cropping

### Text Effects
Apply generative AI to typography — filling letters with textures, scenes, and styles that would take hours in Photoshop manually.

### Structure Reference
Upload a sketch or rough layout, and Firefly generates a polished image matching your composition. Game-changing for concept artists and designers.

### Style Reference
Match the visual style of a reference image. Maintain brand consistency across hundreds of generated assets.

### Firefly Video (New in 4)
Generate short video clips from text prompts or images. Early-stage but promising — integrates with Premiere Pro.

## Getting Started

### Option 1: Firefly Web App
1. Go to [firefly.adobe.com](https://firefly.adobe.com)
2. Sign in with your Adobe ID (free account works)
3. Choose Text to Image, Generative Fill, etc.
4. Use prompt + optional reference image

### Option 2: Inside Photoshop
1. Open any image in Photoshop (2024+)
2. Select an area with any selection tool
3. Click "Generative Fill" in the contextual toolbar
4. Type your prompt
5. Press Generate — 3 variations appear as separate layers

### Option 3: Adobe Express
For quick social media content, use Adobe Express — Firefly-powered but with templates and brand kit integration.

## Prompt Tips

**Be specific:**
```
❌ "a coffee shop"
✅ "a cozy corner coffee shop interior, warm lighting, wooden furniture, 
    steam rising from coffee cups, morning sun through windows, 
    Sony A7R photography style"
```

**Use style modifiers:**
- `cinematic lighting`
- `product photography, studio lighting, white background`
- `watercolor painting`
- `architectural visualization`
- `35mm film grain`

**Reference images:** Always use a reference image for style when you need brand consistency.
