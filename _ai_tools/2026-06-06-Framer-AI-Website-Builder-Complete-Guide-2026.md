## What Is Framer?

Framer is a **visual website builder powered by AI** that enables anyone to create stunning, responsive websites without writing code. Originally launched as a prototyping tool for designers, Framer has evolved into a full website publishing platform with powerful AI features layered throughout.

In 2026, Framer occupies a unique position: it produces sites that look and feel like they were hand-crafted by a professional design agency — but can be built in an afternoon by anyone.

### What Makes Framer Different?

Unlike Wix or Squarespace, Framer:
- Uses **real CSS and web standards** under the hood
- Produces sites that are **genuinely fast** (passes Core Web Vitals easily)
- Supports **advanced animations and interactions** without plugins
- Generates sites from text descriptions with AI

Unlike Webflow, Framer:
- Has a much gentler **learning curve**
- Offers built-in **AI content generation**
- Is significantly **more affordable**

## Key AI Features in 2026

### 1. AI Site Generation
Describe your website in plain text — "I need a portfolio site for a freelance UX designer, minimalist and modern" — and Framer generates a complete multi-page website with layout, sections, dummy content, and color scheme. You then customize from there.

This is the starting point that gets you from zero to 80% done in under a minute.

### 2. AI Text Generation
Inside any text element, you can ask the AI to write copy for you. Describe your product or service, and it generates headlines, body copy, CTAs, and About sections that you can refine or accept outright.

### 3. AI Image Generation
No photo? No problem. Framer integrates with AI image generation to produce placeholder and hero images that match your site's aesthetic. You can generate, regenerate, and insert images without leaving the editor.

### 4. AI Localization
Framer can translate your entire website into multiple languages with one click, maintaining layout integrity and adapting copy naturally. Essential for global products in 2026.

### 5. Smart Components
Framer's component system uses AI to suggest the right pre-built elements for your context. Add a pricing table, FAQ accordion, or testimonials section — the AI suggests and places them intelligently within your layout.

## Core Framer Capabilities

### Visual Editor
Framer's editor is canvas-based and feels closer to Figma than a traditional website builder. You can freely position elements, adjust spacing with precision, and see your changes in real time across device sizes.

### Animation and Interactions
This is where Framer genuinely stands out. Adding scroll animations, hover effects, page transitions, and parallax effects is point-and-click — no JavaScript required. The results look expensive.

### CMS
Framer has a built-in CMS for blogs, case studies, portfolios, and product catalogs. Connect your CMS to components and build dynamic pages without a database.

### Responsive Design
Every Framer site is inherently responsive. The breakpoint editor lets you adjust layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile with precision, and AI can suggest responsive adjustments automatically.

### SEO Tools
Built-in SEO controls: meta titles, descriptions, Open Graph images, sitemap generation, canonical URLs, structured data. AI can generate SEO-optimized meta content for every page.

## Limitations

- **No e-commerce** — Framer isn't built for online stores; use Shopify instead
- **Limited custom code** — power users wanting complex custom functionality may hit walls
- **No native membership/gating** — requires third-party tools for private content
- **CMS limits** — lower plans have item limits that can constrain content-heavy sites
- **New platform risk** — though well-funded, it's younger than Webflow/Squarespace

## Getting Started with Framer AI

1. **Go to framer.com** and sign up (free)
2. **Click "Start with AI"** — describe your website concept
3. **Review and refine** the generated site in the editor
4. **Customize content** — use AI text generation for copy
5. **Adjust styling** — fonts, colors, spacing
6. **Add CMS content** if needed
7. **Publish** to Framer subdomain or connect a custom domain

## Tips for Best Results

**AI Prompts That Work Well:**
- Be specific: "SaaS landing page for a project management tool, dark mode, professional"
- Mention your audience: "Portfolio for a motion designer, with showreel section"
- Specify aesthetic: "Minimalist, lots of white space, editorial feel"

**After AI Generation:**
- Replace all placeholder text with real content immediately
- Swap AI images for your actual brand images
- Adjust the color palette to match your brand
- Test on mobile — often needs refinement
