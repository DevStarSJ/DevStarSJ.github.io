## What is Pika Labs?

**Pika Labs** is an AI video generation platform that transforms text prompts and images into high-quality video clips. Since its launch, Pika has gained massive popularity for its ability to create cinematic, animated, and stylized videos with simple text descriptions.

Whether you're a content creator, marketer, or just someone who wants to bring ideas to life, Pika Labs offers an accessible way to produce professional-looking videos without any video editing experience.

## How to Use Pika Labs

### Getting Started

1. **Sign Up**: Visit [pika.art](https://pika.art) and create an account
2. **Choose Mode**: Text-to-video, image-to-video, or video-to-video
3. **Enter Prompt**: Describe what you want
4. **Configure**: Set aspect ratio, duration, and style
5. **Generate**: Wait for your video (typically 1-3 minutes)
6. **Download**: Save in MP4 format

### Prompt Writing Tips

**For Best Results:**
```
"Aerial drone shot of a coastal city at sunset, 
golden hour lighting, smooth camera movement, 
hyper-realistic, 8K resolution, cinematic"
```

**Include:**
- Subject/scene description
- Camera movement (pan, zoom, tracking)
- Lighting conditions
- Style (cinematic, anime, realistic)
- Quality keywords (4K, HD, detailed)

### Using Image-to-Video

1. **Upload Image**: Drag your image to Pika
2. **Add Motion Prompt**: Describe how it should move
3. **Adjust Settings**: Camera motion, duration
4. **Generate**: Create the animated version

Example prompt for a landscape photo:
```
"Gentle breeze moving through the trees, 
clouds slowly drifting, birds flying in distance"
```

## Advanced Techniques

### Style Consistency
Keep the same style across multiple clips by using consistent prompts:
```
"[Scene description], Studio Ghibli style, 
soft colors, hand-drawn animation feel"
```

### Camera Movements
Specify exact camera work:
- `dolly in` - Camera moves forward
- `pan left/right` - Horizontal rotation
- `tilt up/down` - Vertical rotation
- `tracking shot` - Following movement
- `crane shot` - Vertical camera movement

### Negative Prompts
Exclude unwanted elements:
```
"Beautiful sunset beach scene, 
negative: blurry, distorted, low quality"
```

## Limitations

- Maximum video length is 10 seconds (extendable with regeneration)
- Complex physics and interactions may look unnatural
- Human faces can sometimes be distorted
- Text rendering in videos is inconsistent
- Some prompts may require multiple attempts

## Tips for Better Results

1. **Be Specific**: Detailed prompts yield better videos
2. **Use References**: Mention specific styles, movies, or artists
3. **Iterate**: Generate multiple versions and pick the best
4. **Combine Clips**: Use external editors to create longer sequences
5. **Quality Keywords**: Include "high quality", "detailed", "4K"
6. **Camera Direction**: Always specify camera movement
7. **Lighting**: Describe lighting conditions explicitly
