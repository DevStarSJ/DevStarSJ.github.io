HeyGen offers a diverse library of photorealistic AI presenters in various ages, ethnicities, and professional styles. Avatars move naturally, blink, gesture, and maintain eye contact — indistinguishable from a real recording in many cases.

### 2. Instant Avatar Creation
Upload a 2-minute video of yourself, and HeyGen creates your **personal AI avatar** in about 30 minutes. Your digital twin can then present any script in your voice and likeness — even while you sleep.

### 3. Voice Cloning
Record 3 minutes of your voice, and HeyGen clones it. Your avatar speaks in your exact voice, with your natural cadence and tone. No robotic text-to-speech — it sounds like *you*.

### 4. Video Translation (130+ Languages)
This is HeyGen's killer feature: **automatic video translation with lip sync**. Upload any video, choose target languages, and HeyGen:
- Transcribes the original audio
- Translates to target language
- Re-synthesizes audio in the original voice (translated)
- Re-syncs lip movements to match the new audio

The result: your video, in German, Japanese, Spanish — with the original presenter's voice, cloned and translated.

Write or paste your script, add pauses, emphasis, and pronunciation guides. The editor previews how the avatar will deliver each line before you generate.

## How to Create Your First HeyGen Video

### Step 1: Choose an Avatar
Browse the avatar library and select one that matches your brand tone. Filter by gender, age, style (professional, casual), and ethnicity.

### Step 2: Write Your Script
Type or paste your script in the editor. HeyGen supports:
- **SSML tags** for pronunciation and pacing
- **Pause markers** `[pause]` for natural delivery
- **Emphasis tags** for important words

**Pro tip:** Write conversationally. Formal business writing sounds unnatural when spoken. Read it aloud first.

### Step 3: Choose Voice
Select from 300+ text-to-speech voices, or use your cloned voice. Preview voices before finalizing.

### Step 4: Customize Appearance
- Background: Upload custom backgrounds or choose from templates
- Layout: Choose from pre-designed layouts or create custom
- Logo: Add your company logo
- B-roll: Overlay screen recordings, images, or animations

### Step 5: Generate & Download
Click "Submit" and your video renders in 2-5 minutes. Download as MP4 or share via link.

## Tips for Best Results

### Script Quality Matters Most
The avatar can only be as good as your script. Invest time here. Short, punchy sentences work best. Avoid complex technical jargon unless your audience expects it.

### Match Avatar to Brand
A casual D2C brand shouldn't use a stiff formal avatar. Choose or create an avatar that reflects your brand personality.

### Use B-Roll for Complex Explanations
When explaining a product or process, overlay screen recordings or diagrams. The avatar + B-roll combination increases comprehension dramatically.

### Leverage Templates
Don't start from scratch every time. Create templates for recurring video types (weekly updates, product announcements) and reuse them.

### Quality Check Before Finalizing
Preview at 1.25x speed — if it sounds good fast, it sounds great at normal speed. Check lip sync closely, especially on custom avatar videos.

## Limitations

- **Uncanny valley risk:** Very close inspection can reveal AI artifacts, especially in complex gestures
- **Emotional range:** Avatars struggle with deeply emotional content (grief, intense excitement)
- **Long-form content:** Very long scripts (30+ minutes) are better split into segments
- **Source video quality for translation:** Low-quality input videos produce lower-quality translated output
- **Content restrictions:** Cannot generate misleading or deceptive content representing real people without consent
