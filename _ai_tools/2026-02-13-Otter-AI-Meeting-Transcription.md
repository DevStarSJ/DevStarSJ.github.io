## What is Otter.ai?

**Otter.ai** is an AI-powered transcription service that converts speech to text in real-time. It's designed primarily for meetings, interviews, lectures, and any scenario where you need accurate, searchable notes without manual typing.

With integrations for Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, Otter has become an essential productivity tool for professionals who want to focus on conversations rather than note-taking.

## Key Features

### 1. Real-Time Transcription

Otter transcribes conversations as they happen with impressive accuracy. The AI:
- Identifies different speakers
- Punctuates automatically
- Handles multiple accents
- Works in noisy environments

### 2. Meeting Integration

Otter integrates seamlessly with:
- **Zoom**: Auto-joins and transcribes
- **Google Meet**: Direct integration
- **Microsoft Teams**: Full support
- **Calendar sync**: Auto-schedules recordings

### 3. OtterPilot™

The AI assistant can:
- Join meetings automatically
- Take notes in real-time
- Generate meeting summaries
- Extract action items
- Share notes with participants

### 4. Search and Highlight

Every word is searchable. Click on any word in the transcript to jump to that exact moment in the audio. Highlight key points for easy reference.

## How to Use Otter.ai

### For Live Meetings

1. **Connect Calendar**: Link your Google or Microsoft calendar
2. **Enable OtterPilot**: Turn on auto-join for meetings
3. **Start Meeting**: Otter joins automatically
4. **Review**: Access transcripts after the meeting
5. **Share**: Send notes to attendees

### For Recorded Audio

1. **Upload**: Drag and drop audio/video files
2. **Wait**: Processing takes a few minutes
3. **Edit**: Review and correct any errors
4. **Export**: Download as TXT, DOCX, or PDF

### For Mobile Recording

1. **Open App**: Launch Otter on iOS/Android
2. **Record**: Tap the record button
3. **Transcribe**: Real-time text appears
4. **Save**: Auto-syncs to cloud

## Tips for Better Accuracy

1. **Use Quality Microphones**: Better audio = better transcription
2. **Minimize Background Noise**: Find quiet spaces
3. **Speak Clearly**: Enunciate, especially for technical terms
4. **Add Custom Vocabulary**: Train Otter on industry-specific terms
5. **Correct Mistakes**: The AI learns from your edits

## Limitations

- Accuracy drops with heavy accents or multiple speakers talking simultaneously
- Free tier limited to 300 minutes/month
- Requires internet connection
- Non-English languages have lower accuracy
- Background music can interfere with transcription
