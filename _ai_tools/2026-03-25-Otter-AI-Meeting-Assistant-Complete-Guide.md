## What Is Otter.ai?

Otter.ai is an AI-powered meeting assistant that provides real-time transcription, automated summaries, and action item extraction. Founded in 2016 and now handling millions of meetings monthly, Otter integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and more — joining your calls automatically to capture everything without any manual effort.

## Key Features

### 1. Real-Time Transcription
Otter's core capability — live transcription that appears as words are spoken:
- **Speaker identification**: Names each speaker automatically (learns over time)
- **99%+ accuracy** for clear audio in English
- **Multiple languages**: 30+ languages supported
- **Live captions**: Accessible to all meeting participants via shared link

### 2. OtterPilot — The Automated Meeting Bot
OtterPilot automatically joins your scheduled meetings (Zoom, Meet, Teams, Webex) without you needing to manually start recording:
- Joins 2 minutes before meeting start
- Records and transcribes in real-time
- Stays even if you're late or have to leave early
- Sends post-meeting summary automatically

### 3. AI Meeting Summary
After every meeting, Otter generates:
- **Abstract summary**: 3-5 sentence overview
- **Bullet point outline**: Key discussion topics
- **Action items**: Automatically extracted tasks with assigned owners
- **Key quotes**: Important statements highlighted
- **Keywords**: Searchable topic tags

### 4. Otter AI Chat
Ask questions about any meeting transcript using natural language:
- "What did Sarah say about the Q3 budget?"
- "What are all the action items from this week's meetings?"
- "Summarize the main objections raised about the product launch"

The AI searches across all your transcripts to answer contextual questions.

### 5. Integrations
Otter connects with your existing workflow:
- **Calendar**: Google Calendar and Outlook for automatic meeting detection
- **Zoom/Meet/Teams**: Native bot integration
- **Slack**: Post summaries automatically to channels
- **Salesforce**: Log meeting notes to CRM automatically
- **HubSpot**: Sync meeting insights to contacts

### 6. Collaboration Features
- **Comments**: Add inline comments on transcript segments
- **Highlights**: Mark important moments during live transcription
- **Shared workspaces**: Team access to meeting library
- **Follow-up emails**: Generate and send meeting recap emails with one click

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Set Up Calendar Integration
1. Create account at [otter.ai](https://otter.ai)
2. Connect Google or Outlook calendar
3. Grant Otter permission to join scheduled meetings

### Step 2: First Meeting
- OtterPilot will auto-join your next Zoom/Meet/Teams call
- You'll see the transcription link shared in the meeting chat
- Review the summary and action items within 5 minutes of meeting end

### Step 3: Train Speaker Identification
- Name each speaker in your first few transcripts
- Otter learns voice signatures over time
- After 3-5 meetings with the same people, identification becomes automatic

### Step 4: Custom Vocabulary
(Business+) Add:
- Company name and product names
- Industry jargon
- Colleague names
- Client names

This dramatically improves accuracy for domain-specific language.

## Pro Tips for Power Users

**Keyboard shortcuts during live meetings:**
- `Ctrl/Cmd + 1-4`: Insert predefined highlights
- `Ctrl/Cmd + I`: Insert image or screenshot
- Space bar (mobile): Start/pause recording

**Silence the bot when needed:**
Send "stop recording" in meeting chat — OtterPilot will exit.

**Search across all meetings:**
Otter's universal search finds specific topics across months of transcripts — invaluable for "didn't we discuss this two months ago?"

**Action item export:**
Copy all action items to clipboard for direct paste into Asana, Notion, or your task manager of choice.

**Vocabulary for better accuracy:**
Before high-stakes meetings, add technical terms or uncommon names to vocabulary settings.

## Limitations

- **Accuracy drops** with heavy accents, background noise, or technical jargon (mitigated by custom vocabulary)
- **Privacy concerns**: Cloud-based transcription means recordings are stored on Otter's servers
- **OtterPilot presence** can sometimes feel awkward to guests unfamiliar with AI bots
- **Non-English support** is significantly less accurate than English
- **Storage limits** — older transcripts may be archived on lower plans
