## Getting Started with Motion

### Initial Setup

1. **Connect your calendars**: Google, Outlook, or Apple Calendar
2. **Set work hours**: Define when you're available
3. **Add existing tasks**: Import from other tools or start fresh
4. **Configure preferences**: Meeting buffers, focus time, etc.

### Creating Tasks Effectively

Include these details for best results:
- **Deadline**: When must this be done?
- **Duration estimate**: How long will it take?
- **Priority**: High, medium, or low
- **Schedule preferences**: Morning/afternoon, specific days

### Sample Task Entry

```
Task: Prepare client presentation
Deadline: Friday 5pm
Duration: 3 hours
Priority: High
Notes: Need quiet focus time, preferably morning
```

Motion will find three hours of morning focus time before Friday, avoiding meetings and low-energy periods.
