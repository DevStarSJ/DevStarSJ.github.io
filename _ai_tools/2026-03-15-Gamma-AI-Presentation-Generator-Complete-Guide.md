## What Is Gamma AI?

Gamma is an AI-powered presentation and document creation platform. Unlike traditional slide tools, Gamma generates complete, beautifully designed presentations from a brief text description — including content, layout, visuals, and formatting.

**Key differentiators:**
- Generate a full deck in **~30 seconds** from a prompt
- Responsive design that works on all devices
- Built-in analytics to track viewer engagement
- Publish as a website, not just a file

## How Gamma Works

### Step 1: Start with a Prompt
Go to [gamma.app](https://gamma.app) and click **Create New**. Choose from:
- **Generate**: AI creates everything from your description
- **Paste in text**: Convert existing content to slides
- **Template**: Start from a curated template

### Step 2: Describe What You Want
Enter a brief description:
```
"A 10-slide pitch deck for a B2B SaaS startup that helps restaurants 
reduce food waste using AI inventory management. Include market size, 
problem/solution, business model, and team slides."
```

### Step 3: Review AI-Generated Outline
Gamma shows you a proposed outline before generating. You can:
- Edit, reorder, or remove sections
- Adjust the number of slides
- Specify the tone (professional, casual, academic)

### Step 4: Generate & Refine
Click **Generate** and within 30-60 seconds you have a complete presentation. Then:
- Edit any text inline
- Swap images with a click
- Change the entire theme with one click
- Ask AI to rewrite specific sections

## Pro Tips

**1. Be Specific in Your Prompt**
```
❌ "Make a presentation about marketing"
✅ "Create a 12-slide presentation about content marketing strategy 
   for B2B SaaS companies, targeting CMOs, with a data-driven approach 
   and actionable tactics for each stage of the funnel"
```

**2. Use the Paste Feature**
Have a Word doc or Google Doc? Paste it directly into Gamma and watch it transform into a presentation automatically.

**3. Leverage AI Card Edits**
Don't accept the first generation as final. Use AI to iteratively refine each card until it's perfect.

**4. Mix Card Types**
Gamma supports many card types in one deck: text cards, image cards, video embeds, code blocks, tables. Mix them for variety.

**5. Export When Needed**
For clients who need traditional files:
- Export as PDF
- Export as PowerPoint (.pptx) — paid plan
- Share as web link
