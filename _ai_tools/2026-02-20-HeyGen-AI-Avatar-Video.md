## Step-by-Step: Creating Your First HeyGen Video

### Step 1: Write Your Script

Keep it conversational. Aim for 130-160 words per minute (normal speech rate):

> "Hi there. I'm excited to show you our new project management dashboard. In just three minutes, I'll walk you through the key features that help teams stay on track. First, let's look at the timeline view..."

**Tips:**
- Write how you speak, not how you write
- Use short sentences
- Avoid complex technical jargon
- Add natural pauses with commas

### Step 2: Choose Your Avatar

Browse HeyGen's library:
- Filter by gender, age, ethnicity

- Select or upload a background image/video

### Step 5: Generate and Download

1. Click "Generate Video"
2. Wait 2-10 minutes (depending on length)
3. Preview in HeyGen
4. Download as MP4 (720p, 1080p, or 4K)
5. Share directly or upload to YouTube/LinkedIn/Vimeo

## Tips for High-Quality Results

### Script Optimization

- **Start with a hook**: First 5 seconds determine watch time
- **Use contractions**: "I'm" not "I am" sounds more natural
- **Mark pauses**: Use `...` or `[pause]` in your script
- **Keep it short**: Under 3 minutes for business videos

### Avatar Selection

- Match avatar to your audience
- Corporate B2B → formal business attire
- Consumer/startup → casual, friendly avatar
- Consider diversity and representation

### Quality Settings

- Always use 1080p minimum for professional use
- Enable captions for accessibility (80% of LinkedIn videos watched without sound)
