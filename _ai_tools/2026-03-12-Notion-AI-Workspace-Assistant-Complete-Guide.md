# Notion AI: Your Intelligent Workspace Assistant

**Notion AI** transforms the already-powerful Notion workspace into an intelligent productivity system. From drafting documents to analyzing databases, Notion AI is deeply integrated into your workflow — not bolted on as an afterthought.

## What Is Notion AI?

Notion AI is an AI layer built directly into Notion — the popular all-in-one workspace for notes, wikis, tasks, and databases. It uses a combination of top-tier LLMs (including GPT-4 and Claude) to help you:

- Write and edit content faster
- Summarize long documents
- Auto-fill database properties
- Answer questions about your workspace
- Generate action items from meeting notes

Unlike standalone AI tools, Notion AI knows the context of your workspace — it can reference your pages, databases, and projects.

---

## Core Features

### 1. AI Writing Assistant
Highlight any text and invoke AI to:
- **Improve writing** — fix grammar, tone, and clarity
- **Make shorter/longer** — adjust length on demand
- **Change tone** — professional, casual, persuasive
- **Translate** — 100+ languages supported
- **Continue writing** — extend your draft naturally

### 2. Summarize Pages
Drop any lengthy document — meeting notes, research papers, project briefs — and get a crisp summary in seconds. Works on entire pages or selected sections.

### 3. Q&A Search
Ask Notion AI questions like:
- *"What did we decide about the Q2 marketing budget?"*
- *"Find all tasks assigned to me that are overdue"*
- *"Summarize the feedback from the user interviews last month"*

It searches across your entire workspace and synthesizes a direct answer.

### 4. AI Autofill for Databases
For any database, you can add an AI-powered property that automatically fills based on a custom prompt. Examples:
- Auto-generate tags from article content
- Summarize CRM notes into one-line deal status
- Classify support tickets by priority

### 5. Action Items from Notes
Paste meeting notes → Notion AI extracts action items, owners, and deadlines automatically.

## Notion AI Q&A: The Game Changer

The **Q&A feature** is what sets Notion AI apart from generic AI tools. Instead of searching through pages manually, you ask natural questions and get answers with citations pointing to the source pages.

This effectively turns your Notion workspace into a **searchable knowledge base** that answers in plain language.

## How to Enable Notion AI

1. Open any Notion page
2. Press **Space** on an empty line (or hit `/AI`)
3. Choose your action: Draft, Summarize, Translate, etc.
4. For Q&A: Click the AI search icon in the sidebar
