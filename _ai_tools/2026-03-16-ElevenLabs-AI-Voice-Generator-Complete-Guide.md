## Tips for Best Results

**1. Clean your script**
Remove filler words, abbreviations, and unusual formatting. ElevenLabs reads what you write — "Dr." may read differently than "Doctor."

**2. Use SSML-like tags**
ElevenLabs supports pause and pronunciation control:
- Add `<break time="0.5s" />` for pauses
- Use the pronunciation dictionary for custom words
- Adjust stability/similarity settings per paragraph

**3. Match voice to content**
High-energy voices for promotional content, warm and steady voices for educational material, character voices for fiction narration.

**4. Record quality audio for cloning**
For voice cloning, use a quiet room, a decent microphone, and 2-3 minutes of varied speech (not just one tone). Avoid music or background noise.

**5. Use Projects for long content**
Don't paste an entire chapter into the single-text box. The Projects feature handles chapter management, character assignment, and batch rendering much more efficiently.
