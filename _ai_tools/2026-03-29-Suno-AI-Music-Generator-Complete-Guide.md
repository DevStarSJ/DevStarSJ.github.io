## What Is Suno AI?

Suno is a generative AI music platform that creates original songs complete with:
- **Vocals** — actual singing in any style
- **Lyrics** — auto-generated or custom
- **Instruments** — full backing tracks
- **Genre matching** — from pop to death metal to J-pop

It's not a sample-based tool — Suno creates entirely original audio from scratch. The results are surprisingly professional, especially for certain genres like pop, hip-hop, and electronic music.

## How It Works

### Basic Generation (Simple Mode)
1. Go to [suno.ai](https://suno.ai)
2. Type a description of the song you want
3. Click **Create**
4. Get 2 variations in ~30 seconds

**Example prompt:**
```
An upbeat indie pop song about a programmer who falls in love with an AI, 
catchy chorus, clean guitar, energetic drums
```

### Custom Mode (Full Control)
Enable **Custom Mode** to specify:
- **Lyrics:** Write your own or paste existing
- **Style of Music:** Detailed genre/instrument description  
- **Title:** Song name

```yaml
Title: "Compile Me Tender"
Lyrics: |
  [Verse 1]
  Running loops in my mind all night
  Debugging dreams by the screen's cold light
  Every function I wrote for you
  Returns a value that feels so true
  
  [Chorus]
  Compile me tender, compile me sweet
  Let our code complete
  Git push to my heart, merge request for two
  Compile me tender, I love you
  
Style: Indie pop, jangly guitar, warm vocals, nostalgic 80s synths
```

## Advanced Tips

### Extend Your Song
After generating, use the **Extend** feature to make a song longer. You can guide the extension: "Add a guitar solo section" or "Bring in a bridge that slows down."

### Remaster / Remix
Use **Cover Song** mode to take your favorite songs and reinterpret them in a different genre:
- "Beethoven's 5th as a trap banger"
- "Sweet Home Alabama as K-pop"
- "Any pop song as an acoustic folk cover"

### Inpainting (Edit Sections)
Regenerate specific parts of a song you don't like while keeping the rest. Click on the waveform to select a section and regenerate just that part.

### Use [Section Tags] in Lyrics
Structure your song properly:
```
[Intro]
[Verse 1]
[Pre-Chorus]
[Chorus]
[Verse 2]
[Bridge]
[Outro]
[Instrumental break]
[Build-up]
[Drop]
```
