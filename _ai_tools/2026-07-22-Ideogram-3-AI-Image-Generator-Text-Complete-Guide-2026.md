## What Is Ideogram?

Ideogram is an AI image generation platform founded in 2023 by former Google Brain researchers. Its defining innovation: **generating images with accurate, readable text** — something that plagued earlier AI models like DALL-E and Stable Diffusion for years.

Ideogram 3, released in 2026, takes this capability to new heights while dramatically improving overall image quality, photorealism, and creative range.

## Getting Started with Ideogram 3

### Sign Up
1. Visit [ideogram.ai](https://ideogram.ai)
2. Sign up with Google, Apple, or email
3. Free plan includes daily generation credits

### Basic Generation

1. **Write your prompt**: Be specific about what you want, including any text elements
2. **Select a style**: Choose from the style options (Realistic, Design, Illustration, etc.)
3. **Set aspect ratio**: Square, landscape, portrait, or custom
4. **Generate**: Click the generate button and get 4 variations

### Prompt Writing for Text

For best results with text in images, use quotation marks around the text you want:

**Example prompt:**
```
A coffee shop sign reading "Daily Grind" in rustic wooden lettering, 
warm lighting, cozy atmosphere, lifestyle photography style
```

**For logo design:**
```
Modern tech startup logo for a company called "NovaSpark", 
minimalist design, electric blue and white, clean sans-serif typography
```

## Advanced Tips

### 1. Specify Font Style
Don't just say "text" — describe the font:
- "Bold serif typography"
- "Handwritten chalk lettering"
- "Neon glowing text"
- "Embossed gold metallic letters"
- "Vintage distressed type"

### 2. Describe Text Placement
Tell Ideogram where the text should appear:
- "Text centered at the top of the image"
- "Title overlaid on the sky portion"
- "Logo in bottom-right corner"

### 3. Use Multiple Text Elements
Ideogram 3 handles multiple text elements:
```
Book cover design with title "The Last Signal" in large gothic font 
at top, subtitle "A Thriller Novel" in smaller text below, 
author name "J.K. Morrison" at the bottom
```

### 4. Negative Prompts
Use negative prompts to avoid common issues:
- "no blurry text"
- "no misspelled words"
- "sharp, legible text"

### 5. Iterate and Edit
Use Ideogram's remix feature to tweak specific elements while keeping the overall composition.
