## What is Zapier?

Zapier is a no-code automation platform that connects over 7,000 apps and services. A "Zap" is an automated workflow that runs when a trigger event occurs. For example:

- *When* a new lead fills out a form → *then* add them to your CRM, send a welcome email, and notify your sales team on Slack
- *When* a customer submits a support ticket → *then* create a task in Asana and reply with a confirmation
- *When* you publish a blog post → *then* share it on LinkedIn, tweet about it, and save it to Notion

## The AI Revolution in Zapier

Zapier's AI features transform how you build and run automations:

### AI Zap Builder
Describe what you want in plain English:
> "When someone submits a new entry in my Typeform survey, save their response to Google Sheets, send them a personalized thank-you email via Gmail, and add them to my Mailchimp audience"

Zapier AI will build the entire workflow, configure the steps, and set up field mappings — ready for your review and activation.

### AI Steps (Formatter, Code, and More)
Add AI-powered processing steps directly in your Zaps:

- **Classify text** — Is this email urgent or routine? Positive or negative?
- **Extract information** — Pull name, email, phone from unstructured text
- **Generate content** — Create personalized email drafts or summaries
- **Translate** — Convert content between languages automatically
- **Summarize** — Condense long emails or documents to key points

### Copilot (AI Assistant)
Zapier's Copilot feature helps you troubleshoot errors, optimize workflows, and discover automation opportunities across your connected apps.

## Tips for Effective Automation

**Start simple** — Begin with a 2-step Zap before building complex multi-step workflows. Master the basics first.

**Use filters** — Don't trigger actions on every event. Add filter conditions to only run Zaps when they matter.

**Test thoroughly** — Always test your Zaps before trusting them with real data. Use Zapier's built-in testing tools.

**Monitor task usage** — Track your task consumption to avoid hitting plan limits unexpectedly.

**Leverage paths** — Use Paths (conditional branches) to handle different scenarios in a single Zap.

**Document your Zaps** — Add descriptions to your Zaps so you (and your team) remember why they exist.
