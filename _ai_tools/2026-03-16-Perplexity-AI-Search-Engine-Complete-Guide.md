## What Is Perplexity AI?

Perplexity AI is an **AI-powered answer engine** that searches the web in real time and synthesizes results into a single, well-sourced response. Think of it as a hybrid between Google and ChatGPT — you ask a question in natural language, and it returns a direct answer with numbered citations you can verify instantly.

**Core strengths:**
- **Real-time web access** — no knowledge cutoff for factual queries
- **Inline citations** — every claim is linked to a source
- **Follow-up questions** — conversation-style exploration of any topic
- **Deep Research mode** — multi-step autonomous research reports

---

## Key Features

### 1. Instant Answers with Sources
Unlike standard chatbots that may hallucinate, Perplexity retrieves live web content and grounds its answers in verifiable sources. You see exactly where every piece of information comes from.

### 2. Focus Modes
Perplexity lets you narrow the search scope:
- **Web** — broad internet search
- **Academic** — peer-reviewed papers and scholarly sources
- **YouTube** — video content and transcripts
- **Reddit** — community discussions and real opinions
- **Wolfram|Alpha** — math and computation
- **News** — latest headlines

### 3. Deep Research
Perplexity Pro's **Deep Research** feature autonomously conducts multi-step research, reads dozens of sources, and delivers a comprehensive report — similar to hiring a research assistant. A full competitive analysis or literature review takes minutes instead of hours.

### 4. Collections & Spaces
Organize your research into **Spaces** — shared knowledge bases where teams can add sources, run queries, and build institutional memory. Great for competitive intelligence, due diligence, or ongoing research projects.

### 5. Perplexity Pages
Generate polished, shareable research reports from any query. Pages include structured sections, visuals, and full citations — ideal for sharing findings with colleagues or clients.

## Best Use Cases

### Research & Due Diligence
Before meetings, investments, or important decisions, run Perplexity queries to get up-to-date briefings with sources you can verify. Ask "What are the latest developments in [company/topic]?" for instant intelligence.

### Academic Study
Use Academic focus mode to surface relevant papers. Students and researchers use Perplexity to understand new fields quickly and find primary sources for further reading.

### Competitive Analysis
Ask "How does [Product A] compare to [Product B] in 2026?" and get a structured breakdown sourced from reviews, forums, and news — saving hours of manual research.

### Fact-Checking
Perplexity excels at quickly verifying claims. The inline citations make it easy to spot when sources disagree or when a claim isn't well-supported.

### Technical Problem-Solving
For coding errors, configuration issues, or "how to" questions, Perplexity often surfaces the most current Stack Overflow threads and GitHub issues — avoiding the stale answers ChatGPT might give from its training cutoff.

## Pro Tips

**1. Be specific in your prompts**
"What are the best AI tools for video editing in 2026, focusing on free options?" will get far better results than "AI video tools."

**2. Chain follow-up questions**
Perplexity maintains conversation context. Start broad, then drill down: ask a general question, then follow up with "Can you elaborate on point #3?"

**3. Upload documents for analysis**
Pro users can upload PDFs, spreadsheets, or images and ask questions about them — great for contract review, report analysis, or comparing specifications.

**4. Use the mobile app**
The Perplexity iOS and Android apps support voice search and have a clean, fast interface. The "Discover" tab surfaces trending research topics.

**5. Save and organize with Spaces**
For ongoing projects, create a dedicated Space and add relevant sources. Team members can query the curated knowledge base for consistent, shared research.

## Limitations to Know

- **Not a replacement for primary sources** — always follow citations to verify critical information
- **Bias in source selection** — popular sites may be over-represented
- **Complex reasoning** — for nuanced analysis, GPT-4o or Claude in direct conversation may outperform
- **No persistent memory** — each new session starts fresh (Spaces help mitigate this)
