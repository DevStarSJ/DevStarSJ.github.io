## Core Features Deep Dive

### 1. Brand Voice Training

This is Jasper's biggest differentiator. Instead of sound like ChatGPT, your content sounds like *you*:

**How to set it up:**
1. Go to Brand Voice in your workspace
2. Paste 3-5 examples of your best content (blog posts, emails, ads)
3. Describe your brand personality (e.g., "Professional but approachable, uses data to back claims, avoids jargon")
4. Jasper analyzes and creates a voice profile

Once trained, every piece of content you create will automatically adopt your brand's tone, vocabulary, and style.

**Why it matters:** A Fortune 500 company writing 1,000 blog posts needs them all to sound like the same brand. Jasper makes this possible without lengthy editing.

### 3. Documents (Long-Form Editor)

Jasper's full-page editor for long-form content:

- Start with a blog post brief (topic, tone, audience, keywords)
- Use "Compose" to generate sections automatically
- "Improve" existing text to enhance quality
- "Summarize" to condense long sections
- Real-time collaboration for teams

**Power feature**: Document command bar

Type `//` in any document and issue commands:
- `//write a 3-paragraph intro about [topic]`
- `//create 5 H2 headers for this article`
- `//rewrite this paragraph to be more persuasive`

### 5. SEO Mode (SurferSEO Integration)

For content marketers focused on organic traffic:

1. Connect your SurferSEO account
2. Enter your target keyword
3. Jasper generates a content brief with:

This is how Jasper users compete for high-value keywords at scale.

## How to Get Maximum ROI from Jasper

### Build Your Brand Voice First

Before generating any content, invest 30 minutes in brand voice setup. This single step makes every subsequent piece 3x better.

### Create a Prompt Library

Save your best-performing prompts as templates for your team:
- Your top-performing Facebook ad formula
- Your signature email subject line structure

### Use Jasper for First Drafts, Humans for Final Polish

The most effective workflow:
1. **Jasper**: Generate first draft (5 minutes)
2. **Human**: Edit, fact-check, add unique insights (15 minutes)
3. **Jasper**: Improve weak paragraphs (5 minutes)
4. **Human**: Final review and approval (5 minutes)

Result: High-quality content in 30 minutes instead of 3 hours.
