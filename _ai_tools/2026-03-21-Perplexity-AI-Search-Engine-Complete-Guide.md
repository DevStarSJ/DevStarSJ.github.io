## How to Use Perplexity AI Effectively

### Basic Search
Just type your question naturally:
- ❌ "best AI tools 2026"  
- ✅ "What are the best AI productivity tools for remote workers in 2026, and what are the pros and cons of each?"

### Using Collections
Organize your research:
1. Click "Collections" in the sidebar
2. Create a new collection (e.g., "Market Research Q1 2026")
3. Save relevant threads to the collection
4. Share with teammates via link

### File Analysis (Pro)
Upload a research paper and ask:
- "Summarize the key findings of this paper"
- "What methodology did the researchers use?"
- "What are the limitations acknowledged by the authors?"
- "How does this compare to [other paper]?"

### Building a Research Report
Chain multiple questions to build a comprehensive report:
1. "Overview of the electric vehicle market in 2026"
2. "What are the top EV manufacturers by market share?"
3. "What are the major challenges facing EV adoption?"
4. "What government incentives exist for EVs in the US and EU?"

## Tips for Power Users

**1. Use the @ symbol to select sources**
Type `@youtube` to search specifically in YouTube, or `@reddit` for Reddit discussions.

**2. Add context in your question**
"As a marketing director at a SaaS company targeting SMBs, what are the most effective LinkedIn marketing strategies in 2026?"

**3. Ask for tables and comparisons**
"Create a comparison table of the top 5 project management tools in 2026, including pricing, key features, and best use case."

**4. Chain research with "Based on the above..."**
After an initial answer, follow up: "Based on the above, what are the potential risks I should be aware of?"

**5. Save to Collections immediately**
Don't wait — if a thread is useful, save it right away before you lose it in your history.
