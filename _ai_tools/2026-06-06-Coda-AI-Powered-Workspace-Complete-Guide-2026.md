What if your documents could do things? Not just store information — but run calculations, update automatically, trigger notifications, and power your entire team's workflow?

## What Is Coda?

Coda is an **all-in-one document platform** that blurs the line between docs, spreadsheets, databases, and apps. A single "Coda Doc" can contain:
- Rich text pages (like Notion or Google Docs)
- Tables with formulas (like Airtable or Excel)
- Buttons, controls, and automations (like a lightweight no-code app)
- AI-powered features throughout

Founded in 2014 by Shishir Mehrotra (former VP of YouTube/Google), Coda has grown into a serious enterprise platform used by teams at Spotify, Figma, BuzzFeed, and thousands of other companies.

## Key AI Features in 2026

### 1. Coda AI (Native Assistant)
Every Coda doc now has a native AI assistant that can:
- **Summarize** long documents or table data
- **Write and rewrite** sections in your voice
- **Answer questions** about the content in your doc
- **Analyze data** in tables and provide insights
- **Generate content** from structured data (e.g., turn a table of product features into marketing copy)

### 2. AI Table Operations
This is where Coda AI gets powerful for data. You can:
- Add an "AI column" that generates content for each row automatically
- Extract structured data from unstructured text
- Categorize, tag, and classify rows using AI
- Summarize lengthy text fields per row

Example: A CRM table where each row is a customer, and an AI column writes a personalized follow-up email draft for each contact.

### 3. AI-Powered Formulas
Coda's formula language is powerful but complex. AI can now help you write formulas in plain English: "Show me all tasks due this week that aren't completed, sorted by priority." It translates your intent into the correct formula.

### 4. Coda Brain (2026 Feature)
Coda Brain is the cross-doc AI layer that can:
- Reference content from any connected Coda doc
- Answer questions that span multiple documents
- Build a team knowledge base that's always up to date
- Integrate with external sources (Slack, Jira, Google Drive)

This makes Coda the AI-native alternative to Confluence for teams who want their knowledge base to be smart, not static.

## Core Platform Features

### Tables and Databases
Coda tables work like a hybrid of spreadsheets and relational databases. You can:
- Link rows across tables (like a relational database)
- Use 300+ formulas for calculations and transformations
- Create views (Kanban, Calendar, Gallery, Timeline) from any table
- Filter, group, and sort data across dimensions

### Docs and Pages
Each Coda doc can have unlimited pages — write documentation, meeting notes, project plans, and wikis all in one place. Pages can embed live tables, charts, and interactive components.

### Buttons and Automations
Add buttons that trigger actions: send emails, update rows, create calendar events, post to Slack, call APIs. Automations run on schedules or in response to row changes. This turns documents into lightweight apps without any code.

### Integrations
Coda connects to 500+ tools including Jira, GitHub, Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Zoom, and Google Workspace. Two-way sync means your Coda tables stay live with external data.

### Packs (Extensions)
Coda's Pack Studio lets you build or install custom integrations (called Packs) that pull external data directly into your tables. Community-built Packs for Stripe, Airtable, Twitter, and dozens more.

## Real-World Use Cases

### OKR and Goal Tracking
Teams use Coda to build OKR trackers that automatically pull in data from Jira (engineering velocity), Salesforce (sales numbers), and Google Analytics (growth metrics) — all in one live doc.

### Product Roadmap
Product managers love Coda for roadmaps because a single doc can contain: the strategy doc, the feature table with priorities, the sprint board, and the release notes — all linked and live.

### Meeting Notes + Actions
Templates that capture meeting notes, auto-assign action items, and sync them to Jira or Asana. Coda AI can summarize the meeting and extract action items automatically.

### Customer Research
UX researchers build interview trackers with AI columns that extract themes and insights from raw interview notes, dramatically speeding up synthesis.

## Limitations

- **Steep learning curve** — the "docs that do things" paradigm takes time to internalize
- **Performance** — very large docs with thousands of rows can feel sluggish
- **Not a spreadsheet replacement** — for heavy number-crunching, Excel/Sheets are better
- **AI costs** — AI features consume credits; high-usage teams may need Team plan
- **Less popular than Notion** — fewer templates and community resources
