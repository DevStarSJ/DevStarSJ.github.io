## What Is Leonardo AI?

Leonardo AI is a web-based AI image generation platform that launched in 2022 and has grown to over 15 million users. Built on top of Stable Diffusion and fine-tuned with custom models, Leonardo offers a feature set that goes well beyond "type a prompt, get an image" — including custom model training, image-to-image workflows, and a comprehensive suite of creative tools.

## Key Features

### 1. Phoenix & Alchemy Models
Leonardo's flagship **Phoenix** model delivers photorealistic and stylized outputs with strong prompt adherence. **Alchemy** is a post-processing pipeline that enhances detail, coherence, and quality — essentially upscaling intelligently rather than just scaling pixels.

### 2. Custom Model Training
This is Leonardo's killer feature: **train your own LoRA models** on your own images. Upload 10-20 reference images of a character, style, or object, and Leonardo trains a fine-tuned model that you can use for all future generations. This enables:
- **Character consistency** across dozens of images
- **Brand-consistent** visual outputs
- **Style transfer** from reference artists or aesthetics
- **Product visualization** with real objects

No GPU required — training happens on Leonardo's infrastructure.

### 3. AI Canvas
The AI Canvas is a Photoshop-like interface for AI editing:
- **Inpainting**: Replace or modify specific regions
- **Outpainting**: Extend images beyond their original borders
- **Sketch-to-image**: Draw rough shapes and have AI complete them
- **Element masking**: Isolate and regenerate specific elements

### 4. Image Guidance Tools
- **ControlNet integration**: Use depth maps, edge detection, or pose estimation to control compositions
- **Image-to-image**: Transform existing images while maintaining structure
- **Style reference**: Apply visual styles from reference images

### 5. Motion (Video Generation)
Leonardo's Motion feature animates static images with subtle or dramatic movement — turning your AI art into short looping videos. While not as sophisticated as Runway or Kling, it's impressively easy to use.

### 6. Real-Time Generation
The **Real-Time Canvas** feature shows live generation as you type, letting you iterate on prompts with instant visual feedback. It's genuinely magical for rapid ideation.

## Pro Tips

**Prompt structure for best results:**
```
[Subject], [Style], [Lighting], [Camera/Composition], [Quality modifiers]

Example: "Portrait of a cyberpunk detective, neon-lit street, rain, 
cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, photorealistic, 8k"
```

**Use Element weights:** Add `::2` after key terms to increase their weight, or `::0.5` to reduce.

**Negative prompts that always help:**
`low quality, blurry, distorted, ugly, bad anatomy, watermark, text, logo`

**Aspect ratios:**
- 1:1 for social media
- 16:9 for wallpapers/presentations
- 2:3 for portrait/phone wallpapers
- 3:2 for landscape photography look
