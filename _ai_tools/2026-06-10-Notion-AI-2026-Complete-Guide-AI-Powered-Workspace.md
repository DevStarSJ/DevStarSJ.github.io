## What Is Notion AI?

Notion AI is the AI layer built directly into Notion's workspace. Unlike standalone AI chatbots, it has context about *your specific pages, databases, and documents* — making it dramatically more useful for personal and team knowledge work.

Key capabilities:
- **Q&A across your workspace** — Ask questions, get answers from your own content
- **AI writing assistant** — Draft, edit, improve, and translate documents
- **Autofill** — Populate database properties automatically
- **Meeting summaries** — Paste a transcript, get an instant summary with action items
- **AI blocks** — Dynamic content that updates based on rules you set

---

## Core AI Features

### 🔍 Ask AI (Q&A Mode)
This is arguably the most powerful feature. You can ask Notion AI anything about your workspace:

> "What are the outstanding action items from last week's team meetings?"
> "Summarize our product roadmap for Q3"
> "What did we decide about the new pricing strategy?"

It searches across pages, databases, and documents to find relevant information and synthesize a clear answer. It's like having a research assistant who has read everything in your Notion.

### ✍️ Writing Assistant
Invoke Notion AI anywhere in a page with the `/AI` command or by selecting text:

- **Draft from scratch** — "Write a project brief for a new mobile app"
- **Improve writing** — Fix grammar, improve clarity, adjust tone
- **Make shorter/longer** — Adjust length to fit the context
- **Translate** — Supports 30+ languages
- **Explain simply** — Break down complex text

### 📊 Autofill for Databases
This feature alone can save hours per week for teams. Set up Autofill properties and Notion AI will:
- **Summarize** each record based on its linked content
- **Extract key information** (names, dates, categories) from documents
- **Generate tags** based on page content
- **Create action items** from meeting notes

### 📋 Meeting Summaries
Paste any meeting transcript into Notion and use AI to generate:
- **TL;DR summary** (3-5 bullets)
- **Key decisions made**
- **Action items with owners**
- **Follow-up questions**

This works whether you paste a raw transcript from Zoom, Google Meet, or any other tool.

## AI Blocks (New in 2026)

- A summary block that pulls from linked meeting notes

## Pro Tips for Power Users

### 1. Build a Personal Knowledge Base
The more you put into Notion, the smarter Notion AI becomes *for you*. Import your notes, documents, and meeting logs. Every page you add is training data for better Q&A answers.

### 2. Create AI-Powered Templates
Build a meeting notes template that automatically:
- Summarizes the discussion
- Extracts action items
- Tags the project

Just paste the transcript and let AI do the rest.

### 3. Use Autofill for CRM
If you run a small business or freelance, Notion's database + Autofill can serve as a surprisingly capable lightweight CRM:
- Track contacts and companies
- Autofill conversation summaries from your notes
- Generate follow-up email drafts

### 4. Combine with Notion Integrations
Notion connects with Slack, GitHub, Google Drive, Jira, and more. AI can then reason across data from all these sources when it's synced into your workspace.

## Limitations

- **Workspace-only context** — It can't browse the web or access external data by default
- **Slow with large workspaces** — Q&A can lag in accounts with thousands of pages
- **Writing quality** — General writing assistant isn't as good as Claude or GPT-4 for long-form content
- **No image generation** — Stays strictly text-based

---

## Getting Started in 5 Minutes

1. Open Notion and create a new page
2. Type `/AI` to open the AI command menu
3. Try "Summarize this page" on an existing document
4. Ask a question with "Ask AI" about your workspace
5. Set up Autofill on any database property
