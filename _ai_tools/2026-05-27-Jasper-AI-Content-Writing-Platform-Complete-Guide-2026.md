## What Is Jasper AI?

Jasper AI (formerly Jarvis) is a cloud-based AI writing platform designed specifically for marketing and content teams. It helps users create:

- Long-form blog posts and articles
- SEO-optimized content
- Social media copy
- Email sequences
- Paid ad copy (Google, Facebook, LinkedIn)
- Product descriptions
- Video scripts
- Press releases

What sets Jasper apart from general AI tools is its **marketing-specific templates**, **Brand Voice** training, and deep **SEO integration**.

---

## Key Features

### Brand Voice

Jasper's standout feature. You train it on your existing content — website copy, past blog posts, brand guidelines — and it learns your tone, style, and vocabulary.

Once set, every piece of content Jasper generates sounds like *you*, not a generic AI. This is critical for brand consistency at scale.

### Jasper Art

Beyond text, Jasper includes an AI image generator powered by Stable Diffusion and custom models. Generate blog header images, social media graphics, and ad creatives directly within Jasper — no switching tools.

### SEO Mode (with Surfer SEO)

Jasper integrates natively with **Surfer SEO**, which analyzes top-ranking pages for your target keyword and gives you real-time optimization guidance as you write.

Write a blog post in Jasper, see your content score live, and know exactly which terms to include to rank.

3. Keep them all aligned to the same messaging

## Jasper Workflow

A typical Jasper content workflow looks like this:

1. **Set Brand Voice** — upload brand guidelines and samples
2. **Choose a template** (or use the long-form editor)
3. **Describe your content** — topic, audience, tone, key points
4. **Generate first draft** — takes seconds
5. **Refine with AI commands** — "make this punchier," "add a statistic," "shorten by 30%"
6. **Run SEO check** — if using Surfer integration
7. **Final human edit** — always review before publishing

## Tips for Better Jasper Outputs

1. **Train Brand Voice thoroughly** — the more content you feed it, the more accurate it gets
2. **Be specific in descriptions** — vague prompts get vague output
3. **Use templates as starting points** — don't just use the output directly
4. **Iterate with commands** — Jasper is designed for back-and-forth refinement
5. **Always human-review** — AI writes fast; humans write well
