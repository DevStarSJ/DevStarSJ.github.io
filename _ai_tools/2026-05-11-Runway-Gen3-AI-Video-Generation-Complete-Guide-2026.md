# Runway ML Gen-3: Complete Guide to AI Video Generation 2026

Runway ML has established itself as the premier AI video generation platform for creatives, filmmakers, and marketers. With the release of Gen-3 Alpha and subsequent updates, Runway has pushed the boundaries of what's possible with AI-generated video. This guide covers everything from basics to advanced techniques.

## What is Runway Gen-3?

Runway Gen-3 Alpha is Runway's third-generation text-to-video and image-to-video model. Key improvements over Gen-2 include:

- **Higher fidelity** — sharper, more detailed video output
- **Better motion coherence** — smoother, more natural movement
- **Improved prompt following** — the model better understands complex instructions
- **Longer clips** — up to 10 seconds per generation
- **More consistent subjects** — characters and objects maintain appearance across frames

## Core Features

### Text-to-Video
Describe a scene and Runway generates a short video clip:
```
A drone shot flying over a misty mountain valley at golden hour, 
cinematic, 4K quality
```

### Image-to-Video
Upload a still image and bring it to life with motion:
- Photo of a person → Make them look around, blink, smile
- Landscape photo → Add flowing water, moving clouds, swaying trees
- Product shot → Subtle rotation, lighting effects

### Video-to-Video
Transform existing video with style transfer, color grading, or motion modifications.

### Inpainting & Outpainting
Edit specific regions of a video or extend the frame beyond original borders.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Create an Account

Visit [runwayml.com](https://runwayml.com) and sign up. The free plan provides 125 one-time credits to get started.

### Step 2: Choose Your Generation Mode

From the dashboard, select **Gen-3 Alpha** (or the latest model available). Choose between:
- **Text to Video**
- **Image to Video**
- **Video to Video**

### Step 3: Craft Your Prompt

For Text-to-Video:
```
[Camera Motion] [Scene Description] [Lighting] [Style] [Quality Tags]

Example:
Slow dolly forward through an ancient library with floating books,
warm candlelight, cinematic depth of field, film grain
```

### Step 4: Configure Settings

- **Duration**: 4 or 10 seconds
- **Resolution**: 768p or higher (on paid plans)
- **Seed**: Lock a seed to generate variations of the same clip

## Prompt Engineering for Video

Video prompting requires different thinking than image prompting. Consider:

### Camera Movement Language
- `Slow push in` — camera moves forward slowly
- `Pan left/right` — horizontal sweep
- `Tilt up/down` — vertical sweep
- `Crane shot` — elevated moving shot
- `Handheld` — slight natural camera shake
- `Drone/aerial` — overhead perspective

### Motion Descriptors
- `Slow motion` — dramatically slowed
- `Time lapse` — fast-forwarded scene
- `Smooth` — no camera shake
- `Dynamic` — active, energetic movement

### Visual Style Tags
- `Cinematic`, `film grain`, `depth of field`
- `35mm`, `anamorphic`, `IMAX`
- `Photorealistic`, `hyperrealistic`
- `Studio lighting`, `golden hour`, `blue hour`

### Full Example Prompts

**Nature Scene:**
```
Aerial drone shot descending through fog above an ancient forest, 
shafts of morning light breaking through the canopy, birds scatter, 
cinematic 4K, photorealistic
```

**Character Shot:**
```
Close-up of a woman's face in soft window light, she slowly turns 
to look at camera with a slight smile, shallow depth of field, 
warm skin tones, film photography aesthetic
```

**Abstract/Creative:**
```
Colorful paint drops falling in slow motion into clear water,
macro lens, vibrant colors, smooth motion, studio lighting
```

## Advanced Techniques

### Image-to-Video for Consistent Characters
1. Generate a character image with Midjourney or DALL-E
2. Use that as the reference image in Runway
3. Prompt for specific motion: "The character walks forward, looking straight ahead"
4. This maintains face/character consistency far better than text-only

### Multi-Shot Storytelling
Generate multiple individual clips and assemble in Runway's built-in editor or a video editor:
1. Establish shot (wide)
2. Character reaction (close-up)
3. Action sequence (dynamic)
4. Resolution shot (wide)

### Style Consistency with Seeds
When a generation looks exactly right, note the seed number. Use that seed with slightly different prompts to create variations that maintain the same visual style.

### Negative Prompts
Use negative prompts to avoid common issues:
```
Prompt: [your scene]
Negative: blur, distortion, deformed faces, flickering, artifacts
```

## Common Issues & Solutions

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|---------|
| Faces look distorted | Use image-to-video with reference face |
| Too much camera movement | Add "static camera" or "locked shot" to prompt |
| Poor lighting | Specify exact lighting: "golden hour backlight" |
| Motion too fast | Add "slow motion" or "gentle movement" |
| Not photorealistic | Add "photorealistic, hyperrealistic, 8K" tags |
