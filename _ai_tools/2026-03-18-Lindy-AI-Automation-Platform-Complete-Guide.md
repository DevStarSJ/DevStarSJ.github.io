# Lindy AI: The Personal AI Automation Platform That Replaces Your VA

What if you could hire a virtual assistant that works 24/7, never misses a follow-up, and costs a fraction of a human VA? That's the premise of Lindy AI — a platform that lets you build and deploy personal AI agents that handle your email, calendar, CRM, and complex multi-step workflows.

## What Is Lindy AI?

Lindy AI is a **no-code AI agent platform** where you create "Lindies" — personal AI employees with specific jobs. Each Lindy has:
- A defined **role and purpose**
- Access to specific **tools and integrations**
- **Trigger conditions** (when to activate)
- **Memory** of past interactions
- The ability to **take actions** on your behalf

Think of it less like a workflow automation tool (Zapier/Make) and more like hiring an AI team member who understands context and can handle ambiguity.

---

## Core Concepts

### Lindies = AI Employees
Each Lindy is configured with:
- **Instructions**: Natural language description of their job
- **Tools**: Email, calendar, Slack, Notion, HubSpot, etc.
- **Trigger**: What wakes them up (new email, webhook, schedule, message)
- **Memory**: What they remember across conversations

### Meeting the Team
Lindy ships with pre-built Lindy templates:
- 📧 **Email Assistant** — triages inbox, drafts replies, follows up
- 📅 **Meeting Scheduler** — handles back-and-forth scheduling
- 🔍 **Research Agent** — searches web, summarizes findings
- 💬 **Customer Support** — responds to support tickets
- 📊 **Sales Prospector** — researches leads, drafts cold emails
- 📝 **Content Writer** — generates content from templates

---

## Key Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Decision Making
Unlike traditional automation (if X then Y), Lindies use LLMs to handle edge cases. A Lindy can:
- Understand the *intent* of an email, not just keywords
- Decide whether to reply, escalate, or archive
- Draft a contextually appropriate response
- Handle situations not explicitly programmed

### 🔗 Deep Integrations
Lindy connects to 200+ apps:
- **Email**: Gmail, Outlook
- **Calendar**: Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, Calendly
- **CRM**: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive
- **Communication**: Slack, Teams, WhatsApp Business
- **Productivity**: Notion, Airtable, Google Sheets
- **Custom**: Webhooks and REST API

### 🧠 Persistent Memory
Lindies remember:
- Your preferences and past decisions
- Relationships (client history, communication style)
- Ongoing tasks and context
- Custom data you explicitly teach them

### 👥 Lindy-to-Lindy Collaboration
Lindies can delegate to other Lindies:
- Email Lindy detects a sales lead → hands off to Sales Lindy
- Research Lindy gathers data → Content Lindy writes the article

## Setting Up Your First Lindy

### Step 1: Create Account
1. Go to [lindy.ai](https://lindy.ai)
2. Sign up with Google or email
3. Connect your Google/Microsoft account

### Step 2: Create a Lindy
1. Click "Create a Lindy"
2. Choose a template or start from scratch
3. Write instructions in plain English:

```
You are my email assistant. Your job is to:
1. Triage my inbox every morning at 9 AM
2. Flag urgent emails (response needed within 24h) in red
3. Draft replies for common questions about pricing
4. Archive newsletters and promotional emails
5. Create calendar events when someone proposes a meeting time

My name is Alex. I prefer concise, professional email tone.
Never commit to meetings on Fridays.
```

### Step 3: Connect Integrations
- Add Gmail access
- Add Google Calendar
- Configure which folders/labels to monitor

### Step 4: Set Triggers
- **Schedule**: Run every morning at 9 AM
- **Event**: Trigger when a new email arrives
- **Webhook**: Trigger from external system

### Step 5: Test
Send a test email or manually trigger the Lindy to verify behavior.

## Practical Lindy Recipes

### Recipe 1: Sales Follow-Up Machine
```
Trigger: New lead added to HubSpot

Instructions:
1. Research the prospect's company (LinkedIn, website)
2. Identify their industry, size, and potential pain points
3. Draft a personalized cold email referencing their specific situation
4. Create a follow-up sequence: Day 1, Day 4, Day 10
5. Log all activity in HubSpot notes
```

### Recipe 2: Meeting Prep Assistant
```
Trigger: 1 hour before any calendar meeting

Instructions:
1. Get the names of all attendees
2. Search my email history for past conversations with them
3. Check HubSpot for their customer/prospect status
4. Search the web for recent news about their company
5. Create a meeting brief in Notion with: attendees, history, talking points
6. Send me a Slack message with the brief link
```

### Recipe 3: Content Repurposing Pipeline
```
Trigger: New article published on my blog

Instructions:
1. Read the full article
2. Create 5 LinkedIn post variations
3. Create 10 tweets/X posts from key insights
4. Write a newsletter section based on the article
5. Draft a short video script for a YouTube short
6. Save all to Notion "Content Calendar" database
```

### Recipe 4: Customer Support Triage
```
Trigger: New support email arrives

Instructions:
1. Categorize: billing, technical, feature request, complaint
2. Check if this customer has open tickets (Zendesk)
3. For simple questions: draft a complete response
4. For technical issues: create a bug ticket in Jira with details
5. For billing: flag to the accounts team via Slack
6. Always respond within 1 business hour
```

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of Lindy

**1. Be explicit about boundaries**
```
Never send an email on my behalf without showing me a draft first
Only schedule meetings between 10 AM and 4 PM EST
Always CC my assistant sarah@company.com on client emails
```

**2. Use memory intentionally**
```
Remember that Client X prefers text to email
Remember our pricing is $5,000 for starter, $15,000 for enterprise
```

**3. Start simple, then expand**
- Begin with a single Lindy for email triage
- Add complexity (HubSpot sync, Slack alerts) once the core works
- Create specialized Lindies for different workflows

**4. Review before deploying to "auto-send"**
- Run in "draft mode" first (Lindy drafts, you approve)
- Gradually switch to autonomous mode once you trust the outputs
