## Pro Tips

### 1. Start with References
Upload images that represent your desired style before generating. Recraft learns from visual examples better than text descriptions alone.

### 2. Use Negative Prompts
Tell Recraft what NOT to include:
- "no gradients"
- "no shadows"
- "no realistic textures"

### 3. Iterate on the Canvas
Generate multiple options, keep what works, delete what doesn't. The infinite canvas makes A/B comparison natural.

### 4. Export Strategically
- Vectors for logos and icons
- High-res raster for photos/illustrations
- Multiple sizes for responsive design

### 5. Build Style Libraries
Save your best generations as style references. Build a library over time for instant consistency.
