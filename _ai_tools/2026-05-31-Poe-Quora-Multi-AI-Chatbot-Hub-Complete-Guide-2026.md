# Poe by Quora: One App to Access Every AI Model

What if you could access Claude, GPT-4o, Gemini, Llama, and dozens of other AI models — plus thousands of specialized AI bots — all through a single subscription and interface? That's exactly what **Poe by Quora** delivers.

In a landscape where AI capabilities are fragmenting across multiple expensive subscriptions, Poe offers a compelling value proposition: a unified AI hub that keeps up with the rapidly evolving AI model ecosystem.

## What Is Poe?

Poe is an AI chatbot platform developed by Quora that aggregates access to leading AI models from multiple providers under one roof. Instead of maintaining separate subscriptions to Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Meta, Poe gives you access to all of them — plus a marketplace of thousands of specialized bots built on these models.

## Models Available on Poe

As of 2026, Poe includes access to:

### Frontier Models
- **Claude 3.5 Sonnet & Opus** (Anthropic) — Best for analysis and writing
- **GPT-4o & GPT-4o mini** (OpenAI) — Most versatile
- **Gemini 1.5 Pro & Flash** (Google) — Huge context window

### Specialized Models
- **Claude for coding**
- **DALL-E 3** for image generation

## Key Features

### 🔄 Model Switching
Switch between AI models mid-conversation or start new chats with specific models. Compare how different models respond to the same prompt. Great for understanding model strengths and differences.

### 🤖 Bot Marketplace
Browse and use thousands of community-created bots, each customized with specific system prompts and capabilities:
- Writing assistants (technical, creative, academic)
- Coding helpers with specific language focus
- Domain experts (legal, medical, financial — for informational use)
- Language tutors
- Character role-play bots
- Research assistants

### 🔧 Create Your Own Bot
Build and publish custom bots with:
- Custom system prompts
- Personality and tone settings
- Knowledge base files
- Monetization options (earn from your bot's subscribers)

### 💬 Multi-Bot Conversations
Unique to Poe: you can have multiple bots respond to the same message simultaneously, allowing you to compare outputs or use bots collaboratively.

### 📱 Cross-Platform Access
Available on iOS, Android, and web. Sync conversations across devices.

### 🧠 Memory Features
Poe supports giving bots memory of your preferences and previous interactions (feature varies by bot and subscription).

## Use Cases

Testing which model performs best for a specific task? Poe lets you send the same prompt to Claude, GPT-4o, and Gemini simultaneously and compare responses side-by-side.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Download or Visit
Get the app from App Store/Google Play or visit [poe.com](https://poe.com).

### Step 2: Create a Free Account
Sign up with email, Google, or Apple. Free tier includes limited daily messages.

### Step 3: Choose a Model
Browse available models from the sidebar. Try Claude for thoughtful conversation, GPT-4o for general tasks, or explore the bot marketplace.

### Step 4: Explore Bots
Browse the marketplace for specialized bots. Search by category (writing, coding, language learning, etc.) to find bots optimized for your use case.

### Step 5: Create Your Own Bot
Click "Create Bot" to build a custom AI assistant with your own system prompt and share it with the community.

## Tips for Power Users

1. **Pin your favorite models**: Add frequently used models to your sidebar for quick access
2. **Compare responses**: Use the "Talk to multiple bots" feature when evaluating approaches
3. **Find niche bots**: The marketplace has thousands of specialized bots — search before creating your own
4. **Use long context models**: For large documents, switch to Gemini 1.5 Pro's million-token context
5. **Check model rankings**: Poe provides user ratings for community bots — sort by top-rated to find quality

## Limitations

- Message limits on premium models even with paid tier
- Slight lag in accessing newest model versions compared to native apps
- API access is limited compared to direct provider APIs
- Some advanced features (like custom GPTs or Claude Projects) are native to their respective platforms only
- Bot quality in the marketplace varies significantly

## Conclusion

Poe is an excellent choice for anyone who wants to explore the AI landscape without committing to multiple expensive subscriptions. The model variety, bot marketplace, and unified interface make it genuinely useful, especially if you're still figuring out which AI models work best for your workflows.

For power users who have settled on a specific model and need advanced integrations, native subscriptions might still be better. But for AI exploration, comparison, and casual to moderate use, Poe delivers exceptional value.
